    Battery Backup System Testing in Tuckahoe, VA

    Storms knock out power when the pit fills fastest. A backup pump exists for exactly that hour. We check the battery date, clean the terminals and confirm the charger is actually charging, since a dead charger quietly drains the battery to nothing over a few months.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in Tuckahoe

    The check valve sits on the discharge pipe a foot or so above the pump and keeps the water in the line from falling back into the pit. When it fails, the pump pumps the same water twice. Motors wear out faster. Our plumber tests the valve, confirms there is a weep hole below it to prevent air lock, and checks each joint to the wall.

    Float Switch Inspection in Tuckahoe, VA

    The float switch tells the pump when to start and stop, and a float with too little travel between on and off makes the pump short cycle dozens of times an hour. We watch the float through a full cycle. Then we set the on and off points so the pump runs longer, less often.

    Zoeller and Wayne Sump Pump Maintenance in Tuckahoe

    Brand matters less than people think, but parts do not cross over cleanly between Zoeller, Wayne, Liberty and Little Giant. We match the float and seals to the model number on the label. A submersible Zoeller and a pedestal Wayne get two different checklists. We know both.

    Check Valve and Discharge Pipe Repair in Tuckahoe, VA

    A bad check valve can nearly double how often the pump starts. Every startup puts heat into the motor. We swap worn valves for quiet, spring-loaded models and inspect the discharge pipe for cracks and loose couplings all the way to the exit point. Discharge needs to land far enough from the slab or footings that it does not seep right back into the pit.

    Wayne Sump Pump Service in Tuckahoe, VA

    Some Wayne models draw water through a top suction intake, which helps them avoid pulling in the sludge that settles at the bottom of a pit. We check that intake and the float mechanism for buildup. Any brand can rattle its discharge fittings loose over time. Easy fix.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Service in Tuckahoe

    Zoeller pumps like the M53 and M63 use cast iron housings and an integral float switch, and they tend to outlast cheaper plastic units. We still check the casting for cracks and the float for free travel. Common Zoeller parts ride on our trucks.

    Annual Sump Pump Inspections in Tuckahoe

    Once a year is the minimum. Each yearly visit covers the motor, the impeller, the float and the discharge line. Then we fill the pit and time how long the pump takes to empty it, because a slow drawdown is the earliest sign of a worn impeller or a partly blocked line.
