    Frequently Asked Questions About Basement Sump Pump Installation in Tuckahoe

    Figure on roughly $800 to $2,000 for most basement sump pump installs in Tuckahoe. We quote after seeing it.

    A straight pump replacement usually does not need a permit. New pits, new discharge routes and new circuits may, so we look up the local rules and file for a permit when one is needed.

    Changing out a pump in an existing pit takes about two to three hours. A new pit with slab cutting and a concrete patch takes most of a day, since the concrete needs a little time to firm up before the pump goes into service.

    Discharge belongs in the yard, a dry well, or an approved drainage area at least 10 feet from the foundation. Most local codes do not allow sump water in the sanitary sewer, and it can draw a fine.

    Swapping an identical plug-in pump is doable for a handy homeowner. Cutting a pit, wiring a new outlet and routing discharge are not, because a mistake there leads to a cracked slab, a shock hazard, or water pumped right back against the foundation in Tuckahoe. Those parts call for a licensed plumber.

    Hurricanes, tropical storms and strong coastal storms bring heavy rain and tidal flooding to eastern Virginia, especially in low-lying areas. Outages and storms arrive together, so we recommend a battery backup in most basements. It switches on by itself when the main pump loses power, and it also catches the water if the primary switch sticks or the pump simply cannot keep up with a heavy inflow. Think of it as cheap insurance.

    Plan on 4 to 8 hours for a standard install. A new pit with slab cutting can stretch it to a full day, and tricky electrical access or a long discharge run through a finished wall adds time on top of that. We tell you up front.
