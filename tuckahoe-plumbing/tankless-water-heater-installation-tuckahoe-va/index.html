    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Tuckahoe, VA

    Calcium scale is the most common reason tankless heaters fail. Water hardness in Virginia varies by region, with more mineral-heavy water in the western limestone valleys and softer water closer to the coast. Once scale coats the heat exchanger, efficiency drops and error codes start, and over time it can crack the exchanger and void the warranty. A yearly flush prevents it. We install isolation valves for that.
