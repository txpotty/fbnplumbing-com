    Effluent Pump Replacement in Tuckahoe, VA

    Effluent pumps in a dosing chamber last roughly 8 to 15 years, and they usually quit without much warning at all. Humming with no flow points to a jammed impeller or a failed capacitor. We pull it and test the discharge line before setting a new one. Floats get replaced at the same time.

    Drain Field Line Jetting in Tuckahoe

    Distribution laterals clog from the inside with solids and slime, especially after years without pumping. We jet each lateral from the distribution box or a cleanout at the far end, and what washes back shows how plugged it was. Roots get cut.     Septic Maintenance and Small Repairs in Tuckahoe

    A sludge judge, a long clear tube, tells us how full the tank really is. Each maintenance call includes a sludge and scum reading, and we pump when the solids reach about a third of the tank depth. Filters get rinsed. Small cracks around the riser get sealed before groundwater finds them.

    Inlet and Outlet Baffle Replacement in Tuckahoe, VA

    Baffles hold back the scum layer on top and the sludge on the bottom so only clear effluent leaves the tank. Concrete ones corrode from the acid that sewer gas forms and eventually fall off into the tank. Once the outlet baffle is gone, solids head straight for the field. We replace them with PVC sanitary tees. Usually a short job.

    Septic Tee Repair in Tuckahoe

    Sanitary tees on the inlet and outlet crack or pop loose, sometimes from a careless pump-out hose. Without the outlet tee, the floating scum layer flows straight into the field lines and seals the trenches. We glue in a new Schedule 40 tee. An effluent filter goes on if the outlet has room.

    Septic Pipe Repair in Tuckahoe, VA

    Old homes may still have clay tile or Orangeburg pipe running to the tank, and both fail badly with age. Expansive clays in some areas swell when wet and shrink when dry, which can crack foundations and shift buried sewer lines. Shifting ground and roots crack the joints and flatten the pipe. We replace the bad run with 4-inch PVC, set on sand bedding with a quarter inch per foot of fall. Short sections take an afternoon.
