    Drywall Patching and Restoration in Tuckahoe, VA

    PEX Repiping in Tuckahoe, VA

    PEX is flexible plastic tubing that can run long distances without a fitting, which means fewer joints hidden in your walls. Fewer joints, fewer leaks. PEX shrugs off scale and chlorine, survives freezing better than copper and installs faster, so it is our most common repipe material. We use PEX-A with cold expansion fittings that keep the full inside diameter of the pipe open. Flow stays strong at the last shower.

    Commercial Repiping in Tuckahoe, VA

    Commercial buildings in Tuckahoe, VA often have larger pipe, risers that serve several floors and tenants who cannot lose water for a full day.     Repiping for Hard Water in Tuckahoe, VA

    Even moderate hardness in Tuckahoe can leave scale in water heaters over time, so regular flushing helps them run longer and more efficiently. Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium scale inside pipe, and over years it narrows the bore and speeds up corrosion in metal lines. Pressure suffers. In hard water homes, PEX-A is often the better pick since it resists scale, and a softener loop or filter can be added during the job. Adding the loop now costs less than later.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Tuckahoe, VA

    PEX tubing resists freezing damage, corrosion and scale better than most metal pipe. It is quieter too. Because PEX flexes, it absorbs some of the shock that causes water hammer, and long runs with few fittings mean fewer places for a leak to start inside a wall. We keep PEX out of sunlight and away from water heater flues.

    Copper Repiping in Tuckahoe, VA

    Some homeowners prefer copper for its long track record and rigidity. We install Type L copper, which has a thicker wall than the Type M used in many older homes, with lead-free solder or ProPress fittings. Expect a slightly longer job. Copper repipes usually cost more and take a day longer than PEX, mostly because every turn needs a fitting.
