    Cost-Effective Sewage Ejector Pump Upgrades in Norfolk, VA

    Upgrading an old ejector can often keep the existing pit. With a sound basin, a new pump, fresh check valve, shutoff and alarm give you most of a new system for far less. We tell you if the basin is done.

    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Norfolk

    Sump pumps and ejector pumps look alike in a catalog but do very different jobs. A sump pump moves groundwater from a perimeter drain to the yard, while an ejector moves sewage from toilets and sinks to the sewer, and the ejector basin has to be sealed and vented because of the gas. Never mix the two.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Basin & Pit Installation in Norfolk

    Digging the pit means cutting the slab and excavating below it, deep enough to set the basin flush with the finished floor. Expansive clays in some areas swell when wet and shrink when dry, which can crack foundations and shift buried sewer lines. The basin sits on gravel with backfill around it so rising groundwater cannot float it, and new concrete seals the floor and locks it down. A floating basin breaks pipe.

    Basement Bathroom Ejector Systems in Norfolk

    Basement bathrooms sit below the building sewer, so the toilet, shower and sink drain to a sealed basin instead of flowing by gravity. We cut the slab, trench the drain lines to the basin, set it, run a two-inch vent up through the house to the roof and pipe the discharge to the building drain with a check valve and shutoff. The slab gets repoured.

    Upflush and Macerating Toilet Systems in Norfolk

    Macerating systems like Saniflo add a bath fixture or two without breaking the concrete floor. It sits behind the toilet, grinds waste and pumps it through a small line, often three-quarter or one inch, to a drain above, which suits a small half bath. They need a vent too.

    New Construction Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Norfolk, VA

    On a new build, we set the basin ahead of the slab pour. That is the cheapest time, since there is no concrete to cut, and we stub the drains, the vent and the discharge to the plans so the rough-in inspection goes smoothly. It keeps the pour on schedule.

    Laundry and Wet Bar Ejector Pumps in Norfolk, VA

    Below-grade laundry rooms and wet bars produce gray water that still needs to be lifted to the sewer. A smaller laundry tray pump or a compact ejector can handle it, sized for the 15 or more gallons a washer dumps at once, with a lint filter so fibers do not wrap the impeller. Lint kills small pumps.
