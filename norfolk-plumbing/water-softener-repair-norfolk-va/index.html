    Valve, Resin and Brine Repair in Norfolk, VA

    Salt Bridge and Brine Tank Service in Norfolk, VA

    The tank looks full but makes no brine. A salt bridge has formed and stopped the salt from dissolving. We break up bridges, clean out mushy salt, inspect the float and brine well and repair the safety valve. Pellet salt bridges less in warm or humid spaces, so we may suggest switching if your softener sits in a hot garage.

    Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Norfolk, VA

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates the suction that draws brine into the resin tank. Sediment and salt residue clog its tiny ports. Once it clogs, the brine tank fills with water instead of emptying, and the softener regenerates with plain water that does nothing to the resin. We clean or replace it and check the brine line for air leaks.

    Softener Repair vs Replacement Cost in Norfolk, VA

    Most softener repairs cost $150 to $450, while a resin replacement often runs $400 to $900 depending on tank size. Installing a new quality softener usually runs $1,500 to $3,500. Sound tank and valve, repair wins. An obsolete valve with scarce parts tips the decision toward a new head or a new unit.

    Bypass and Fitting Leak Repair in Norfolk, VA

    Bypass valves, the O-rings where the valve meets the tank, cracked fittings and the drain line are the usual leak points. Even a small drip wastes water and can stain a garage floor or rot a cabinet. We replace O-rings or fittings and test under pressure. A cracked mineral tank usually means replacement.

    No-Salt Water Conditioner Repair in Norfolk, VA

    No-salt conditioners do not remove minerals, they change how scale forms, but they still need service. Scale returning or a drop in water pressure usually means the conditioning media is spent or the sediment prefilter is clogged. FBN Plumbing repairs these units in Norfolk, VA. Media lasts a few years.

    Resin Bed Replacement in Norfolk

    Chlorine eats resin slowly, and after enough years the beads swell, break and stop exchanging the calcium and magnesium they are supposed to hold. This is common with older systems on treated city water. FBN Plumbing removes the old resin and installs fresh, high-capacity media. Capacity comes back right away.

    Emergency Water Softener Repair in Norfolk

    A softener stuck in regeneration can run water to the drain for hours or flood a utility room. Urgent calls like that get priority. The crew in Norfolk puts the unit in bypass first, then finds out whether the valve, the timer or the drain line caused it. Most of the time it is a stuck piston or a failed motor that can be replaced on the same visit.

    Cracked tanks, loose fittings and failed O-rings are behind most softener leaks. Even a slow drip wastes water and soaks the floor. Once we find the source, from the bypass to the drain connection, the failed seal or fitting gets replaced. Then it gets a pressure test.
