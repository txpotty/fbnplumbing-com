    Commercial Gas Water Heater Repair in Norfolk, VA

    Restaurants and salons in Norfolk, VA burn through hot water faster than any house, and a down heater stops the business.     Power Vent Water Heater Repair in Norfolk

    Power vent heaters push exhaust through PVC with a blower on top of the tank. When one quits, it is usually the blower motor, the pressure switch or the hot surface igniter. Condensate pooling in a vent with the wrong slope can also trip the pressure switch, so we check the pipe run all the way to the termination before we sign off on a part. We read the error code first. Every suspect part then gets tested with a meter.

    Tank Leaks and Anode Rods in Norfolk

    Corrosion starts at the anode rod and works its way into the steel once the rod is spent. Even moderate hardness in Norfolk can leave scale in water heaters over time, so regular flushing helps them run longer and more efficiently. We pull the anode, check how much magnesium or aluminum is left and replace it if the core wire is showing. A fresh anode, a drained tank and a relief valve that seats properly can add years to a heater in a Norfolk home that has not started leaking from the shell. A wet bottom seam is different. That tank needs replacing.

    Bradford White and Rheem Gas Water Heater Repair in Norfolk, VA

    Bradford White and Rheem tanks make up a big share of what we repair in Norfolk, VA. Most Rheem gas units run a White-Rodgers or Honeywell valve with a blinking status LED. Bradford White uses its own sealed combustion design with a lint screen at the base and a thermal switch, and it needs a specific kit when the pilot assembly goes bad. Both kits ride on the truck. Using factory parts keeps your tank warranty valid.

    Burner Tray and Venting Repair in Norfolk

    Rust scale on the burner tray is a sign the flue baffle or the tank top is shedding. The burner comes out, the ports get cleared and the flame arrestor gets a look. Then the vent gets checked from the draft hood to the roof, because a flue that is loose, sagging or capped by a bird nest can push carbon monoxide back into your Norfolk home. We hold a smoke pencil at the draft hood to see which way the air moves.

    Repairing Older Gas Water Heaters in Norfolk

    Older tanks with standing pilots and no vapor sensor are still out there in Norfolk, mostly in basements and utility closets. Parts for them are simpler, and a universal thermocouple fits most. Some old setups lack a sediment trap. We add one during the repair.

    Pilot Light and Ignition Service in Norfolk

    No hot water and a dark pilot window usually point to the ignition side of the heater. The tech pulls the pilot assembly and checks the thermocouple tip, the orifice and the igniter wire. Common pilot kits ride on most of our trucks.
