    Frequently Asked Questions About Trenchless Pipe Repair in Norfolk

    CIPP lining usually costs $80 to $250 per foot. Bursting runs a bit less per foot. Pipe bursting often runs $60 to $200 per foot plus the access pits, and a typical residential sewer line totals $4,000 to $15,000 depending on length and condition.

    Most cracked, rooted or separated pipes can be lined. Collapses are different. A fully collapsed or deeply sagging line may need bursting or a dig, and the camera shows us which.

    Trenchless repair requires specialized equipment, materials and training. It should be done by an experienced contractor.

    We use liners and resins from established manufacturers such as Perma-Liner and similar systems rated for sewer use, picked to match the pipe size and condition.

    CIPP liners and resins from Perma-Liner. Prep is done with Picote cutters. Each product is proven in sewer use and meets or beats local code, which matters for pipe that should last decades.

    Cast iron, clay, PVC and concrete can all be lined. The pipe's shape counts. A pipe that has fully collapsed or is badly misaligned may be a better fit for bursting than lining, and our camera inspection tells us which trenchless method suits your line.

    Lining builds a new pipe inside the old one. Bursting swaps the pipe out. A CIPP liner cures inside the host pipe, and bursting fractures the old pipe while dragging new HDPE in behind it.

    Lining reduces the inside diameter slightly, but the smooth surface often improves flow. Pipe bursting can maintain or increase the diameter.
