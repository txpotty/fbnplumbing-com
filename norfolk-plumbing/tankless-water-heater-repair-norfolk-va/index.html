    Recirculation Pump Repair in Norfolk

    Recirculation cuts the wait for hot water at a far bathroom from a minute or more to a few seconds. When the pump fails or the check valve sticks, you get long waits again or lukewarm water in the cold line. We test the pump, look for a thermal bypass valve under the farthest sink, and set the schedule so it runs around your mornings and evenings. Two settings, mostly.

    Flow Sensor and Thermistor Repair in Norfolk

    Tankless heaters start when the flow sensor sees about half a gallon a minute, then trim the burner using thermistor readings. A dirty inlet screen or a failing sensor shows up as no hot water, temperature swings or a unit that drops out mid-shower. Our tech cleans the screen, checks sensor values in the service menu and tests thermistor resistance against the chart. Usually under an hour.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Repair in Norfolk

    Pressure at full fire matters on a gas tankless burner, not just at the moment it lights. We put a manometer on the inlet test port and run every hot tap in the house, because pressure that drops below the manufacturer's minimum under load causes flame loss and cold slugs. Then we check the exhaust. Codes come off when the numbers are right.

    Commercial Tankless Water Heater Repair in Norfolk

    Restaurants and laundromats in Norfolk often run two to six tankless units on a common manifold. When one unit drops out, the rest carry the load and scale up sooner, so we check the cascade controller, the isolation valves on each heater and the water quality coming in. A down kitchen loses money. We schedule around service hours when we can.

    Emergency Tankless Water Heater Repair in Norfolk, VA

    A tankless heater that dies on a cold morning in Norfolk is an emergency for a family of five. No-hot-water calls jump the line, and the truck carries the parts that fail most: flame rods, igniters, flow sensors, thermistors and inlet filters. A leaking heat exchanger gets isolated at the service valves and the part goes on order that day. Control boards take longer.

    Noritz Tankless Water Heater Repair in Norfolk, VA

    Noritz heaters hold up well, but their flow sensors and vent connections still wear out. A code 90 points to combustion trouble, often a dirty fan or a restricted intake. Our tech cleans the burner and fan, checks every vent joint, and compares the flow sensor reading to the spec sheet. Gaskets get replaced if they leak.

    Same-Day Tankless Repair in Norfolk

    Water dripping from inside the cabinet, or a fault you cannot reset, needs a look the same day. We bring sensors, igniters, flame rods and filters, so a good share of visits end with hot water.     Navien Tankless Water Heater Repair in Norfolk

    Navien condensing units run a second stainless exchanger that pulls heat from the exhaust. That makes condensate, and a plugged trap or a full neutralizer will shut the heater down with a drain or flue error. On NPE-A models we also test the built-in recirculation pump, after clearing the trap and checking vent pitch. Then we fire it at low and high flow.
