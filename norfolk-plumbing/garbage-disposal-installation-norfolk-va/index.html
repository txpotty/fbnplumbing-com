    Double bowl sinks put the disposal on one side and a continuous waste or end outlet tee on the other.     Batch-Feed Units for Kitchens Without a Switch in Norfolk

    Batch-feed disposals only run when the stopper lid is in place, which adds safety for families with kids. Because they need no wall switch, they suit kitchens where a new switch would mean cutting drywall or a tile backsplash. The drain hookup is the usual setup.

    Switching From Batch-Feed to Continuous-Feed in Norfolk, VA

    Going from batch-feed to continuous-feed means adding a way to switch it on. That can be a wall switch, an air switch button on the sink deck or a switched outlet under the sink, and the choice depends on how hard it is to run wire to the spot you want. Air switches avoid new wiring in the wall. The drain usually stays the same.
