    High-Efficiency Water Softener Installation in Norfolk

    High-efficiency softeners use upflow brining and metered valves to cut salt and water use. A new metered unit often uses far less salt and water than an older model on a timer, which shows up on both bills. The savings add up. Several models are available.

    Water Softener Installation for New Construction in Norfolk, VA

    Building a home in Norfolk, VA? New construction is the cheapest time to add a softener loop, because the walls are open and the main can be routed past the garage or utility room without cutting finished drywall. Builders call us in early. Hose bibs can stay on hard water.

    Fleck Water Softener Installation in Norfolk, VA

    Fleck valves, especially the 5600SXT and 7000SXT, are on a lot of the softeners we install. They are simple to program, easy to rebuild and parts are stocked almost everywhere, which matters when a seal fails ten years in. We program yours to your water. We confirm the setup before leaving.

    Water Hardness Testing and Programming in Norfolk

    Testing is what makes the settings right. We test for hardness, iron and chlorine before installing, then program the regeneration frequency and salt dose so you get soft water without wasting salt. Iron changes the math. We review the settings with you before we leave.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Norfolk, VA

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. FBN Plumbing sets them up with the right resin and brine tanks. A metered control valve regenerates only when needed, which keeps salt and water use down. Most effective option.

    Commercial Water Softener Installation in Norfolk, VA

    Restaurants, hotels and laundromats feel hard water in their equipment first. We install bigger tanks or twin alternating units for businesses, which keeps soft water flowing during regeneration. Equipment lasts longer. We schedule around your hours.

    Softener Loop Installation in Norfolk

    No loop? A loop is the plumbing that routes the main line through a softener, and many older homes were built without one. We cut it in for you. Outdoor taps can stay on hard water, which saves salt. Future service gets easier too.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Norfolk

    Salt-free conditioners do not soften water, and we say so up front. They change the minerals into crystals that do not stick to pipe walls and heating elements, so scale drops off without adding sodium or using a drain line. Little upkeep is needed. They suit moderate hardness.
