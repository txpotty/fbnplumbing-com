    Why Choose FBN Plumbing for Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Norfolk, VA?

    An expansion tank is cheap insurance for a water heater that costs much more. Skipping it can shave years off the heater, because every heating cycle on a closed system spikes the pressure and stresses the tank welds, the fittings and the relief valve. It is a common failure.

    • System Protection, The tank takes up the extra volume from heating, so your heater and pipes are not strained.
    • Code Compliance, Many local codes require a tank on new heaters where the system is closed.

    Standard Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Norfolk

    Most tank-style water heaters on a closed system need an expansion tank on the cold supply. We tee it into the cold line a few feet from the heater, precharge the air side to your measured house pressure, and strap it to the wall so the weight never hangs on the pipe in your Norfolk home. We watch the gauge through one heat cycle.

    Brands and Quality Products in Norfolk, VA

    Potable-rated tanks are the only kind we install on drinking water. Watts, Amtrol and Z-Flex all make good ones, with liners that keep water off bare steel and diaphragms rated for drinking water, which matters because a hydronic heating tank looks similar but is not approved for your supply in Norfolk, VA. The label tells you which is which.

    Commercial and Multi-Unit Expansion Tanks in Norfolk

    Apartment buildings and offices with several heaters need tanks sized for the whole hot water system. Storage tanks and recirc loops get counted in the sizing, floor-mount the larger tanks with proper supports, and plan the work to keep hot water interruptions short for tenants in Norfolk, VA. We can come after hours.

    Preventative Expansion Tank Maintenance in Norfolk, VA

    Air precharge leaks down slowly over the years, like a bike tire. We check it during water heater service, and with the water pressure relieved we top the charge back up to match your house pressure, then look over the fittings and the strap in Norfolk, VA. An undercharged tank wears out fast.

    Expansion Tank Replacement & Upgrades in Norfolk

    A waterlogged expansion tank does nothing at all. The old diaphragm tears and the tank fills with water, and from then on your system acts like it has no tank, so we replace it with a new, correctly sized unit and upsize if your household or heater has changed since the first one went in at your Norfolk home. The swap itself is quick.

    Brands and Tank Materials in Norfolk, VA

    Butyl diaphragms and lined chambers keep water from touching the steel shell. Private wells serve many rural Virginia homes, and some well water carries iron, manganese, low pH or sulfur that calls for treatment. We use stainless or lead-free brass connections that resist corrosion, and we avoid tanks made only for hydronic heating, since those are not approved for drinking water and can rust from the inside. Potable labels matter.

    Pressure Reducing Valve and Expansion Tank Pairing in Norfolk

    Pressure reducing valves are common, and they protect fixtures from high street pressure. The catch is they create a closed system, so we test the PRV output, replace it if it is creeping, and pair it with a correctly charged expansion tank so pressure stays steady throughout the house in Norfolk. Creeping PRVs are common.

    Expansion Tanks With Tankless Water Heaters in Norfolk, VA

    Tankless systems hold very little water, so most do not need an expansion tank. A recirc loop or buffer tank can change the answer, and we check for them before recommending anything, because nobody in Norfolk, VA should pay for a part that does not do a job.
