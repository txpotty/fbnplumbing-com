    Toilet Brands We Replace in Norfolk

    Brand matters less than model, because every major maker sells a great toilet and a mediocre one at nearly the same price. We look at the flush valve size and MaP score.     Replacing Pressure-Assisted Toilets in Norfolk, VA

    Pressure-assisted toilets use a sealed vessel in the tank that stores air under line pressure and pushes water through the bowl hard, which helps in houses with long, low-slope drain runs. They are loud. Replacement means matching the vessel brand for parts later, and we check that house pressure is in the range the unit needs.

    What a Standard Swap Includes in Norfolk

    The swap itself runs about two hours: water off, tank drained, old toilet out, flange scraped and inspected, new toilet set on a fresh seal, and a braided stainless supply line connected. Then the leveling. We caulk the base except for a small gap at the back so any future leak shows up instead of hiding under the floor.

    One-Piece Toilet Replacement in Norfolk, VA

    One-piece toilets have no tank-to-bowl joint, so there are fewer leak points and fewer crevices to clean. They cost more and weigh more. Getting one up a staircase takes two people, and we set it without dragging it across your tile so the floor stays unscratched and the wax ring lands centered on the flange.

    Commercial Toilet Replacement in Norfolk, VA

    Office and retail restrooms wear out toilets faster than any house, and a replacement there usually means a floor-mount or wall-hung bowl with a flushometer instead of a tank. Work gets scheduled around your business hours. Bowls are matched to the flush valve's gallons per flush, since a mismatch leaves waste behind or wastes water.

    Toilet Rough-In Adjustments in Norfolk, VA

    Rough-in is the distance from the finished wall to the center of the drain, usually 12 inches, sometimes 10 or 14 in older homes. Measure to the closet bolts, not the baseboard. If the new toilet's rough-in does not match, it either sits too far out or will not fit. An offset flange can correct an inch or two without moving the drain.

    Gravity-Flush Toilet Replacement in Norfolk

    Gravity-flush toilets are the simplest and most forgiving, and a well-made one with a 3 inch flush valve will outflush most of the older toilets it replaces. Replacement parts are on every hardware shelf. If the floor is uneven, we shim it level, then caulk the base and test several full flushes.
