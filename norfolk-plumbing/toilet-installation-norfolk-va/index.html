    Types of Toilet Installation in Norfolk, VA

    Toilet Replacement and Upgrades in Norfolk, VA

    Swapping an old 3.5 gallon toilet for a modern one is the easiest water savings in the house. The old unit comes out in about twenty minutes. The rest is flange cleanup and shimming the new bowl level on a floor that has settled with age. We haul the old one away.

    Two-Piece Toilet Installation in Norfolk

    Two-piece toilets come with the tank and bowl separate, joined by a rubber gasket and two or three bolts. We assemble them on the floor before setting. Even tightening, alternating sides, matters more than force, since uneven pressure either cracks the china or leaves one bolt weeping. It should not wobble.

    Pressure-Assisted Toilet Installation in Norfolk

    Pressure-assisted toilets have a sealed vessel inside the tank that uses line pressure to push the flush. They need at least 20 psi to work right. We check static pressure at a hose bib before recommending one, and we warn customers about the noise, since the flush is loud enough to hear across the house. They rarely clog.

    Rough-In Toilet Installation in Norfolk, VA

    Rough-in is the plumbing that goes in before walls and floors close up. For a toilet that means a 3 inch drain with a vent, plus a cold supply stub. The drain goes 12 inches out from the finished wall line, which means we add the drywall thickness to the framing measurement. Then the inspector sees it open.

    Comfort Height Toilet Installation in Norfolk, VA

    Comfort height bowls stand about two inches taller than standard ones. Consider who uses the bathroom first. Shorter adults and kids sometimes find the taller seat leaves their feet dangling, while anyone with bad knees or a hip replacement usually finds it much easier to sit and stand. Installation is the same as a standard bowl.

    Smart Toilet Installation in Norfolk, VA

    Smart toilets with heated seats and built-in bidets need two things the old toilet did not: power and a dedicated water connection. Most require a GFCI outlet within reach of the cord. The water connection and mounting are ours, and when an outlet has to be added we schedule a licensed electrician instead of doing that wiring. Then we test every button.

    Wall-Hung Carrier Systems in Norfolk

    In-wall carriers from Geberit and Grohe each mount a little differently, and the instructions matter. We follow the maker's frame height chart exactly. A frame set wrong puts the bowl too low or too high, and once tile is up there is no adjusting it. Measure twice here.

    High-Efficiency Toilet Installation in Norfolk, VA

    High-efficiency toilets flush on 1.28 gallons or less, against 3.5 gallons or more for toilets from before the early 1990s. Check for the WaterSense label. A MaP rating of 800 grams or better is what we look for, since a weak low-flow toilet that needs double flushing saves no water at all. Dual-flush models add a lighter half flush.

Soft plywood around the flange gets cut out and replaced with new subfloor, glued and screwed, so the toilet sits on something solid. It adds an hour, sometimes two.
