    PEX and CPVC Pipe Repair in Norfolk, VA

    Plastic supply pipe like PEX and CPVC is common in homes built over the last few decades. Each fails its own way. PEX can fail at crimp rings, older brass fittings or where it was exposed to UV light in an attic, while CPVC turns brittle with age and heat and cracks when bumped, and we use parts and methods made for each pipe type. Brittle CPVC needs a gentle hand.

    PEX Pipe Repair and Installation in Norfolk

    PEX is flexible and handles freezing better than rigid pipe. It still fails. Most PEX leaks happen at a crimp ring that was not fully seated, an early brass fitting that dezincified or a section damaged by sunlight in an attic, so we replace the failed joint with the right fitting for that brand of PEX. Usually a quick fix.

    Frozen and Burst Line Repair in Norfolk

    Pipes in exterior walls, attics and crawlspaces freeze first. Frozen water cracks it. The split usually does not leak until the ice thaws, so a pipe that froze overnight may start spraying the next afternoon once the house warms up. We repair the split and add insulation. Heat tape is an option on problem runs.

    Drain and Waste Pipe Repair in Norfolk

    Drain pipe made of ABS, PVC or cast iron can crack, separate or leak at joints. Stains give it away. A leaking drain often shows up as a stain below a bathroom or a musty smell in a cabinet, and we locate the leak, replace the damaged section and test the drain under full flow before closing up. We look at venting as well.
