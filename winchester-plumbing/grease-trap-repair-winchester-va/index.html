    Damaged Baffle Repair in Winchester

    Lid, Gasket and Riser Repair in Winchester, VA

    Grease Interceptor Clog Removal in Winchester

    Grease does not stay liquid for long once it cools in a pipe. Within a few feet of the sink it hardens like candle wax along the top of the pipe.     Baffle Repair and Replacement in Winchester

    Inside a concrete gravity interceptor the baffle is usually a poured or precast wall with a crossover opening. A crumbling wall lets grease slip into the second compartment and out the outlet.
