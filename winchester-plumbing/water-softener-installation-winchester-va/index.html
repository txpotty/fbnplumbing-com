    Softener Loop Installation in Winchester

    No loop? A softener loop is a pair of capped stubs on the main line, usually in the garage, that lets a softener be connected without cutting pipe later. We cut it in for you. Outdoor taps can stay on hard water, which saves salt. Future service gets easier too.

    Ecodyne Water Softener Installation in Winchester

    Ecodyne softeners are sold at many home stores, and we install them for homeowners who already bought one. Install follows the manufacturer manual for bypass, drain and overflow, then we set hardness and regeneration. We install store-bought units. Programming is part of the job.

    Whole-Home Water Softener Installation in Winchester

    Whole-home softeners treat every gallon that enters the house, right after the main shutoff. Placing it on the main means the water heater, the dishwasher and every fixture get soft water, not just the kitchen. Sizing comes first. Hardness and household use set the grain capacity.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Winchester, VA

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. FBN Plumbing sets them up with the right resin and brine tanks. We program regeneration by metered water use so the system only recharges when the resin is actually spent, which saves a lot of salt. They work best on very hard water.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Winchester

    Ion exchange is the only method that truly removes hardness from water. We install salt-based softeners with a resin tank sized to your grains per gallon, a brine tank, a bypass valve and a drain line that ends at a code-approved air gap. Then we program it. We set regeneration overnight.

    Commercial Water Softener Installation in Winchester, VA

    Restaurants, hotels and laundromats feel hard water in their equipment first. Commercial softeners we install use larger resin tanks or twin-tank alternating systems so soft water is available around the clock, even during regeneration. Equipment lasts longer. We schedule around your hours.

    Fleck Water Softener Installation in Winchester, VA

    Fleck valves, especially the 5600SXT and 7000SXT, are on a lot of the softeners we install. They are simple to program, easy to rebuild and parts are stocked almost everywhere, which matters when a seal fails ten years in. We program yours to your water. We confirm the setup before leaving.
