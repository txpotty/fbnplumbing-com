    Causes of Sewer Line Failure in Winchester, VA

    Shifting soil is one of the main reasons sewer joints separate, crack or drop out of grade over the years. Expansive clays in some areas expand when wet and shrink when dry, which can crack foundations and shift buried sewer lines. Roots are the other big one, since a hairline gap at a clay joint is enough for a feeder root to get in and then swell into a mat that catches paper. Then age finishes the job. Cast iron scales and thins, and clay bells crack under load.

    Sectional Point Repair in Winchester

    A point repair is a short liner, often 3 to 5 feet long, set over one bad spot. It rides in on an inflatable packer, guided by the camera footage, and the packer inflates to press the resin-soaked sleeve against the pipe wall until it cures. It fixes a single crack or root entry, not a whole failing line. Sound lines with one bad joint are the right candidates.
