    Why Polybutylene Fails in Winchester

    Polybutylene reacts with oxidizers like chlorine that water utilities use to disinfect tap water. The inner wall of the pipe and fittings slowly turns brittle, and small cracks grow until the pipe splits. Private wells serve many rural Virginia homes, and some well water carries iron, manganese, low pH or sulfur that calls for treatment. Heat makes it worse. Hot lines and fittings near the water heater fail more than anything else.

    Manufactured Home Poly Replacement in Winchester, VA

    Manufactured homes from the poly era often have polybutylene lines in the floor or the belly underneath. We replace those lines with PEX sized for the home and secure them properly to the frame. Exposed lines get checked. Disconnecting hoses, shutting off outdoor spigots and keeping crawlspace vents closed in winter are easy ways to prevent freeze damage. Heat tape and insulation on exposed runs get inspected and replaced where needed, so the new supply lines are protected under the home.

    Emergency Polybutylene Pipe Repair in Winchester, VA
