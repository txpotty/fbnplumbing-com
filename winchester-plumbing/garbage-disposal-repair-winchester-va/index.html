    Humming Disposal Repair in Winchester

    Humming with no spin almost always means a jam. Spoons, glass, bones and pits cause most of them. We cut power, work the flywheel free with a hex wrench on the bottom of the unit, remove the object and then test the motor and the reset button with water running.

    Noisy Disposal Repair in Winchester

    Grinding, rattling or a hum that sounds rough usually mean trouble inside the unit. Loose mounting rings rattle too. We check the mount first, then the chamber for debris, then listen to the motor with the chamber empty, because a motor that whines with nothing in it has bad bearings.

    Disposal Reset and Electrical Repair in Winchester

    When a disposal will not turn on at all, the thermal overload may have tripped or the switch may be bad. The reset, breaker, wall switch and wiring all get tested, along with air switches and the magnetic lids on batch-feed units. Most electrical faults are easy once found.

    Jam Clearing and Motor Repair in Winchester

    Bones, fruit pits and bottle caps lodge between the grind ring and the impeller lugs, and the motor stalls. A motor that stalled for a long time may have overheated, so we test it under load before calling the job done.
