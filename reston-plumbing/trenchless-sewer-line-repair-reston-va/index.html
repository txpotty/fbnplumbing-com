    Cured-In-Place Pipe Lining in Reston, VA

    CIPP stands for cured-in-place pipe. We wet out a felt tube with epoxy resin, invert it into the cleaned line with air pressure, and hold it against the old pipe wall until the resin hardens. Cure time runs a few hours. The finished liner seals cracks and leaking joints in the original pipe.

    Sewer Line Video Inspection in Reston

    We record the full camera run on every job, from the cleanout out to the city main or the septic tank. You get the file. On the footage you can see what we see, roots at a joint at 22 feet and an offset at 38 feet where the pipe dropped. That map drives the repair plan.

    Root Intrusion Fixes in Reston, VA

    Roots enter at the joints, where clay bells or cast iron hubs have a small gap or a failed gasket. Once inside, they branch into a mat that fills the pipe. We cut them out with a mechanical root cutter or a hydro jet, then line the pipe so those joints are sealed for good and the line stops needing a cleaning every few months. The roots cannot get back in.

    Trenchless Drain Pipe Repair in Reston, VA

    Smaller drain lines inside the house can be lined too, including 2-inch kitchen drains and the 3-inch cast iron stacks found in many older homes. We work through cleanouts and roof vents, so the drywall and tile stay where they are. After curing, a small robotic cutter reopens each branch connection from inside the pipe. That saves opening a finished wall.

    Trenchless vs Traditional Sewer Repair Cost in Reston, VA

    Point repairs often cost $1,500 to $3,500, while full lining of a residential lateral often runs $4,000 to $12,000 depending on length and diameter. Pipe bursting typically ranges from $5,000 to $15,000. On a short, shallow run, a traditional dig can come in lower. Once you add back concrete and sod, the gap usually closes, and we price both options side by side so you can see the difference.

    Cast Iron Sewer Line Lining in Reston, VA

    Cast iron rusts from the inside out, and the bottom of the pipe goes first. That is called channel rot. We descale the pipe with chain knockers or carbide cutters spinning on a flexible shaft, which brings the wall back to bare metal so a liner can bond to it. Under a slab, lining beats breaking up the floor in two rooms.

    Trenchless vs Traditional Sewer Repair Costs in Reston, VA

    Pipe Bursting in Reston, VA

    Two pits are standard for a burst, one at the house end near the foundation and one out near the street or the city tap. Before pulling, the crew heat-fuses sections of HDPE into one continuous length on the ground. The winch or hydraulic puller then drags the bursting head and the new pipe through. A 60-foot pull often takes under an hour.
