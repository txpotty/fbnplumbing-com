    Home Tankless Flushing in Reston

    Water hardness in Virginia varies by region, with harder water in the western limestone valleys and softer water closer to the coast. Inside a tankless heater, that water runs through a small copper or stainless coil while the burner fires, and every minute of hot water leaves a little calcium behind. A pump on the service valves pushes descaling solution through the coil for roughly an hour. The flow returns. Temperatures stop swinging, and the unit quits shutting down on high-limit.
