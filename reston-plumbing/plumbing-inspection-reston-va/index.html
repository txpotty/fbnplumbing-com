    What a Plumbing Inspection Covers in Reston, VA

    Preventive Maintenance Checks in Reston, VA

    A maintenance check is a lighter version of a full inspection for owners who want to stay ahead of problems. We flush the water heater, exercise the main shutoff and angle stops, check pressure and look for slow drains and weeping fittings. It takes about an hour. Doing this every year or two adds years to a water heater and keeps the shutoffs working for the day you really need them.

    Commercial Plumbing Inspections in Reston, VA

    Commercial buildings have equipment homes do not, so our commercial inspections cover more ground. We look at backflow assemblies, grease interceptors, commercial water heaters, flush valves and the building sewer. Records matter for inspectors. Managers use the report to fix problems before an inspector or water provider flags them.

    Whole-Home Plumbing Inspections in Reston

    Our whole-home inspections focus on the problems we see most in Virginia houses. That list starts with old supply pipe, worn angle stops, aging water heaters, slow drains and sewer lines with roots or sags. Each gets tested, not guessed at. A corroded fitting or partial blockage found now will not become a ceiling stain or a holiday backup later.

    Water Pressure and Supply Pipe Review in Reston, VA

    We measure static water pressure and check the pressure reducing valve if one is installed. High pressure ages fixtures fast. Anything over 80 psi cuts the life of hoses, fixtures and heaters, and we note the supply pipe type, from copper and CPVC to poly, Kitec and galvanized.

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Reston, VA

    Many older homes have clay, cast iron or Orangeburg sewer lines that can crack, sag or fill with roots. We run a high-resolution camera from a cleanout to the city connection or septic tank. The video shows offsets, bellies, roots and breaks along with their depth and location, and you keep a copy of the recording for your records or for a contractor.

    Water Heater Safety Inspection in Reston

    A water heater stores a lot of hot water under pressure, so safety items matter. We test the T and P valve, confirm its discharge line runs to a safe place, look for an expansion tank where one is needed and check the drain pan. Gas units get a CO check. Backdrafting heaters can push combustion gases indoors, which is why we test the vent and report problems before we leave.

    Fixture, Drain and Shutoff Valve Check in Reston, VA

    We run every faucet, shower and toilet and look for slow drains, leaks and weak flow. Shutoffs get tested too. Angle stops and the main shutoff valve often seize over the years, and we also listen for gurgling drains that point to venting problems, since small fixes like these are easy to plan once you know about them.

    Leak Detection and Slab Screening in Reston

    A spinning water meter with all fixtures off is a clear sign of a hidden leak. We also check for warm floor spots. Meter tests, pressure loss checks and a look for damp baseboards come first, and slab homes get acoustic and thermal follow-up. Catching a slab leak early limits damage to floors and foundations.
