    Showerhead and Arm Leak Repair in Reston

    Shower arms leak at the threads more than people realize. A leak at the arm threads follows the pipe into the wall, and it shows up as bubbling paint in the next room. Fresh thread tape fixes it. We can add a handheld or slide bar while we are there.

    Showerhead Repair and Replacement in Reston

    Showerheads clog from the inside out. Scale builds behind the face plate and in the flow restrictor, so the spray goes uneven, shoots sideways or drops to a trickle even when the valve is wide open. Soaking helps sometimes. When it does not, we install a new head with soft rubber nozzles that you can wipe clean with a thumb.

    Shower Valve Replacement in Reston

    Temperature that swings or a handle that fights you points to the valve. The new valve will have scald protection. You can step up to a thermostatic valve for tighter control.

    Weak Shower Spray Repair in Reston, VA

    Most weak showers come down to the head. Scale packs the nozzles and the restrictor screen behind them, and the spray drops off a little at a time until one day it feels like a garden mister. A soak can bring it back. A clogged cartridge gets replaced, then we test the flow.

    Low Shower Pressure Repair in Reston, VA

    Low pressure at the shower with good pressure at the sink narrows it down fast. That points to the head, the flow restrictor or the cartridge, not the main or the water heater, and it is usually something we can fix within an hour. We start with the head.     Commercial Shower Service in Reston

    Shower Temperature Swing Repair in Reston

    Cold blasts when a toilet flushes mean the pressure-balance spool is stuck.
