    What Our Sewer Clearing Covers in Reston, VA

    Preventative Sewer Line Maintenance in Reston

    Lines that clog from roots once a year can be cleaned before they clog. That beats a 2 a.m. backup. FBN Plumbing sets up scheduled cutting or jetting for homes in Reston with older clay or cast iron, typically on a one- to two-year cycle based on regrowth. Each visit includes a quick camera check.

    Tree Root Sewer Clogs in Reston

    Clay and older cast iron sewer pipe have a joint every few feet. Roots love those joints. Roots from trees and shrubs push into the gaps and fill the pipe, so we cut them with a sewer machine fitted with root saws and then run a camera to see how much damage remains. Ongoing root problems may call for a treatment schedule or lining.

    Main Line vs. Branch Line Clogs in Reston, VA

    One slow drain is a branch clog. Several slow drains is the main. Water rising in the shower when you flush is a strong sign the clog is past the point where those two lines join, so the pattern of slow drains tells us where to go in. Starting in the right place matters.

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Reston

    A sewer camera is a waterproof head on a push rod with a locator transmitter built in. It shows us everything. Cracks, offsets, sags, roots and heavy scale all show up on the screen, while the locator marks the spot and depth from above. That keeps digging to a minimum. You get a copy of the footage.

    Identifying and Repairing Damaged Pipes in Reston

    A clog that keeps coming back usually means broken pipe. No amount of clearing fixes a crack. Our camera finds the crack, offset or collapse, while the locator marks it so the repair can be quoted right. FBN Plumbing gives you options from a single dig to full replacement.

    Cleaning vs. Repairing a Sewer Line in Reston, VA

    A sound pipe with a grease or root clog just needs cleaning. Cracked pipe is different. Offset joints, cracks and collapsed sections keep clogging no matter how often they get cleared, so the camera footage decides whether you spend a few hundred dollars on cleaning or more on a spot fix, liner or replacement. We walk you through it.

    Commercial Sewer Line Clearing in Reston, VA

    A backed up sewer at a restaurant or office means closing the doors. Every hour costs money. FBN Plumbing clears commercial mains with heavy sectional machines and a high-flow jetter, and we can work overnight or before opening so customers and staff never notice we were there. Grease-heavy lines get jetted, not just cabled.

    Hydro-Jetting for Stubborn Clogs in Reston

    Some lines get cabled three times a year and still clog. The cable is not the fix. Those lines are usually coated in grease or scale, and a cable just cuts a small hole that closes back up. Jetting removes the coating down to the pipe wall. The results hold up far better.
