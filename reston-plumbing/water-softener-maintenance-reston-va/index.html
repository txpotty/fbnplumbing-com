    Softener Brands We Work On in Reston

    Most softeners sold in the last 20 years run on a handful of valve platforms, mainly Fleck, Clack, Autotrol and a few proprietary heads. The valve matters more than the brand. Kinetico and Culligan use their own designs, so we check part availability before quoting.

    Commercial Twin-Tank Maintenance in Reston

    Salons, car washes, hotels and restaurants put far more gallons through a softener than any house does, and a hard-water day can mean spotted glassware or a scaled ice machine.     Control Valve and Timer Diagnostics in Reston, VA

    The control valve runs every stage of regeneration, from backwash through brine draw to the final refill. We advance it manually through each cycle and watch for a motor that stalls, a drive gear that skips, a piston that drags or a drain that never shuts off. A worn piston is a common find.

    Resin Bed Checks and Regeneration Settings in Reston

    Resin does the actual softening. Each bead trades sodium for calcium and magnesium until it runs out of capacity. We compare the softened hardness against the gallons used since the last regeneration to tell whether the bed is still at full strength.

    Resin Rebedding in Reston

    Chlorine and chloramine in treated water slowly swell and soften resin beads, and iron or sediment can coat them until capacity drops off. A resin cleaner is the first thing we try. If the bed is spent, we empty out the old resin and refill the tank with new high-capacity media, which runs far less than a new softener.

    Regeneration Programming and Hardness Testing in Reston, VA

    Plenty of softeners still run factory settings. Either the installer left the defaults in, or the family got bigger and the settings stayed put. We enter your raw hardness and household size, then set a metered valve to regenerate by gallons used instead of every few days on a timer.

    Salt-Free Conditioner Service in Reston

    Salt-free conditioners, usually template assisted crystallization units, do not remove hardness. A hardness test still reads high afterward. They change the minerals into tiny crystals that are less likely to stick. The media needs replacing every three to five years, and the sediment prefilter ahead of it needs changing every few months, which is the part most owners skip.
