    Wall-Hung Commercial Bowl Replacement in Reston, VA

    Wall-hung commercial bowls sit on steel carriers inside the wall, and when one cracks we also check that the carrier bolts and the outlet gasket are still sound. A loose carrier will crack the next bowl. Flushometer valves get reconnected and tested before anyone uses the restroom again.

    Flange Repair During Bowl Replacement in Reston, VA

    The flange is the ring that anchors the bowl to the drain, and the only time anyone sees it is when the old bowl comes off. Look at it then or not at all. A cracked or sunken flange gets repaired before the new bowl goes down, since a new bowl on a bad flange will rock and leak within months.

    Bowl Replacement Versus Full Toilet Replacement in Reston, VA

    Bowl-only swaps make sense when the tank is newer and the bowl is still made, since a matched bowl often costs $100 to $350 while a new basic toilet starts around $200. Labor is similar either way. An old tank or a discontinued model usually tips it toward a full replacement, and we show you both prices.

    Cracked or Damaged Toilet Bowl Replacement in Reston

    Porcelain that cracks below the waterline leaks every time the bowl refills, and the water usually runs down inside the base where you will not see it for weeks. Hairline cracks still count. We pull the bowl, check the flange and the subfloor, then set a matching bowl on a fresh seal.

    Emergency Bowl Replacement in Reston

    Shut the angle stop behind the toilet the moment you see water coming from a cracked bowl, then pull towels around the base to slow the spread. Then call us. We stop the leak, remove the broken bowl safely and install a replacement or a full new toilet if no match is on hand. We also check for water in the floor below.

    Toilet Bowl Replacement for Older Homes in Reston, VA

    Houses built before the 1970s often have a lead closet bend or a cast iron flange caulked with oakum, and those need gentle handling when the bowl comes off. Old pipe cracks easily. Prying on the flange is something we avoid and use repair rings or flange adapters made for cast iron, so the drain stays intact.

    Pressure-Assist Toilet Bowl Replacement in Reston

    Pressure-assist toilets use a sealed vessel inside the tank, and the bowl is matched to that vessel's flush rate, so a bowl-only swap means finding the exact paired model. Many are not sold separately. If the pair is unavailable, a full replacement is the clean answer, and we will price both.

    Toilet Bowl Replacement for Remodels in Reston

    Remodels are a good time to change the bowl shape or height, as long as the rough-in stays the same and the new footprint covers the old flooring cutout. We check that before you order. We coordinate with your contractor or tile crew so the flange ends up at the right height above the finished floor.
