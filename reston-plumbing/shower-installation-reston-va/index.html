    Walk-In Shower Installation in Reston

    Walk-in showers depend on floor slope. Without a curb to hold water back, the floor has to pitch a quarter inch per foot toward the drain, and the waterproofing has to run well past the shower opening. We set that slope. We add blocking for grab bars while the studs are bare.

    Shower Valve and Trim Installation in Reston, VA

    The valve sits behind the tile for decades. We install pressure-balancing, thermostatic and diverter valves, set to the depth your trim kit needs and plumbed with integral stops when the valve has them. Handles and the head come later. Waiting keeps the trim clean during tiling.

    Shower Valve and Fixture Installation in Reston, VA

    Pressure-balancing valves are the baseline for any new shower. Thermostatic valves hold a set temperature and add volume control, which works well with rain heads and handhelds. Either one protects against scalding. The outlet count usually decides it.

    Steam Shower Plumbing in Reston

    Steam showers need four things: a generator, a water supply, a drain line and an electrical circuit. The enclosure needs a sealed door and a sloped ceiling.
