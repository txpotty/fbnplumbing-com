    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Reston

    Polybutylene was used in a lot of homes built in the 1980s and early 1990s. It fails without warning. The gray plastic turns brittle as chlorine in the water attacks it, and buried runs tend to split at fittings or where the pipe was bent tight during installation. Patching it just moves the next leak. We use PEX or copper.

    Water Main Underground Repair in Reston

    Your water main is the pipe between the meter and the house. You own that part. When it breaks, we locate the leak without digging, expose the pipe and cut out the bad section and join in new pipe with buried-rated fittings. Rusted steel lines rarely patch well.

    Underground Pipe Repair Cost Comparison in Reston, VA

    A single point repair on a shallow water line is the cheapest option. Pull-through costs more. It avoids open trenching and the long-term risk of more leaks on old pipe, and open trenching becomes necessary when the old path is unusable. We price all three.

    Trenchless Pipe Repair in Reston

    Trenchless repair replaces or relines buried pipe without opening its full length. Two small pits, usually. We can pull new PEX or HDPE through the old pipe path or line a sewer lateral with resin, so concrete and established plantings stay put. Not every pipe qualifies. The route and condition decide it.
