    Electric Tankless Water Heater Installation in Reston, VA

    Electric tankless heaters make sense in Reston, VA homes without gas, or for a single sink or a guest bath far from the main heater. A whole-home electric unit draws heavy amperage.     Propane Tankless Water Heater Installation in Reston

    Moving the heater is common on a tankless job because the old tank spot is not always the best place. Mounting near the gas meter on a garage or outside wall shortens the gas line and venting, though it can add distance to the bathrooms. We weigh both. Long hot runs may call for recirculation.

    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Reston, VA

    Calcium scale is what kills most tankless units early. Water hardness in Virginia varies by region, with more mineral-heavy water in the western limestone valleys and softer water closer to the coast. Scale coats the heat exchanger, cuts efficiency and triggers error codes, and left alone it can crack the exchanger and end the warranty claim before it starts. A yearly flush prevents it. We install isolation valves for that.
