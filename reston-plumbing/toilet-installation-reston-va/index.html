    Commercial Flushometer Toilets in Reston

    Sloan and Zurn flushometer valves are the standard on commercial toilets for a reason. There is no tank to refill.     Wall-Mounted Toilet Installation in Reston

    Wall-mounted toilets hang off a steel carrier bolted to the floor inside the wall. An access plate hides the tank. The carrier holds the whole load, so its feet get anchored to slab or framing and it gets plumbed before any wall finish goes on. Plan on a remodel, not a swap.

    Bathroom Remodel Toilet Installation in Reston, VA

    New tile over old vinyl can raise a bathroom floor by half an inch or more. That leaves the old flange sitting low.     Comfort Height Toilet Installation in Reston, VA

    Comfort height bowls stand about two inches taller than standard ones. Consider who uses the bathroom first. Kids and shorter adults may not like their feet off the floor, but people with sore knees or a new hip usually love the extra height. Installation is the same as a standard bowl.
