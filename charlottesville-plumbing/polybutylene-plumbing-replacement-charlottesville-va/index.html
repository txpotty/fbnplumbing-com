    Why Polybutylene Fails in Charlottesville

    Polybutylene reacts with oxidizers like chlorine that water utilities use to disinfect tap water. Over the years, the pipe and its acetal fittings get brittle from the inside, then crack along the inner wall until one splits under normal pressure. Even moderate hardness in Charlottesville can leave scale in water heaters over time, so regular flushing helps them run longer and more efficiently. Heat makes it worse. Hot lines and fittings near the water heater fail more than anything else.

    Emergency Polybutylene Pipe Repair in Charlottesville, VA

    A burst poly fitting can push water across a floor or through a ceiling in minutes. Shut the main first. We stop the water, splice in a temporary repair to restore service and then plan the full repipe on a calm schedule. Photos of the failure help with your insurance claim.

    Fitting Failure and Emergency Repair in Charlottesville

    Acetal plastic and copper crimp ring fittings are often the first part of a poly system to fail. A burst fitting in an attic or wall can cause major damage in minutes.
