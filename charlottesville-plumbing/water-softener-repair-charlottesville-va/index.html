    Salt Bridge and Brine Tank Service in Charlottesville, VA

    The tank looks full but makes no brine. A hard crust has formed and is keeping the salt from dissolving.     No-Salt Water Conditioner Repair in Charlottesville, VA

    No-salt conditioners do not remove minerals, they change how scale forms, but they still need service. Scale returning or a drop in water pressure usually means the conditioning media is spent or the sediment prefilter is clogged. FBN Plumbing repairs these units in Charlottesville, VA.     Brine Tank and Salt Bridge Repair in Charlottesville, VA

    A crust of salt across the brine tank stops regeneration, and hard water shows up at every tap within a day or two. We break up and remove the bridge and clean the tank. Cracked brine tanks on older units get replaced. Fill the tank halfway.

    Resin Bed Cleaning and Replacement in Charlottesville

    Fouled resin is the usual reason a softener regenerates on schedule but still sends hard water to every faucet in the house. Iron and sediment coat the beads so they cannot grab hardness. We test the resin, clean it with a resin cleaner or replace it. New resin restores full capacity.

    Salt Bridge & Brine Tank Repair in Charlottesville

    Salt bridges form when a hard crust builds across the brine tank and the salt below it stops dissolving into the water. Salt sits there unused, and regeneration fails. After breaking up the crust, we clear the sludge and test the float and brine well. A cracked brine tank gets swapped for a new one.

    Water Softener Leak Detection & Repair in Charlottesville, VA

    Cracked tanks, loose fittings and failed O-rings are behind most softener leaks. A small drip still wastes water and damages flooring. We find the source, whether it is the bypass, the valve-to-tank seal or the drain connection, and replace the seal or fitting that failed. Then it gets a pressure test.

    Water Softener Leak Repair in Charlottesville, VA

    Drips around fittings waste water. Our plumbers find the source, whether seals, O-rings, hoses or internal parts. We replace the failed part with silicone-lubricated O-rings rated for potable water in your Charlottesville, VA home and pressure test the connection.

    Salt Usage and Settings Repair in Charlottesville

    Too much salt or water use usually means settings that do not match your hardness or household. Older timer units regenerate on a schedule no matter how much water you used.
