    Main Line vs. Branch Line Clogs in Charlottesville, VA

    One slow drain is a branch clog. Several slow drains is the main. Water rising in the shower when you flush is a strong sign the clog is past the point where those two lines join, which is why we ask which fixtures are acting up before we decide where to open the line. The right entry point saves time.

    Cleaning vs. Repairing a Sewer Line in Charlottesville, VA

    A sound pipe with a grease or root clog just needs cleaning. Cracked pipe is different. Offset joints, cracks and collapsed sections keep clogging no matter how often they get cleared, and the camera tells you which path makes sense. We walk you through it.

    Residential Clogged Sewer Lines in Charlottesville

    Most home sewer clogs come from three things: roots, grease and wipes. Many times they team up. Grease coats the pipe, wipes catch on it and roots lock everything into a plug. We clear it with a sewer machine, then camera the line to see which one started it.

    Grease and Wipes Sewer Blockages in Charlottesville, VA

    Flushable wipes do not break down the way toilet paper does. Not even close. Mixed with grease that hardened in the main, they make a plug that a cable can only partly clear. Jetting scours the walls and flushes the debris downstream. Toss wipes in the trash.

    Tree Root Removal Services in Charlottesville

    Older Charlottesville homes with mature trees see more root clogs than anyone. The joints are the weak point.     Tree Root Invasion Removal in Charlottesville

    Roots find sewer lines by following moisture in the soil. A hairline gap is enough. Once inside, they grow into a dense mat that catches toilet paper and waste until the whole line stops flowing.     Hydro-Jetting Services in Charlottesville, VA

    Jetting sends water at thousands of psi through a nozzle that sprays backward and drives itself down the pipe. The pipe gets cleaned all the way around. A cable punches a hole through a grease plug, and a jetter clears the coating that starts the next clog. We camera the pipe first. Badly cracked pipe gets a gentler approach.

    Hydro-Jetting for Stubborn Clogs in Charlottesville

    Some lines get cabled three times a year and still clog. The cable is not the fix. Those lines are usually coated in grease or scale, and the cable only bores a narrow path through the middle that closes up again within weeks. Jetting removes the coating down to the pipe wall. The results hold up far better.
