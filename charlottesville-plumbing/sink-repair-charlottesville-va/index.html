    Recurring Sink Clog Repair in Charlottesville, VA

    Monthly clogs have a cause past the trap. We look past the wall with a longer cable or a camera for a sag or for buildup where the sink line meets the tub. Now and then it is a plugged vent. A sink that gurgles as it drains is the giveaway.

    Commercial Sink Repair in Charlottesville

    Restaurants and salons cannot wait a week for a sink. Three-compartment sinks and hand sinks have to work for the health inspector, so we schedule around your hours and bring commercial-grade faucets and basket strainers. Most commercial clogs are grease. We also check the grease interceptor connection when the drain backs up.

    Sink Parts Replacement in Charlottesville, VA

    Most sinks do not need replacing. A new tailpiece or pop-up assembly costs a fraction of a new sink and installs in an hour, and the bowl and faucet you already like stay right where they are. Finishes get matched when the part exists. Discontinued faucets sometimes force a full faucet swap.

    Pop-Up Stopper Repair in Charlottesville, VA

    Stoppers that will not hold water have a linkage problem. Either the clevis slipped on the lift rod or hair has wrapped the stopper so it will not seat. Cleaning fixes half of them. The other half get a new pop-up drain in a matching finish.

    Leaking P-Trap Fix in Charlottesville

    Check the slip nuts first. Overtightened plastic slip nuts crack, and the beveled washers inside them flatten until each joint weeps. The whole trap gets replaced as a kit. A 1-1/4 inch kit for bath sinks, 1-1/2 inch for kitchens.

    Kitchen Sink Repair in Charlottesville

    Kitchen sinks take the most abuse in the house. Basket strainers loosen and leak around the putty, pull-down sprayer hoses split inside the cabinet, and the disposal flange works loose from years of vibration. The wet part is the one we fix. A cracked composite or porcelain bowl is a replacement, and we will say so.

    Low Water Pressure at the Sink in Charlottesville, VA

    Unscrew the aerator before anything else. Most single-faucet pressure problems are a packed aerator screen, and after that we look at the cartridge and the angle stop for debris. Low pressure everywhere points to a different problem. We test the pressure regulator in that case.

    Overflow and Drain Flange Leak Repair in Charlottesville, VA

    Some leaks only show up when the bowl is full. A leak like that is usually the overflow gasket or the flange putty, which only get wet when the bowl fills. The drain comes out and gets resealed. Marble and some granite get silicone, because putty can stain them.
