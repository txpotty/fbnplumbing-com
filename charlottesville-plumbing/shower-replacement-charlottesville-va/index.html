    Frequently Asked Questions About Shower Replacement in Charlottesville

    Shower valve replacement usually costs $450 to $1,200. Shower pan replacement generally runs $1,500 to $4,000, and a full rebuild with tile can cost $8,000 to $20,000 or more depending on finishes and repairs. Valve swaps cost the least.

    Leaks that come back after repairs are the clearest sign. Cracked pans, returning mold, soft floors and chronic drain clogs all suggest it is time. Looks count too.

    For most homes, Moen or Delta valves are hard to beat. For the unit or the base, Sterling and American Standard make durable acrylic and fiberglass options, and Kohler makes plenty of both valves and bases. Budget shapes the pick.

    Staining and cracks on the pan or walls are early signs. Fixtures that drip constantly, mold that will not clean off and poor pressure from old plumbing all suggest replacement over repair, as does a need for better accessibility. We check before recommending.

    Housing in Virginia spans centuries, from historic homes with cast iron and galvanized pipe to newer subdivisions plumbed with PEX. Galvanized supplies and old drain lines often need updating during a shower replacement. We check the old pipes and recommend what to replace so the new shower is not tied to failing plumbing. Old pipe fails eventually.

    Valve and pan replacements may need one, depending on the city and scope. We check requirements and handle permits as needed.

    Cosmetic updates are fine to DIY. A full shower replacement involves plumbing, waterproofing and sometimes structural repair, and a mistake in any of them can lead to leaks and damage that cost far more than the job. Hire a licensed plumber.

    Tub-to-shower conversions are one of our most common jobs. We remove the tub and install a new pan, walls and fixtures in its place, moving the drain to suit the pan. It is easier to use.
