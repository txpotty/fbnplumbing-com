    Hot Water Slab Leak Repair in Charlottesville, VA

    Hot lines leak under slabs more than cold lines. Expansion wears them out. The pipe swells and shrinks against rock every time hot water flows, and a recirculation pump keeps that going constantly, thinning the wall. We repair or reroute the line and can adjust the pump to cut wear.

    Slab Leak Reroute in Charlottesville

    Older copper under a slab tends to leak again. A reroute stops the cycle. We leave the leaking run in the ground and pull new PEX through the attic and down the walls, cutting openings where they are easiest to patch, often behind a vanity or in a closet.
