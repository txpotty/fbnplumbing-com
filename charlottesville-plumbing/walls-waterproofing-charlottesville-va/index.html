    Cost-Effective Waterproofing Solutions in Charlottesville, VA

    Not every wet wall needs a $15,000 excavation.     Drainage System Integration in Charlottesville, VA

    Water has to go somewhere once a wall is sealed. Footing drains, interior drain tile and French drains in the yard all do the same job at different depths. We match the drain to where the water comes from, which might be a high water table, a yard that slopes toward the house, or a gutter dumping at the corner. We extend downspouts at least four feet from the wall.

    Interior Walls Waterproofing in Charlottesville, VA

    Interior drain tile handles water that already got through the wall. We cut a channel in the slab along the perimeter, lay perforated pipe in washed stone, and run it to a sump basin so the pump can push the water outside. The wall still gets wet. The difference is that the water no longer ends up on your floor. This route costs less than digging outside and makes sense when a patio or driveway sits against the foundation.

    Foundation Crack Repair & Sealing in Charlottesville

    Hairline cracks in poured concrete walls leak more water than their size suggests. We inject them with low-pressure polyurethane foam, which expands to fill the crack from the inside face to the soil side, or with epoxy when the crack also needs structural bonding. Horizontal cracks are different. A horizontal crack often signals soil pressure pushing the wall in, so we check for movement with a level before we seal anything. Vertical cracks are usually a one-visit job.

    Commercial Wall Waterproofing in Charlottesville

    Restaurant kitchens, gym locker rooms and salon wash stations put water on walls all day. We waterproof commercial showers, mop sink walls and restroom wet walls with membranes rated for constant water exposure, and we repair the supply and drain piping behind them if demolition turns up a leak.     Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Charlottesville, VA

    Wet baseboards outside the shower or a stain on the ceiling below usually point to the pan. We plug the drain, fill the pan with an inch or two of water, and mark the waterline and check it a day later. A drop means a leak. Rebuilding means a mortar pre-slope of a quarter inch per foot, a new PVC liner or bonded membrane, and a clamping drain tightened onto the liner. The new pan gets its own flood test before tile.

    Waterproofing for Older Homes in Charlottesville, VA

    Houses built before modern drainage rules often have no footing drain at all, or a clay tile drain that collapsed decades ago. Many homes built from the late 1970s into the 1990s used polybutylene supply lines, a material known for leaks and sudden failure. Fieldstone and old brick foundations need breathable products. A rigid coating on soft brick traps moisture and makes the face spall off over a few seasons, so we use lime-compatible or vapor-open coatings and pair them with a new interior drain. We repoint failed mortar joints before sealing.

    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Charlottesville

    Tile and grout are not waterproof. Water passes through grout every time you shower, so the layer behind the tile, cement board with a membrane or a foam board system, is what actually keeps the studs dry. We tear out failed tile, install new backer, and apply Schluter Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban at the thickness the manufacturer calls for. We reinforce corners, niches and valve penetrations with fabric or preformed pieces.
