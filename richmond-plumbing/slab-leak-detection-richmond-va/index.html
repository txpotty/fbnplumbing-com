    Slab Leak Reroutes in Richmond

    A reroute abandons the leaking line under the floor. New pipe runs through the walls or attic instead, which avoids breaking concrete and often costs less than opening a finished floor.     Hot Water Slab Leak Detection in Richmond

    The hot side fails more than the cold. Heat speeds up corrosion, and the pipe expands and contracts every time someone showers, wearing against the gravel and concrete until a pinhole opens. Warm floors and a water heater that runs nonstop are the signs. Energy bills often rise.

    Camera Inspection for Under-Slab Drains in Richmond, VA

    Under-slab drains crack as well.     Thermal Imaging for Slab Leaks in Richmond, VA

    A leaking hot line heats the concrete over it. A thermal camera shows that warm bloom through tile, vinyl and even thin carpet, often spreading outward from the leak point in a recognizable shape. Cold-side leaks will not show. It shrinks the search area before we listen.

    Acoustic Slab Leak Detection in Richmond

    Water escaping a pressurized pipe makes a hiss that carries up through concrete. A ground microphone picks it up.
