    Hot Water Slab Leak Repair in Richmond, VA

    Hot lines leak under slabs more than cold lines. Expansion wears them out. Each time hot water runs, the pipe expands against concrete and gravel, and recirculation pumps keep that cycle going all day, which grinds the pipe wall thin at the rub points. We repair or reroute the line and can set the recirculation pump on a timer.

    Slab Leak Repair for PEX and Copper Lines in Richmond, VA

    Copper and PEX fail in different ways under a slab. Copper pinholes. Old copper wears through where it touches gravel or reacts with the water, while PEX usually leaks at a fitting that was crimped short or chewed by rodents near an access point. Trucks in Richmond, VA stock fittings for each type.

    Direct Slab Leak Repair in Richmond, VA

    Direct repair is often the most practical fix for a single leak. A small section of slab gets cut. With the pipe exposed, the damaged length is cut out, new pipe is joined in, and the line is pressure tested before the soil goes back in and gets compacted in layers. The concrete is patched flush with the floor.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Repair in Richmond

    Cold leaks don't make warm spots. Watch the bill instead. Look for a rising bill, a meter that spins with nothing on or damp carpet, since a cold leak wets the soil as much as a hot one. Your Richmond home is no exception. We isolate the cold line and repair or reroute it.

    Slab Leak Pipe Rerouting in Richmond

    A reroute abandons the leaking pipe under the slab. The new pipe runs through the attic. For older Richmond homes, it bypasses the whole buried run of tired copper or galvanized and removes the chance of another leak there. We keep drywall cuts small at fixture walls.
