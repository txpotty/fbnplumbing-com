    Frequently Asked Questions About Grease Trap Cleaning in Richmond

    Small under-sink traps often start around $200 a visit. A large interceptor with a hardened grease cap can run past $1,000. The biggest variable is the gallons hauled, because disposal facilities charge by volume and a 1,500-gallon tank is a full truckload.

    Everything in the trap comes out, from the floating grease cap down to the sludge of food solids on the floor. Haulers call it brown grease. It is different from the yellow grease in your used fryer oil bin, which is recycled separately and should never be poured into the trap.

    Watch the floor sinks during dish rush. Water that lingers in them is an early warning. Other signs include a rancid smell near the dish station, grease showing up in the cleanout nearest the street, and drains that gurgle after the dish machine empties.

    FOG stands for fats, oils and grease. Warm, it pours like water, and cool, it sets like wax. Inside a sewer pipe it builds up in layers on the walls, traps food and wipes, and can shrink the opening to the size of a pencil.

    Zurn, Schier, Watts and Big Dipper units turn up across Virginia every week, along with Rockford traps and older steel boxes from makers that are long gone. We can usually find parts. For discontinued units we can often fit a universal baffle or flow control, or quote a replacement if the body has rusted through.

    Skimming off the top layer yourself is usually allowed. Disposing of it is where people get in trouble. Pretreatment programs often require a licensed hauler's record for each pump-out, so a kitchen that handles its own cleaning may have no paperwork to show when the inspector asks.

    Slow sinks come first. Gurgling floor drains and a sour smell by the dish station usually follow. Gray water rising through a floor sink means the trap is far past 25 percent and grease is already moving downstream.

    There is no parts warranty on a pump-out, since nothing gets installed. The work itself is guaranteed. Should it drain poorly after our visit, or a backup trace back to something we missed, the crew returns and makes it right.
