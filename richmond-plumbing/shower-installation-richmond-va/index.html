    Frameless glass walk-ins are popular, and the plumbing behind them has to be dead on.     Accessible showers start with blocking in the wall. Blocking goes in for grab bars, the valve goes where a seated person can reach it and a handheld goes on a slide bar. No high curbs. We follow ADA guidelines when you ask.

    Curbless Shower Installation in Richmond

    Curbless showers need the floor lowered in the shower area. A wood floor gets dropped joists or a recessed pan, while a slab gets cut and lowered so the floor flows straight in. A linear drain often works best. We plan the slope with your contractor before demo.

    Pressure-balancing valves are the baseline for any new shower. Thermostatic valves hold a set temperature and add volume control, which works well with rain heads and handhelds. Either one protects against scalding. We help you choose based on how many outlets you plan.
