    Recurring Clog Repair in Richmond

    Monthly clogs mean there is a physical cause. Cleaning will not stop it.     Kitchen Sink Drain Repair in Richmond, VA

    Kitchen drains take grease, food and soap scum. No drain clogs more often. We clear the branch, replace corroded or cracked tailpieces, fix leaking slip nuts and correct trap heights so the arm has the right fall to the wall. We also check the disposal and dishwasher connections.

    Leaking Drain Pipe Repair in Richmond, VA

    Drain leaks are low pressure, so they drip instead of spray. Most go unnoticed for months. Corrosion, shifting framing and cracked fittings are the usual causes, and we trace the leak by running each fixture and watching the joints, then cut out the damaged section and replace it with matching PVC, ABS or cast iron. Every repair is done to code.
