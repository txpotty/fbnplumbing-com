    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Richmond, VA

    Calcium scale is the most common reason tankless heaters fail. Even moderate hardness in Richmond can leave scale in water heaters over time, so regular flushing helps them run longer and more efficiently. Scale coats the heat exchanger, cuts efficiency and triggers error codes, and left alone it can crack the exchanger and end the warranty claim before it starts. Flush it yearly. We install isolation valves for that.

    Navien Tankless Water Heater Installation in Richmond

    Navien builds condensing tankless units with stainless heat exchangers, and several models come with a recirculation pump built in. That pump can push hot water toward a far bathroom on a schedule, which shortens the wait at the tap without adding a separate pump under the sink. Venting is usually two-inch or three-inch PVC. Condensing units also need a condensate drain.
