    Dishwasher Connection With Disposal Installation in Richmond

    Every new disposal has a plug sealing the dishwasher inlet. We knock it out with a screwdriver and hammer, fish the plug out of the chamber and connect the hose with a new clamp.     Replacing an Old Disposal in Richmond, VA

    Disposal Installation for Double Sinks in Richmond, VA

    On a double sink, the disposal goes on one bowl and a continuous waste or end outlet tee on the other. We lay out the drain so both bowls drain freely and the disposal outlet does not push water into the other bowl, which happens when the tee sits too close to the disposal discharge. We use heavy-wall fittings.
