    Commercial Sump Pump Solutions in Richmond

    Commercial basements, elevator pits and loading dock drains need pumps rated for more volume. We install duplex systems with alternating pumps. Downtime costs money. Work is scheduled around your operating hours.

    Liberty Pumps Installation in Richmond, VA

    Liberty builds quiet, well-made submersible pumps with good switch options. We install their 250 and 280 series often. Replacement switches are easy to get. We drill a 3/16 inch weep hole in the discharge to stop air lock.

    Sump Pump Replacement in Richmond, VA

    Floats stick and motors seize when a pump sits idle for months between storms. When we replace a pump we also check the basin for silt, look at the check valve flapper and run water through the discharge line to find any clogs. If the old one could not keep up, we size up. Testing is done with a full pit.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Richmond, VA

    Storms knock out power at the exact moment the pit is filling fastest. A battery backup pump sits beside the primary and runs on its own float. A good battery carries it through most outages. We mount the charger on the wall, off the floor.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Richmond, VA

    Backup systems we install use sealed AGM batteries that do not need water. The charger keeps the battery topped off. Batteries need replacing every few years. Finished basements get the most out of them.

    Emergency Sump Pump Replacement in Richmond, VA

    A dead pump during a storm means the water is already climbing. Our trucks carry 1/3 and 1/2 HP submersibles, check valves and fittings, so in most homes we can pull the old pump and set a new one in one visit. Most swaps take about two hours. We test it with a full pit before leaving.

    Combination Sump Pump System Installation in Richmond, VA

    Two pumps on one riser cover both kinds of failure, a dead motor and a dead outlet. The primary runs on house power and handles the everyday inflow, while the battery unit waits above it on a separate float. Finished basements are the usual reason. We install them in homes where a flood would ruin flooring and drywall.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Richmond

    Submersible pumps live at the bottom of the pit with a sealed, oil-cooled motor. Because the motor is cooled by the surrounding water, they run quieter and can push more gallons per minute up a tall lift than most pedestal pumps. The pit lid sits flush. We seal the cord grommet and the lid on every install.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Installation in Richmond

    Most Richmond, VA sump pump jobs land between $600 and $1,800. That range covers a quality pump, a new check valve, the discharge fittings and the labor to set and test it in a pit that already exists. Digging a pit adds cost. Every line item is on the quote.

    Test it every month by pouring a bucket of water into the pit. Once a year, scoop out silt, check the float for free movement and walk outside to make sure the discharge outlet is clear of leaves and ice. It is quick.

    Three to five hours covers most standard jobs. New pits take longer. You will know the timeline before we start.

    Odd noises, rust, constant cycling or a pump that fails to start are the common signs. Age matters too. We can test it.

    For some homeowners, swapping a pump in a working pit is doable. New pits, discharge routing and electrical changes are better left to a plumber.

    Plan on seven to ten years for a well-kept pump. A pump that cycles every few minutes through a wet spring wears out faster than one that runs a few times a month, and cheap switches fail first. We can check yours.

    Have it cleaned and inspected once a year, ideally before the rainy season. The service covers the float switch, the check valve, the pit and a full test of the discharge line to the outside. Small fixes prevent big floods.

    Swapping a pump in an existing pit takes about two to three hours. A new pit can take a full day.
