    Frequently Asked Questions About Plumbers for Frozen Pipes in Richmond

    Costs range from about $200 for simple thawing to $1,500 or more for a burst pipe inside a finished wall. Where the pipe sits matters most. A frozen line in an open crawlspace is quick work, while a split pipe in a second-floor ceiling means opening drywall, repairing the pipe and leaving the opening ready for a drywall patch.

    A new freeze is not a warranty issue, but it is a warning sign. That pipe is still exposed. We will look at why it froze again and quote insulation, heat cable or a reroute into a heated space, because patching the same exposed run every winter costs more than fixing the cause once.

    Pipe location matters more than brand. Metal pipe cracks more easily. Older galvanized steel and copper in uninsulated attics, crawlspaces and outside walls are the most likely to split, while PEX in the same spot may freeze but often survives, though its fittings can still fail.

    Pulling the right permit protects homeowners at resale, and FBN Plumbing handles permits and inspections within the job. Plumbing codes generally require insulation or heat protection for water pipes in spaces that can drop below freezing, and they limit running supply lines in exterior walls unless the pipe is protected. We follow the adopted local version. Inspectors check it.

    No pipe is freeze-proof. PEX tolerates freezing better than most. It can stretch slightly as ice forms instead of splitting the way copper does, but the fittings at each end can still crack, so location and insulation matter more than the pipe itself.

    Usually not for a simple repair. Big repipes are different. If we replace a long section of supply line, reroute pipes into a different wall, or replace a backflow assembly, your jurisdiction may require a permit and an inspection, and we schedule it for you.

    Most thawing jobs take one to two hours. Burst repairs take one to four hours. Repairs inside finished ceilings or walls take longer because we open the drywall, repair the pipe, test it and leave the opening clean and ready for a patch, and multiple splits on one run add time as well.
