    Grinder Pump Installation in Richmond

    Grinder pumps chop waste into a fine slurry first, which lets them push through small pipe over long distances. A grinder makes sense when the sewer is distant, uphill or pressurized, such as a pressure sewer where each home pumps into a shared main. Grinders run pricier. A standard ejector often covers a basement bath.

    Commercial Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Richmond, VA

    Businesses in Richmond, VA with restrooms, kitchens or break rooms below grade need ejector systems that keep up with heavier, steadier use than a house. Commercial setups often run duplex pumps with an alternating panel, so one pump can fail and the other keeps the restrooms open. Downtime costs money. We size both pumps to carry the full load.

    Duplex and Commercial Ejector Systems in Richmond, VA

    Commercial buildings, multi-unit properties and busy homes may need two-pump setups with an alternating control panel. Alternating the lead pump spreads wear, and when one fails or flow spikes, the second starts on its own and the panel sounds an alarm. Redundancy matters here.

    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Richmond

    Sump pumps and ejector pumps look alike in a catalog but do very different jobs. A sump pump moves groundwater from a perimeter drain to the yard, while an ejector moves sewage from toilets and sinks to the sewer, and the ejector basin has to be sealed and vented because of the gas. They are not interchangeable.

    Zoeller Ejector Pump Installation in Richmond, VA

    Zoeller has made ejector pumps for decades, and its cast iron models are the ones we see still running in older basements. We install Zoeller for both new basins and drop-in replacements, and its standard models often fit existing pits and discharge piping without changes. Floats and switches are stocked locally.
