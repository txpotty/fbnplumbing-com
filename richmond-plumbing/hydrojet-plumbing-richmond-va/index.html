    Root Removal Jetting in Richmond

    Shade trees and shrubs send roots toward small cracks in clay and cast iron pipe. A root nozzle cuts them out. Jetting does not stop roots from returning, because the crack is still there, but it clears the pipe completely and lets us see on camera whether a spot repair or a liner would stop the regrowth.

    Sewer Camera Inspection Before Hydrojetting in Richmond

    Jetting a broken pipe can make the break worse. We camera every line before we set up the jetter. Footage shows the material and joint condition, which sets our nozzle and pressure.

    Tree Root Removal with Hydrojetting in Richmond

    Roots find their way into sewer lines through cracked joints, and older clay pipe has a joint every few feet. A root-cutting nozzle spins through the mass. The jet then flushes the debris to the main, and the camera marks the joints that let the roots in.

    After-Hours Hydrojet Service in Richmond

    Hydrojetting vs. Snaking in Richmond, VA

    Snaking pokes a hole through a clog. A jet scours the entire pipe wall. Snaking suits a one-time clog, while jetting fixes a line that clogs repeatedly.

    Hydrojetting vs Snaking Cost Comparison in Richmond, VA

    Snaking a main line typically costs $200 to $450, while residential hydrojetting usually runs $400 to $900 with a camera inspection. The jet is the bigger bill. Fewer repeat visits often make jetting cheaper over a few years.

    Main Sewer Line Hydrojetting in Richmond

    The main sewer carries every drain in the house, so a blockage there backs up the lowest fixture first. We jet the full main to the street tap. Four-inch mains get a heavy nozzle and high flow to push debris downstream.

    Scheduled Jetting Maintenance in Richmond
