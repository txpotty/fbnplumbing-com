    Sewer Line Backups and Blockages in Richmond

    Water coming up in the tub when the washer drains is a classic main line symptom. The clog is past the branches. We clear it from the nearest cleanout, then run the camera to see what caused it, since a grease clog and a root mass need very different fixes. Older Virginia homes with clay lines often show roots right at the joints. You get the footage either way.

    Root Intrusion Removal in Richmond, VA

    Roots enter through hairline cracks and loose joints, then grow into a mat that catches paper and grease. They come back fast. We clear the line with a root cutting head or a jetter with a chain flail, camera it afterward to see the condition of each joint, and show you whether a liner or a spot repair would stop the cycle for good. Mature trees in Richmond yards make this a common call.

    Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Richmond

    Clay and cast iron crack from settling soil, tree roots and vehicles parked over the line. The crack lets soil in and sewage out. We excavate at the marked spot, cut out the damaged length and install new Schedule 40 PVC with shielded stainless couplings rated for the transition between materials, then test the repair with water and video before closing the hole. Most of these are one-day jobs.

    Sewer Pipe Replacement Options in Richmond, VA

    Replacement makes sense when the camera shows failures every few feet. Patching that line is wasted money. We price open trench replacement, pipe bursting and full-length lining side by side for your Virginia property, with the footage, depth and surface restoration spelled out for each one. Depth, access and what sits on top of the line decide it.

    Tree Root Invasion Solutions in Richmond

    Roots in a sewer line are not the tree's fault. The pipe was leaking first. Warm, wet, nutrient-rich air escaping from a loose joint draws roots toward the line, and once they get in they thicken until they crack the pipe or separate the joint completely. We cut them out, then talk about the long-term fix. A liner or new pipe at that joint is what actually stops regrowth.

    Sewer Pipe Relining in Richmond, VA

    Relining builds a new pipe inside the old one. The old pipe gets jetted clean before anything else. A flexible liner saturated with epoxy is pulled or inverted into place, inflated against the old wall and left to cure, so every crack and leaking joint is covered by one jointless pipe. The finished pipe loses a little diameter. On a four-inch residential line, that rarely matters.

    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Richmond

    Some lines are too far gone for a liner. A fully collapsed section is one example. When the camera shows a collapse, a large offset or a belly deep enough to hold several inches of water, we open a trench, remove the old pipe and lay new Schedule 40 PVC on proper bedding at a quarter inch of fall per foot. We see this most in older neighborhoods with clay pipe. We stage the work so you lose sewer service for a few hours, not days.

    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Richmond

    A hydro-jetter pushes water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full inside of the pipe. A cable only punches a hole. That makes jetting the better choice for grease, scale and fine root growth, and we always jet a line before lining it. Brittle or broken pipe is a different story. We check the pipe on video before we jet it.
