    Standard InSinkErator Installation in Richmond

    A standard install goes into a sink that already has a drain and power. The old disposal comes down, the flange gets scraped clean and a new one goes in on fresh putty. Then the mount ring goes on. The unit hangs on the ring, locks with a quarter turn, and the discharge tube meets the trap on a new slip-joint washer. We connect power through a cord kit or the old hardwire and confirm the ground first.

    Batch Feed vs Continuous Feed in Richmond

    Continuous feed is the type most kitchens have. It runs while the switch is on. A batch feed unit needs its stopper locked in to run, keeping hands out of the chamber, and it needs no wall switch. We wire each one to match.

    Evolution Series Installation in Richmond

    Evolution units grind in two or three stages and carry extra sound insulation around the motor housing, which is why they sit taller and weigh more than a Badger. Clearance under the bowl matters here. Before install day we measure the drop from sink to trap arm so the unit fits. The Compact fits tight cabinets, and the Excel needs the most room.

    Evolution Upgrade Installs in Richmond, VA

    Quiet is the main thing people notice after an Evolution install. Insulation around the motor drops the noise a lot. Later they notice fewer drain clogs, since the staged grind breaks stringy food into finer pieces before it hits the trap. We check for vibration with the unit running.

    First-Time Disposal Installation in Richmond

    Kitchens that never had a disposal need some rework. First, the sink gets a disposal flange. Then the drain gets reworked, and a switched outlet goes in, with an electrician if there is no power in the cabinet yet. We also check the drain slope to the stack.

    Upgrading Your InSinkErator in Richmond, VA

    Moving up from a 1/3 HP builder unit to a 3/4 HP Evolution changes more than the grind. The new unit is taller. Because of that height, the discharge can end up below the trap arm, and we measure first so we can lower the trap if needed. The circuit gets checked for the bigger motor's draw too.

Both appliances get run together.
