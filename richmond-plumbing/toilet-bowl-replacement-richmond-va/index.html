    Flange Repair and Toilet Reset in Richmond, VA

    Toilet Bowl Replacement for Remodels in Richmond

    Remodels are a good time to change the bowl shape or height, as long as the rough-in stays the same and the new footprint covers the old flooring cutout. We check that before you order.     Flange Repair During Bowl Replacement in Richmond, VA

    The flange is the ring that anchors the bowl to the drain, and the only time anyone sees it is when the old bowl comes off. That is the moment to check it. We fix a cracked or sunken flange before the new bowl is set, since a new bowl on a bad flange will rock and leak within months.

    Commercial Toilet Bowl Replacement in Richmond

    Floor-mount commercial bowls take a beating in high-traffic restrooms, and the ones that crack usually do it at the bolt holes from years of being kicked or overtightened. We match the outlet and spud size. We reconnect the flushometer and test it before the restroom reopens.

    Old Toilet Upgrades in Richmond, VA

    Bowls from before 1994 were built for 3.5 gallon flushes or more, and pairing one with a new low-flow tank does not work because the trapway and rim are sized for the larger volume. The tank and bowl are engineered as a pair. For an old toilet, an efficiency upgrade usually means a whole new toilet, and we will price it that way.

    Cost-Effective Bowl Swaps in Richmond, VA

    Swapping only the bowl saves money when the tank is newer, the fill valve and flush valve are in good shape, and the bowl is still made in your color. White bowls are the easiest to find.
