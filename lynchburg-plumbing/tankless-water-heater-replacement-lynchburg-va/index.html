    Gas Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Lynchburg

    A gas tankless unit at full fire can pull 199,000 BTU, roughly five times what a 40 gallon tank burner uses. The gas line usually has to be 3/4-inch or larger.     Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Lynchburg

    Rinnai units we pull are usually 12 to 18 years old, and the failure tends to be a leaking heat exchanger or a dead control board. A current Rinnai often mounts on the same bracket. That keeps wall patching small. We match the vent type or convert to PVC on a condensing model.

    Point-of-Use Electric Tankless in Lynchburg

    Point-of-use electric tankless heaters under a sink are a different animal from whole-house units. They run on a single 20 or 30 amp circuit and work well for a far bathroom or an office sink. Swaps take an hour or two.

    Like-for-Like Tankless Swaps in Lynchburg

    A cracked heat exchanger or a failed control board on an older tankless is usually not worth repairing past the ten-year mark. We match the new unit to your gas line, vent type and mounting spot whenever we can. Most swaps take four to six hours.

    Recirculation Tankless Upgrades in Lynchburg, VA

    Waiting a minute for hot water at the far bathroom wastes thousands of gallons a year in a long single-story house. Navien A models and Rinnai units with internal pumps can recirculate through a return line or a crossover valve under the farthest sink. The pump runs on a timer. Mornings and evenings, usually.

    Condensing Tankless Replacement in Lynchburg

    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE or Rinnai Sensei pass 90 percent efficiency by pulling extra heat out of the exhaust. They vent through PVC. Acidic condensate goes through a neutralizer and then to an approved drain. Frequent swings between mild days and freezing nights through winter put steady stress on pipes and outdoor fixtures across Virginia. Showers and laundry run all year, so the gas savings do too.

    Commercial Tankless Replacement in Lynchburg

    Cascade controls on a commercial rack decide which unit fires first, and after one unit is replaced they need rebalancing. Skip that and the new unit wears out first. Descaling gets scheduled by usage.

    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Lynchburg, VA

    Exterior-mount tankless units hang on an outside wall and vent straight out the front, so there is no vent pipe to run. Sun and weather wear on the covers and wiring. Disconnecting hoses, shutting off outdoor spigots and keeping crawlspace vents closed in winter are easy ways to prevent freeze damage. On every outdoor swap, we check freeze protection and the pipe cover. They are usually the easiest swap.
