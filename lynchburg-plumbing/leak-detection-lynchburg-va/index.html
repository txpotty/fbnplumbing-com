    Underground Water Line Leak Detection in Lynchburg, VA

    The buried line from your meter to the house can leak for months without surfacing, especially in old galvanized pipe. Green grass in a dry spell is a clue. We trace the line with an electronic locator, then use a ground microphone to hear the leak, switching to tracer gas on plastic lines that do not carry sound.

    Slab Leak Detection in Lynchburg

    Concrete slab homes have supply lines buried in sand below the floor, and a pinhole there can run for months before anyone notices. A warm tile is the classic sign. We pressure test the hot and cold sides separately, then use a ground microphone to hear the leak through the concrete and confirm with a thermal camera when the leak is on the hot side.

    Leaks in Walls and Ceilings in Lynchburg, VA

    A brown ring on the ceiling under a bathroom usually points to a shower valve, a drain joint or a toilet wax ring. Our job is figuring out which. Thermal cameras show the cool wet area, a moisture meter confirms it and a borescope through a small hole lets us see the actual pipe before any big opening is made.

    Preventive Leak Inspections in Lynchburg, VA

    Checking for leaks once a year catches the small stuff. We test static pressure, read the meter with everything off and look at supply lines, water heater fittings and shutoff valves. Older homes with galvanized pipe or original water heater connectors get the most out of this, because those parts often fail together. Short visit, big savings.
