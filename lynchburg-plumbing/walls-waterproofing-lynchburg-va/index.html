    How We Keep Water Out of Walls in Lynchburg, VA

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Lynchburg

    Exterior waterproofing means digging down to the footing and coating the outside of the wall. It is the most reliable fix for a leaking basement, because the water never touches the block or concrete. We apply a rubberized asphalt or polymer membrane, add a dimple board to protect it, and set perforated drain pipe in gravel along the footing so water has somewhere to go. Basements are common in northern and western parts of Virginia, while crawlspaces and slabs are more typical in the east and south. We backfill after the drain is tested.

    Waterproofing for Older Homes in Lynchburg, VA

    Houses built before modern drainage rules often have no footing drain at all, or a clay tile drain that collapsed decades ago. Many homes built from the late 1970s into the 1990s used gray polybutylene supply lines, a material known for leaks and sudden failure. Fieldstone and old brick foundations need breathable products. Sealing soft old brick with a rigid coating traps water inside it and makes the face spall off over a few seasons, so we use lime-compatible or vapor-open coatings and pair them with a new interior drain. We repoint failed mortar joints before sealing.

    Commercial Walls Waterproofing in Lynchburg

    Commercial basements, elevator pits and mechanical rooms in Lynchburg take on water the same way houses do, just with more at stake. A wet electrical room can shut a business down. We schedule around operating hours, work with property managers on access, and follow the waterproofing specs written into the building plans for below-grade walls. We keep downtime short.

    Crawl Space Encapsulation in Lynchburg

    Encapsulation seals the whole crawl space, walls and floor, with a reinforced poly liner. We run 12 to 20 mil liner up the walls, tape every seam, seal around piers and pipe penetrations, and usually add a dehumidifier to hold humidity under 60 percent. Frequent swings between mild days and freezing nights through winter put steady stress on pipes and outdoor fixtures across Virginia. Wood rot slows to a stop. Drier air under the floor also cuts down on the musty smell in the house.

    Foundation Crack Repair in Lynchburg

    Crack injection takes about two hours per crack. We set injection ports every 8 to 12 inches along the crack, seal the surface with epoxy paste, and pump in resin from the bottom port up until it pushes out the next port. Polyurethane flexes a bit if the wall shifts, and epoxy bonds the two sides back together. The surface paste gets ground off once it cures.

    Below-Grade and Basement Wall Waterproofing in Lynchburg

    Many homes built from the late 1970s into the 1990s used polybutylene supply lines, a material known for leaks and sudden failure. Any wall below grade sits in soil that gets saturated after heavy rain or snowmelt, and that water pushes against the wall with real hydrostatic pressure. We inject cracks, coat the wall inside or out, and install drain tile where water pools along the footing. A sump goes in if groundwater sits high. Some basements never need a sump.

    Foundation Crack Repair & Sealing in Lynchburg

Most vertical cracks get sealed in one visit.
