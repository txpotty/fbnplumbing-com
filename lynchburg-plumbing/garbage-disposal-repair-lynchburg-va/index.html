    InSinkErator and Other Brand Repairs in Lynchburg, VA

    InSinkErator, Waste King and Moen each use different mounts, so a flange leak is fixed with that brand's kit.     Leak Detection and Repair in Lynchburg, VA

    There are four usual leak points on a disposal: the sink flange, the dishwasher inlet, the discharge tube and the housing itself. We dry everything, run water with a little food coloring and watch to see which joint shows color first, since water runs down the outside of the unit and hides the real source. Most leak points can be repaired. A leak from the housing means it is time for a new unit.

    Noise and Odor Diagnosis in Lynchburg

    A rattle that stops after a few seconds is usually a spoon or a pit bouncing in the chamber. A constant high whine means something else. A steady whine usually points to failing motor bearings, and the noise keeps building until the motor locks up. Most smells start at the splash guard. We pull it, clean the chamber walls and replace the guard if it is torn.
