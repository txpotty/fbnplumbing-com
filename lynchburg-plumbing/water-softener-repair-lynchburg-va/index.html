    Water Softener Problems We Fix in Lynchburg, VA

    Emergency Water Softener Repair in Lynchburg

    A softener stuck in regeneration can run water to the drain for hours or flood a utility room. Urgent calls like that get priority. The crew in Lynchburg puts the unit in bypass first, then finds out whether the valve, the timer or the drain line caused it. Usually a stuck piston or a dead motor is to blame, and both can be replaced the same day.

    Salt Usage and Settings Repair in Lynchburg

    Too much salt or water use usually means settings that do not match your hardness or household. An old timer head regenerates by the calendar, used or not. After retesting hardness, we reprogram the valve or swap in a metered head that regenerates only when needed. Salt bills drop.

    Resin Testing and Replacement in Lynchburg

    Resin beads trap hardness minerals and release them during regeneration. Chlorine and chloramine in city water slowly break resin down until it turns to mush. We test resin capacity, then clean it with resin cleaner or replace it, and a solid tank and valve with fresh resin works like new for years. Mushy resin can clog the valve.

    Salt Bridge & Brine Tank Repair in Lynchburg

    Salt bridges form when a hard crust builds across the brine tank and the salt below it stops dissolving into the water. The tank looks full, but the softener cannot make brine. After breaking up the crust, we clear the sludge and test the float and brine well. A cracked brine tank gets swapped for a new one.

    Control Valve Repair in Lynchburg

    Water that runs to the drain all day usually means the control valve is stuck between cycles. FBN Plumbing technicians rebuild these valves with the correct seal and spacer kit for your model. Mechanical or electronic, we fix the timing. Common parts are on the truck, so most valve repairs are finished the same day without a second trip to the supply house.

    No-Salt Water Conditioner Repair in Lynchburg, VA

    No-salt conditioners do not remove minerals, they change how scale forms, but they still need service. Scale returning or a drop in water pressure usually means the conditioning media is spent or the sediment prefilter is clogged. FBN Plumbing repairs these units in Lynchburg, VA. Media lasts a few years.

    Popular Water Softener Brand Repair in Lynchburg

    GE, Whirlpool, Culligan, Kenmore, EcoPure and Morton make the popular softeners we repair most often, along with the private-label units from hardware stores. Every brand has its own valve layout and weak points. FBN Plumbing technicians know those designs, so diagnosis is quick. Make or model, the repair in your Lynchburg garage is done right.

    Bypass and Fitting Leak Repair in Lynchburg, VA

    Bypass valves, the O-rings where the valve meets the tank, cracked fittings and the drain line are the usual leak points. A minor drip still wastes water and damages floors and cabinets. We replace O-rings or fittings and test under pressure. A cracked mineral tank usually means replacement.
