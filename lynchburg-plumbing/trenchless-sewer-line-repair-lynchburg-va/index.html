    Causes of Sewer Line Failure in Lynchburg, VA

    Shifting soil is one of the main reasons sewer joints pull apart or crack. Expansive clays in some areas swell when wet and shrink when dry, which can crack foundations and shift buried sewer lines. Roots are the other big one, since a hairline gap at a clay joint is enough for a feeder root to get in and then swell into a mat that catches paper. Then age finishes the job. Cast iron scales and thins, and clay bells crack under the weight of soil and traffic.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacement in Lynchburg

    A bursting head is a steel cone, slightly wider than the old pipe, pulled through the line by a hydraulic unit or a winch cable. It cracks the old pipe outward into the soil while a string of new HDPE pipe follows right behind it. Fused joints, no gaps. This is the fix for collapsed pipe.

    Trenchless Drain Pipe Lining in Lynchburg, VA

    Smaller drain lines inside the house can be lined too, including 2-inch kitchen drains and the 3-inch cast iron stacks found in many older homes. After curing, a small robotic cutter reopens each branch connection from inside the pipe. That saves opening a finished wall.

    Perma-Liner and NuFlow Trenchless Systems in Lynchburg

    Perma-Liner and NuFlow both make lining systems we use, each with its own resin chemistry and installation equipment. Both are built to ASTM standards for cured-in-place pipe. So the line gets jetted or descaled to bare wall before any resin goes in.
