    Inlet and Outlet Baffle Replacement in Lynchburg, VA

    Baffles hold back the scum layer on top and the sludge on the bottom so only clear effluent leaves the tank. We replace them with PVC sanitary tees. Usually a short job.

    Septic Pipe Repair in Lynchburg, VA

    Effluent Pump Replacement in Lynchburg, VA

    Effluent pumps in a dosing chamber last roughly 8 to 15 years, and they usually quit without much warning at all. Humming with no flow points to a jammed impeller or a failed capacitor. We pull it and test the discharge line before setting a new one. Floats get replaced at the same time.
