    Undermount Kitchen Sink Installation in Lynchburg

    An undermount depends on its clips and adhesive to stay attached to granite or quartz. We mount the bowl, seal the rim with silicone and brace it while the adhesive sets, which usually takes overnight before the sink can hold a full load of water. Then come the strainers and trap.

    Double Bowl Sink and Continuous Waste in Lynchburg

    Both bowls of a double sink share one trap, using either a center outlet continuous waste or an end outlet tee. The layout depends on your disposal and dishwasher, because the disposal side changes where the tee sits and how much slope each bowl gets. We use heavy-wall fittings.

    Top-mount Sink Installation in Lynchburg

    Drop-in sinks are common in Virginia homes with laminate or tile counters. The rim carries the weight. After checking the cutout, we lay silicone under the lip and draw the clips down evenly so the whole rim sits flat. Then the drain and faucet get connected and tested.

    Stainless Steel Sink Installation in Lynchburg

    Stainless sinks come in gauges from 16 to 22, and the lower the number, the thicker the steel. Thin 22 gauge bowls flex and drum when the disposal runs, so we add sound pads or suggest an 18 gauge or heavier sink if you have not bought one yet. Mounting style changes the plumbing. We connect the strainers, faucet and disposal either way.

    Farmhouse Sink Plumbing in Lynchburg, VA

    Fireclay and cast iron farmhouse sinks can weigh well over 100 pounds empty, before a single drop of water goes in. We build a support frame inside the cabinet and set the sink level before the counter is installed. Deep bowls need a lower trap. We plan the drain for that depth from the start.

    Same-Day Sink Replacement in Lynchburg, VA

    A sink with a crack through the bowl or a strainer that has rusted out cannot be patched for long. We stock the most common double-bowl stainless sizes, which makes same-day replacement common, and we replace the strainers and trap at the same time. We swap the old stops as well.
