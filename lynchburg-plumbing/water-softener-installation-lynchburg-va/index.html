    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Lynchburg

    Ion exchange is the only method that truly removes hardness from water. Every salt-based install includes a sized resin tank, a brine tank, a bypass and a drain to an air gap. Programming is the last step. We set regeneration overnight.

    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Lynchburg

    Reverse osmosis pushes water through a fine membrane that removes most dissolved solids, including the sodium a softener adds. Our RO installs go under the sink with their own faucet, and we can tie in the fridge or ice maker too. Pairs well with a softener. We can handle filter changes.

    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Lynchburg

    On a whole-house system, the softener goes on the main line after the shutoff and before the water heater. We add a bypass valve so you can isolate the softener for service, and we install a pressure regulator if your street pressure runs above 80 psi. Every tap gets soft water. Outdoor taps can be left off.

    Residential Water Softener Installation in Lynchburg

    Hard water costs homeowners money in shorter appliance life and extra cleaning. We test the water, ask about daily use and choose a capacity that lets the system regenerate roughly once a week. That keeps salt use reasonable. One visit covers the full install.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Lynchburg, VA

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. FBN Plumbing sets them up with the right resin and brine tanks. A metered control valve regenerates only when needed, which keeps salt and water use down. Most effective option.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Installation in Lynchburg

    Most Lynchburg, VA softener installs land between $1,500 and $4,000. The price usually includes the equipment and labor, with extra plumbing shown as its own line. Bigger homes cost more. We give written pricing up front.

    Our workmanship carries a warranty, and the equipment has its own manufacturer coverage. Tanks and control valves usually carry 5 to 10 years, and we give you every warranty document for your system after the install. Keep the receipt.

    A softener targets calcium and magnesium to stop scale. Filters handle chlorine, sediment and taste, and many homes pair the two.

    Softeners remove hardness minerals using salt. Conditioners change how minerals behave to reduce scale, but softeners work better on very hard water.

    Some areas require a plumbing permit for this kind of work. FBN Plumbing advises on permits in Lynchburg and can help with the application.

    Manufacturer coverage on the unit is often 5 to 10 years on major parts. We cover our labor, usually for one year, and install to spec to keep the unit warranty valid.

    Sized and installed correctly, a softener should not noticeably drop your pressure. A pressure drop after install usually means the valve is too small or a filter is clogged.

    Adding salt every 4 to 8 weeks is most of the work. Once a year a plumber should check for salt bridges, worn seals and injector clogs. Do not let the salt run out.
