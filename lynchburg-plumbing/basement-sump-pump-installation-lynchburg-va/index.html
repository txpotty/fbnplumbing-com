    The Sump Pump Work We Do in Lynchburg, VA

    Residential Basement Sump Pump Installation in Lynchburg

    A typical house pit holds an 18 by 22 inch basin with a 1/3 horsepower pump, and that covers most homes. Our plumbers size the unit to the water coming in and the height it has to climb, not to the sale sticker. In Lynchburg, we set the basin at the low corner, bed it in washed gravel and run 1-1/2 inch PVC out through the rim joist. Discharge gets carried far enough out that it cannot soak back toward the wall.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Lynchburg

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the water. The motor stays dry and you can reach it without pulling the pump, which is why they suit narrow pits under 14 inches across, and why they often outlast cheap submersibles in Lynchburg homes. They are louder, though.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Lynchburg, VA

    Battery backups earn their keep during outages. Hurricanes, tropical storms and strong coastal storms bring heavy rain and tidal flooding to eastern Virginia, especially in low-lying areas. A second pump goes into the same pit on its own float switch, wired to a sealed AGM battery on a smart charger that tests itself and beeps when the battery gets weak. Your primary pump still handles the everyday load.

    Commercial and Multi-Unit Sump Installations in Lynchburg

    Duplex systems with two pumps and an alternating panel are standard for parking garages and multi-unit buildings with below-grade levels in Lynchburg. The panel swaps the lead pump every cycle, so wear stays even. Staff get a high water alarm. We coordinate the wiring with your electrician.

    Primary Sump Pump Replacement in Lynchburg

    Pumps wear out, usually somewhere between seven and ten years. Out comes the old pump, and we look over the pit, the check valve and the float clearance, then install a new pump on fresh PVC so you are not hanging a new motor on an old cracked fitting in Lynchburg. Most swaps take two hours.
