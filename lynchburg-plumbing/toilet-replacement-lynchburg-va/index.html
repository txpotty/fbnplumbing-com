    Toilet Brands We Replace in Lynchburg

    Brand matters less than model, because every major maker sells a great toilet and a mediocre one at nearly the same price. We look at the flush valve size and MaP score. Parts availability counts too. Proprietary flappers and fill valves mean that ten years from now, a simple fix becomes a special order.

    Commercial Toilet Replacement in Lynchburg, VA

    Office and retail restrooms wear out toilets faster than any house, and a replacement there usually means a floor-mount or wall-hung bowl with a flushometer instead of a tank. We schedule around your hours. We match the bowl to the gallons per flush on the valve, since a mismatch leaves waste behind or wastes water.

    Replacing Pressure-Assisted Toilets in Lynchburg, VA

    Pressure-assisted toilets use a sealed vessel in the tank that stores air under line pressure and pushes water through the bowl hard, which helps in houses with long, low-slope drain runs. They are loud. Replacement means matching the vessel brand for parts later, and we check that house pressure is in the range the unit needs.

    Urgent Toilet Replacement in Lynchburg, VA

    Late-night failures happen, usually a tank that splits along a seam or a bowl that cracks at the base and keeps seeping no matter how many towels go down. Turn off the angle stop and the leak stops. We can usually replace it the next morning, and we will tell you if the floor needs to dry before we set the new one.

    Cracked Toilet Replacement in Lynchburg

    Porcelain cracks below the waterline are not safely repairable, and epoxy fixes on a tank under constant water pressure rarely last more than a few months. You should replace it. We pull the cracked toilet and check the floor for water damage before setting the new one, since a slow crack leak often soaks the subfloor first.

    Wall-Hung Toilet Replacement in Lynchburg

    Wall-hung toilets mount to a steel carrier inside the wall, so replacing one means checking the carrier bolts, the gasket on the outlet and the condition of the wall finish around it. Some carriers need new bolts. We match the new bowl to the existing carrier whenever possible, which avoids opening the wall.

    Cracked Bowl or Tank Replacement in Lynchburg

    Sometimes only the tank is cracked, and if the model is still in production, a matching tank costs less than a whole new toilet. First we find the model number inside the tank. A cracked bowl always means a full replacement, since the bowl carries the seal to the floor and cannot be patched.
