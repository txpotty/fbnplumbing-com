    Emergency Sewer Repair in Lynchburg

    Raw sewage on a basement floor is not a next-week problem. We answer at night and on weekends. The first goal on an emergency call is to get the line flowing, usually with a cable or jetter, and then camera it to find out whether you have a clog or a structural failure that will back up again tomorrow. The real repair may be scheduled for the next morning. In the meantime, we flag which drains to avoid.

    Sewer Pipe Relining in Lynchburg, VA

    Relining builds a new pipe inside the old one. The old pipe gets jetted clean before anything else. A flexible liner saturated with epoxy is pulled or inverted into place, inflated against the old wall and left to cure, so every crack and leaking joint is covered by one jointless pipe. The finished pipe loses a little diameter. On a four-inch residential line, that rarely matters.

    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Lynchburg, VA

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water. Paper and solids settle there. Basements are common in northern and western parts of Virginia, while crawlspaces and slabs are more typical in the east and south. We measure the depth of standing water on camera, dig out that section, re-bed the pipe on compacted gravel and reset the slope to the quarter inch per foot most codes call for on residential laterals. Not every sag needs digging, and we will tell you which yours is.

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Lynchburg

    Every repair starts with a look inside. The camera goes in at the nearest cleanout. You get a copy of the video and a plain written summary of each defect with its distance from the house, which helps if you are buying a home, filing a claim or comparing our price with another company's. Sometimes the answer is that the line is fine.

    After-Hours Sewer Repair in Lynchburg

    Main lines like to fail on holidays. Our phones stay on. An after-hours call gets a tech with a cable machine and a camera, which is enough to restore flow in most cases and to tell you if you have a clog or a broken pipe. If it is structural, we schedule the dig for the earliest safe window. We avoid digging blind at night near gas and electric lines.

    Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Lynchburg

    Orangeburg is wood fiber soaked in coal tar. Over decades it goes oval and blisters. Cast iron from the same era rusts from the inside, builds scale and eventually splits along the bottom where the sewage runs, so by the time a camera shows widespread damage, spot repairs are a poor investment. We replace these lines with PVC. Trenchless bursting is often an option.

    Pipe Burst & Collapse Repair in Lynchburg, VA

    A collapsed sewer pipe stops all flow at the point of failure. Every drain backs up. Collapses usually come from corroded cast iron, crushed clay under a driveway or tar-paper pipe that has finally flattened, and we locate the break and its depth before we start digging. The broken length comes out and new pipe goes in. Lining cannot fix a full collapse.

    Commercial Sewer Repair in Lynchburg

We repair larger diameter lines, add two-way cleanouts so future maintenance is easy, and give managers recorded footage for owners, insurers and their own files. We can work outside business hours.
