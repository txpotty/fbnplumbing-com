    Grinder Pump Installation in Lynchburg

    Grinder pumps chop waste into a fine slurry first, which lets them push through small pipe over long distances. They are the right choice when the sewer is far away, uphill or under pressure, like on a pressure sewer system where the pump feeds a shared force main in the street. Grinders run pricier. For a basement bath near the drain, a standard ejector usually works.

    Ejector Pump Alarms and Backup Options in Lynchburg

    A high water alarm belongs on every ejector system, since a pump failure can lead to a sewage backup quickly. Audible alarms on their own power or Wi-Fi alarms that text you give warning while the pit is still filling, not after waste reaches the floor. Hurricanes, tropical storms and strong coastal storms bring heavy rain and tidal flooding to eastern Virginia, especially in low-lying areas. Battery backup is an option.

    Duplex and Commercial Ejector Systems in Lynchburg, VA

    Commercial buildings, multi-unit properties and busy homes often need duplex systems with an alternating control panel. Alternating the lead pump spreads wear, and when one fails or flow spikes, the second starts on its own and the panel sounds an alarm. Redundancy matters here.

    Zoeller Ejector Pump Installation in Lynchburg, VA

    Zoeller has made ejector pumps for a very long time, and its cast iron models are the ones we see still running in older basements. We install Zoeller for both new basins and drop-in replacements, and its standard models often fit existing pits and discharge piping without changes. Floats and switches are stocked locally.

    Ejector Pump Installation Cost Comparison in Lynchburg, VA

    Replacing a pump in an existing basin is the least expensive option, often done in a few hours.
