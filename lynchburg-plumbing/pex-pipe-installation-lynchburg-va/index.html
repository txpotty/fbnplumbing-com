    PEX Repiping and Installation in Lynchburg, VA

    PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Lynchburg, VA

    There are three grades of PEX, named for how the plastic is cross-linked at the factory. PEX-A bends the easiest and uses expansion rings, PEX-B is stiffer and uses crimp rings or cinch clamps, and PEX-C handles more like B. Every one is listed for potable water. Pipe, fittings and tools come from the same maker on our jobs, which keeps the warranty valid.

    Uponor PEX System Installation in Lynchburg

    Uponor's ProPEX system is PEX-A joined with an expansion tool plus a ring slipped over the pipe end. We stretch the pipe and ring, seat the fitting, and within seconds the plastic shrinks back around the barb into a tight seal. No crimp tool to calibrate. Because the fittings are wide inside, each joint costs very little flow.

    Manifold and Home-Run PEX Layouts in Lynchburg, VA

    Think of a manifold like an electrical panel, but for water lines. A central header feeds each fixture on its own line with its own valve, which lets you shut off one toilet and leave the kitchen running. It uses more pipe. The trade-off is almost no fittings hidden in the walls and steadier pressure when two showers run at once.

    Emergency PEX Pipe Repair & Installation in Lynchburg, VA

    PEX rarely fails on its own. When it does, the cause is usually a nail or screw driven through it, a rodent chewing on an attic run, or a fitting that was never crimped all the way. We carry the common sizes. Most emergency PEX repairs in Lynchburg, VA come down to a cut and a splice, usually under an hour after the wall is open.

    Low pressure at every faucet, across the whole house, usually points at the pipe and not the fixtures.     Full Home PEX Repiping in Lynchburg

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside out. Give it four or five decades and the inside is so packed with rust that a half-inch pipe barely flows, and the first draw in the morning runs brown. A full PEX repipe replaces all of it. You get real pressure back, and the discolored water goes away with the old pipe.
