    Backflow Preventer Testing in Arlington

    Backflow devices protect the drinking water. Yearly tests are often required, and our certified testers check each valve and file results. We repair failures. We also check that devices are protected from heat and freezing where needed. Late tests can mean fines.

    Slab Leak and Hidden Leak Checks in Arlington

    Older neighborhoods in Arlington often have clay or cast iron sewer laterals that deteriorate with age and root intrusion. Buried and under-slab water lines can wear from soil movement and hard water until they start leaking where nobody can see it, sometimes for months. Warm floors are a clue. A spinning meter is another. Acoustic and thermal gear pinpoint it.

    Hot Water Tank Service in Arlington, VA

    Gas and electric tanks need different checks. Gas tanks get a burner, flue and leak check, and electric tanks get element and thermostat tests. Both get flushed. Scale on the tank floor wastes energy and shortens tank life.

    Repipe Assessments in Arlington, VA

    Polybutylene and old galvanized pipe leak more. During maintenance we identify your supply pipe material, look for pinhole leaks, rust stains and pressure drops, and tell you honestly whether repiping is something to plan for now or years from now. We quote PEX and copper. Repiping can be done in stages.

    Getting Ready for the Hard Months in Arlington, VA

    Weak spots show up in bad weather. Most Virginia winters bring several hard freezes, and cold snaps can burst pipes in crawlspaces, garages and exterior walls. We find exposed pipes, unprotected outdoor faucets and lines that need draining before the tough season. Insulation is cheap. Burst pipes are not.

    Water Heater Maintenance in Arlington

    Sediment is the tank's slow killer. Water hardness in Virginia varies by region, with harder water in the western limestone valleys and softer water closer to the coast. We drain and flush the tank, pull and check the anode rod, test the temperature and pressure relief valve, and look over the gas or electric connections for corrosion and wear. Plan on an hour. Less sediment means quicker heating and a longer tank life.

    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Arlington

    Tankless units need descaling. Scale builds up inside the heat exchanger, especially with hard water, and the unit starts throwing error codes, running lukewarm or cutting out mid-shower long before it actually wears out. We pump descaler through the service valves. The inlet filter, venting and gas line get checked too.

    Tank and Tankless Heater Tune-Ups in Arlington

    Mineral-heavy water shortens water heater life. We drain sediment, check the anode rod, inspect the burner or elements and confirm the relief valve opens and reseats, whether the unit is a standard tank or a tankless model on the wall. Cold showers are avoidable. We spot the signs early.
