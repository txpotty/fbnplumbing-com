    Under Slab Copper Leak Repair in Arlington, VA

    Thin-Wall Copper Pinhole Repair in Arlington

    Older copper, especially Type M, pinholes more than heavier pipe. Thin walls wear through first. We locate the leak precisely and either solder in a new section or use a press fitting near framing, with new pipe that is heavier than the original. Nearby pipe gets a look.

    Hot Water Copper Line Repair in Arlington, VA

    Hot water lines corrode faster than cold lines. Heat speeds it up. Recirculation pumps that run around the clock push water through the copper at high velocity and wear it from the inside, which is why we often add a timer to the pump after fixing the leak. Less run time, less wear.

    Copper Pinhole Leak Repair in Arlington

    Pinholes form when the inner wall of copper pipe erodes or pits. Hot lines get it worst. They often appear in thin Type M pipe and on hot water lines where velocity and temperature are higher, which we fix by replacing that length with heavier pipe. More pinholes often follow.

    Burst Copper Pipe Repair in Arlington, VA

    Copper in an exterior wall or attic can split when the water inside freezes. Most Virginia winters bring several hard freezes, and cold snaps can burst pipes in crawlspaces, garages and exterior walls. Close the main valve. The split often does not leak until the ice thaws, so a pipe that froze overnight may start spraying the next afternoon once the house warms up. We cut out the split and solder in new pipe. Then we add insulation.
