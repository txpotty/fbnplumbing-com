    Why Choose FBN Plumbing for PEX Pipe Installation in Arlington, VA?

    Manufacturer rules for PEX are specific: supports about every 32 inches on horizontal runs, a minimum bend radius, and no long sun exposure. We follow them on every run. Ignoring them causes kinks, sags and strained fittings, and it can cost you the manufacturer warranty that should have backed the pipe.

    • Bends Around Framing, One continuous run replaces a handful of fittings copper would need.
    • Freeze Tolerance, PEX stretches as ice forms and usually holds where copper would crack.
    • Home-Run Option, Each fixture can get its own line off a manifold and its own shutoff.

    Citywide Coverage

    From 1970s ranch houses still on galvanized to 1990s builds with gray poly, we repipe homes all over Arlington. A ranch with a big attic routes easily, but a two-story needs a careful plan to reach upstairs baths without cutting finished ceilings. We plan it before cutting.

    Each lead plumber has worked through many whole-house repipes and knows both expansion and crimp systems.
