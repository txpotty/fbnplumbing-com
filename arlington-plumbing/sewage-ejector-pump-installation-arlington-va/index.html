    Grinder Pump Installation in Arlington

    Grinder pumps chop waste into slurry before pumping it, which lets them push through small pipe over long distances. A grinder makes sense when the sewer is distant, uphill or pressurized, such as a pressure sewer where each home pumps into a shared main. They cost more.     New Construction Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Arlington, VA

    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Arlington

    Sump pumps and ejector pumps look alike in a catalog but do very different jobs. A sump pump moves groundwater from a perimeter drain to the yard, while an ejector moves sewage from toilets and sinks to the sewer, and the ejector basin has to be sealed and vented because of the gas. They are not interchangeable.

    Upflush and Macerating Toilet Systems in Arlington

    Macerating systems like Saniflo let you add a toilet and sink without breaking the concrete floor. They need a vent too.

    Laundry and Wet Bar Ejector Pumps in Arlington, VA

Filters save impellers.
