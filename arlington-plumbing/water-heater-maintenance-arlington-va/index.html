    Relief Valve and Safety Testing in Arlington, VA

    The relief valve is the tank's last line of defense.     Tank Water Heater Maintenance in Arlington

    Flushing is the heart of tank maintenance. Water hardness in Virginia varies by region, with more mineral-heavy water in the western limestone valleys and softer water closer to the coast. We run a hose from the drain valve, open it up and let the tank drain until the water runs clear, then refill and check the anode rod, the relief valve and every connection for drips or corrosion. Takes about an hour. Less sediment means more years.

    Flushing a Tank Water Heater in Arlington

    Popping and rumbling mean sediment. Scale on the tank bottom traps heat and causes water to pop and rumble as it heats. The tank drains until the water is clean. Heavy buildup may take several cycles.

    Tune-Up Versus Replacement in Arlington, VA

    A tune-up costs a fraction of a new heater. Installed tanks often run well over a thousand dollars, and regular flushing and anode replacement help a tank reach the high end of its rated lifespan, while tankless units can last many years with proper descaling.     Gas Water Heater Safety Checks in Arlington, VA

    Gas units need a clean burner and a good draft. We check for gas leaks at the valve and connector, watch the flame, clean the burner and confirm the flue pulls exhaust up and out instead of spilling it into the room. CO is the risk. We also test the thermocouple.

    Tankless Water Heater Service in Arlington, VA

    Tankless units need descaling. Mineral scale coats the inside of the heat exchanger, restricting flow and making the unit work harder, and eventually it starts throwing error codes or cutting out in the middle of a shower. We flush it with descaler. Igniter, fan and exhaust venting get checked too.
