    Appliance Connector and Shutoff Repair in Arlington

    Old flexible connectors, especially uncoated brass ones, crack and leak with age. New coated stainless connectors go on, sized to the appliance, with a reachable shutoff valve if the old one is stuck or missing. This is a quick fix. Ranges, dryers, water heaters and furnaces all need proper connections, and most also need a sediment trap ahead of the appliance gas valve.

    Permits and Code Compliance for Gas Work in Arlington, VA

    Most gas piping work beyond a simple connector swap needs a permit in Arlington, VA. The inspector checks pipe sizing, material, support spacing, shutoff locations, sediment traps and the pressure test result before signing off. The permit is on us. Doing it this way keeps your insurance clean and gives you a record of the repair if you sell the house later.

    Buried Yard Line Repair and Replacement in Arlington, VA

    Many older homes have steel gas lines running from the meter to the house or out to a pool heater. The wrap fails eventually. Once the coating cracks, the pipe starts to corrode, and wet soil from sprinklers or a low spot in the yard speeds that up a great deal. Utilities get marked first. We lay polyethylene with tracer wire or coated steel where exposed, then backfill and clean up after the test passes.

    Interior Black Iron Pipe Repair in Arlington

    Threaded black iron pipe can leak at joints after years of vibration or settling. We tighten, reseal or replace fittings and sections with new pipe and gas-rated thread sealant. Rusty runs get swapped out. Heavily rusted pipe in garages, crawlspaces or outside walls is replaced before it fails, and we pressure test the full system afterward.

    After-Hours Gas Line Repair in Arlington

    Gas problems do not wait for business hours, and losing heat or hot water overnight is a real problem. We answer late calls. Once everyone is out and the gas company has secured things, we send a plumber to find the leak, fix it and test the system. The utility unlocks the meter once we pass.

    Cost of Gas Line Repair in Arlington, VA

    Gas line repair prices in Arlington, VA depend on where the leak is and how much pipe has to change. Tightening or replacing a single fitting or connector is usually a few hundred dollars. Trenching and replacing a buried line often lands between $1,000 and $5,000, with length, soil and any concrete in the path setting the final number. We put the price in writing first.

    Gas Leak Repair at Fittings and Valves in Arlington

    Most residential gas leaks are at a threaded joint, a valve stem or a flexible connector, not in the middle of a pipe. That keeps repairs small. Leaking joints get taken apart, cleaned, resealed with gas-rated compound and tightened, and damaged fittings or valves get replaced. Every repair gets a soap test and a system pressure test before we leave.

    Propane Gas Line Service in Arlington, VA

    Propane systems run at different pressures than natural gas and use a two-stage regulator setup between the tank and the house. Regulators fail too. We repair buried and above-ground propane lines, replace leaking regulators and fittings and check that each appliance has been converted with the correct orifices for propane. A natural gas burner running on propane will burn rich and soot up. We test the system the same way we test natural gas piping.
