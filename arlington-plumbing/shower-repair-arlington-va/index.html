    Shower Pan and Wall Leak Repair in Arlington, VA

    Stains on the ceiling below are often the first clue. A meter on the walls and a flood test on the pan show whether the leak is the valve, the pan or the drain. Then we fix the source. With slab floors, the water can travel under the flooring of nearby rooms.

    Same-Day Shower Leak Repair in Arlington, VA

    Shut off the main first if water is getting where it should not. For a sudden wall leak or a valve that will not close, the main valve near the meter is the first move. Call us next. We stop the leak, make the repair or a safe temporary fix and check for anything that needs drying.

    Weak Shower Spray Repair in Arlington, VA

    Most weak showers come down to the head. Scale packs the nozzles and the restrictor screen behind them, and the spray drops off a little at a time until one day it feels like a garden mister. Cleaning often works. If the cartridge is clogged too, we replace it and test the flow again.

    Showerhead and Arm Leak Repair in Arlington

    Shower arms leak at the threads more than people realize. Water that seeps from the arm connection runs back along the pipe into the wall, and you only find out when the paint on the other side starts to bubble. Fresh thread tape fixes it. We can add a handheld or slide bar while we are there.

    Dripping Showerhead Repair in Arlington

    Drips from the head itself almost always come from the valve, not the head. If the head drips with the valve closed, water is slipping past the cartridge, and a new head will not change that. We fix the valve. Drips from the arm threads just need fresh tape and a snug turn.

    Low Shower Pressure Repair in Arlington, VA

    Low pressure at the shower with good pressure at the sink narrows it down fast. With good pressure elsewhere, the problem is in the head, the restrictor or the cartridge, and that is typically a short repair. We start with the head. Low pressure everywhere sends us to the pressure regulator and main valve instead.

    Mixing Valve Repair in Arlington

    Mixing valves blend hot and cold inside the wall. A sticking cartridge or spool shows up as temperature that drifts or a handle that has to go much farther than it used to. A new cartridge usually fixes it. Valves with no parts left get a replacement plan and an honest look at the tile.

    Moen and Delta Shower Repair in Arlington, VA

    Moen 1222 and Delta RP46074 cartridges sit inside a huge share of the showers we open. The Moen 1222 runs the Posi-Temp line, and the Delta RP46074 runs the Monitor 13 and 14 series, and both wear out on a schedule you can almost set a calendar by. We stock both. Original owners can often get the cartridge free under warranty.
