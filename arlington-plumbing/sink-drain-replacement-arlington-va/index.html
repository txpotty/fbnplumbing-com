    Corroded Drain Pipe Replacement in Arlington, VA

    Galvanized drain arms rust from the inside. An old steel stub can crumble at the threads, so we cut it back and join new PVC with a shielded coupling. We handle cast iron the same way. Sometimes that means opening the wall.

    P-Trap Replacement in Arlington

    Thin chrome traps are the ones we replace most. They rust from the inside, the plating flakes off, and a slip nut that will not loosen often twists the whole trap apart, so we cut it out and install heavy-wall PVC or polished brass that matches the room. Then it gets a full flow test. Total time is often under an hour.

    Code Updates During Drain Replacement in Arlington

    Old drains sometimes break current rules. S-traps and drum traps were common long ago, and replacement brings them up to a vented P-trap. An AAV may be allowed where a vent is missing. Local rules decide.

    Bathroom Sink Drain Replacement in Arlington, VA

    Bathroom drains wear out at the pop-up. Pivot nuts leak and drain bodies corrode at the overflow holes, so a sink that will not hold water needs a new pop-up. Half-fixes do not hold here. The new drain matches your faucet finish.

    Leaking P-Trap Replacement in Arlington

    Traps leak at the joints first. The beveled washers inside the slip nuts flatten and harden over the years, and a trap that has been taken apart several times to clear clogs often has stripped plastic threads or a cracked nut. A new trap kit costs little. Labor is most of the bill.

    Utility and Basement Sink Drain Replacement in Arlington

    Basement sinks often drain to a pump. Sinks below the sewer line drain through a pump, so we check its valve and discharge line during the job. Pump trouble is easy to miss. A gurgling pit is a clue.

    Vessel and Grid Drain Replacement in Arlington

    Vessel sinks use a different drain. Bowls without an overflow need an unslotted grid or umbrella drain, or water leaks out the slots. We see the wrong drain often. Swapping it takes under an hour.

    Hard-Water Drain Damage Repair in Arlington, VA

    Even moderate hardness in Arlington can leave scale in water heaters over time, so regular flushing helps them run longer and more efficiently. Scale grows inside pop-up bodies and on stopper rods until the linkage seizes. Once it locks, someone forces the lift rod, the pivot snaps, and the stopper ends up stuck down for good, and by then the scale has also narrowed the tailpiece enough to slow the drain. We replace the whole scaled assembly. Smooth PVC sheds buildup better.
