    Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in Arlington, VA

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor up on a column above the pit, out of the water, which is why a good one can run fifteen years in a dry basement. You will hear one kick on from upstairs. The new column gets set plumb and braced, and we set the float rod stops so the pump turns on and off at the right water levels. They fit pits as narrow as 10 inches.

    Pedestal Pumps for Shallow Pits in Arlington, VA

    Older basements sometimes have a narrow, shallow pit poured right into the floor, and a pedestal pump is often the only thing that fits without breaking concrete. We swap the pump, replace the float rod and stops, and run new 1.5-inch PVC to the check valve. They usually cost less than a submersible.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Replacement in Arlington

    Basement bathrooms below the sewer line drain into a sealed, vented basin, and the ejector pump in that basin has to pass 2-inch solids. It is a different pump entirely. We replace the ejector and its discharge check valve, reseal the lid gasket, and confirm the vent ties in. Leaky lids let sewer gas into the basement.

    High-Capacity Sump Pump Upgrades in Arlington

    If your pump runs every 30 seconds during a storm and still loses ground, it is undersized for the water coming in. First we time the pump cycles and measure the pit refill rate.     Yard Drainage Pit Pumps in Arlington, VA

    Yard drainage pits and window well pumps can sit dry from fall to spring and then seize the first time a big storm fills them. We clean the debris out and install a pump built to handle grit. We route the outlet away from the house. A bucket test every spring catches problems early.
