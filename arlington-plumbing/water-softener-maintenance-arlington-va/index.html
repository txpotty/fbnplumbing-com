    Control Valve and Injector Cleaning in Arlington, VA

    Brine draw depends on a venturi injector, a tiny nozzle and throat that pulls brine out of the salt tank as water rushes past. It does not take much debris to block it.     Hardness Testing at the Tap in Arlington

    We test with a titration kit, counting drops until the sample changes color, because test strips are too rough for setting a valve. Raw water gets tested from an unsoftened hose bib or with the valve on bypass. Softened water comes from the kitchen tap.

    Brine Tank Cleanout and Inspection in Arlington, VA

    Brine tanks collect a gray sludge of salt fines and insoluble grit, and it builds up faster with cheap rock salt. We empty, scrub and rinse the tank. Then we look for cracks around the brine well and overflow fitting and refill with fresh salt.

    Softener Brands We Work On in Arlington

    Most softeners sold in the last 20 years run on a handful of valve platforms, mainly Fleck, Clack, Autotrol and a few proprietary heads. The valve matters more than the brand. Kinetico and Culligan use their own designs, so we check part availability before quoting.

    Full-System Softener Inspections in Arlington

    Repair or Replace a Softener in Arlington, VA

    Resin Rebedding in Arlington

If the bed is spent, we drain the tank, pull the old resin and refill it with fresh high-capacity resin, which runs far less than a new softener.
