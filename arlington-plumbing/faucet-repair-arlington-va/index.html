    Common Faucet Repairs in Arlington, VA

    Cracked Faucet Body Repair in Arlington

    Cracked faucet bodies, usually from freezing or an old brass casting that finally gave out, cannot be repaired and need a replacement faucet. Turn off the stops under the sink. A new faucet can usually go in on the same visit, and we will check the supply lines and stops while the water is off.

    Emergency Faucet Repair in Arlington

    A faucet that will not shut off, or one with a cracked body spraying under the sink, needs the water off before anything else happens. Turn the two small valves under the sink clockwise. If they will not turn, use the main shutoff for the house. We come fast for these.

    Delta and Moen Faucet Repair in Arlington, VA

    Delta and Moen are the faucets we repair most, and both back their cartridges with lifetime warranties for the original owner on many models. Ask us to check. Kohler, Pfister and American Standard each use proprietary cartridges, and we stock the common ones for each brand.

    Compression Faucet Repair in Arlington

    Two-handle faucets with separate hot and cold handles are often compression faucets, which seal by pressing a rubber washer down onto a brass seat inside the valve. Washers harden and seats get pitted. We replace the washer and dress or replace the seat with a seat wrench, and the packing gets redone if the stem leaks.

    Low Water Flow Faucet Repair in Arlington

    Sputtering flow, where the water spits and surges, often means air or debris in the line after a water main repair, or a partly clogged cartridge screen. Flushing usually clears it. On pull-down faucets, the check valves in the hose can stick too, and we clean or replace those.

    Leaky Faucet Repair in Arlington

    Most drips come from the part that seals the water inside the faucet, which is a rubber washer on older two-handle faucets and a cartridge or ceramic disc on newer ones. First we identify which type of valve you have. It matters. With the right part on the truck, most drips are fixed in one visit.

    Bathroom Pop-Up Drain Repair in Arlington

    Pop-up drains on bathroom sinks fail at the pivot ball, the clevis strap, the lift rod or the stopper itself, and a slow drain often turns out to be hair wrapped around the stopper. Try lifting out the stopper first. We replace the pop-up assembly in a matching finish when the old one is corroded or the pivot leaks.

    Ball Faucet Repair in Arlington, VA

    Ball faucets have a single lever over a domed cap, and the drip usually comes from the two small rubber seats and springs that press against the ball. A seats and springs kit fixes most of them. Loose handles happen too. If the handle leaks, snugging the adjusting ring under the cap often fixes it.
