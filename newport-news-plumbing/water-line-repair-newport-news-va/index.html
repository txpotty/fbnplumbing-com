    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Newport News

    A trench through a paver driveway or a mature garden can cost more to restore than the pipe itself. That is where trenchless pays off. We dig a pit at each end and pull HDPE or PEX through with a boring tool or pipe puller, either along the old route or through fresh soil next to it. Most replacements finish in a day. You also get the manufacturer warranty on the new pipe.

    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Repair in Newport News

    High static pressure shortens the life of the service line and every fixture behind it. The usual code limit is 80 psi. We gauge the pressure at a hose bib, and if it reads high we replace the pressure reducing valve with a Watts or Zurn Wilkins unit. Old gate valves that will not close get replaced with full-port ball valves. So the shutoff works when you need it.

    Main Water Line Repair in Newport News

    Your main line is the pipe from the meter to the house, usually 3/4 inch or 1 inch, buried anywhere from a foot to several feet down depending on frost depth. A leak here shows up as a soggy strip of lawn and a meter dial that never rests. We start by closing the house shutoff and watching the dial. A moving dial with the house valve closed means an underground leak. Then a ground microphone narrows the spot, so the crew opens one small pit rather than trenching the whole run.

    Water Pressure Restoration in Newport News

    Low pressure at every fixture points to the service line or the regulator, not the faucets. We put a gauge on a hose bib first. Then we check the pressure reducing valve, the house shutoff and the meter-side valve for a partly closed gate, and look at the pipe itself. Galvanized pipe can lose half its opening to rust scale. The fix might be a new valve or a new line.

    PEX Water Line Repair in Newport News, VA

    PEX itself rarely fails underground, but the joints and the first few feet near the house take a beating. Rodents chew it. A shovel or a fence post auger can nick it, and sunlight exposure before burial weakens the plastic over time. We cut out the damaged section and install new pipe with crimp, clamp or expansion fittings, matched to the PEX brand already in the ground.

    Pipe Bursting and Pulling in Newport News

    Pipe bursting means splitting the old line in place while the new one follows behind it. It works best on straight runs. A cone-shaped head breaks the old pipe outward, while new HDPE comes through right behind the head. You end up with a fresh line and two small pits, and the lawn, sidewalk and driveway in between stay where they are, which is the whole reason people pick this method over a trench.

Bigger meters need bigger pipe. We size the line from the fixture count.     Galvanized service lines were common in houses built before the 1970s, and a lot of them are still in the ground.     Frequently Asked Questions About Water Line Repair in Newport News

    A minor leak repair often starts around $500. Full replacement costs more. A meter-to-house replacement or a trenchless pull can top $5,000 when the run is long, deep or under hardscape, and the price moves with pipe type, depth and whether we trench or bore. We quote it in writing after the inspection.

    Spot repairs usually take a few hours. A full replacement takes about a day. Jobs with a deep line, a lot of hardscape or a trenchless pull sometimes run two or three days when concrete has to be cut and patched or the inspector cannot come right away. We give you a time estimate with the quote.

    In most places the water provider owns the meter and the pipe on the street side of it. The homeowner owns the line to the house. Some providers draw the line at the curb stop instead, so before quoting we check exactly where the leak is. If it turns out to be theirs, we tell you and help you report it.

    We dig a pit at each end of the old line. First, a cable is threaded through. A bursting head on the cable splits the old line outward, while the new pipe is pulled into the path it just opened. It only works when the old pipe is a fairly straight run, which most residential service lines are.

    Fix small leaks early. Little leaks grow. Check your pressure once a year, since a failing regulator quietly pushes the line and every fixture harder than they were built for, and keep big-rooted trees off the pipe route. For older homes in Newport News, replacing an aging galvanized line on your own schedule usually costs less than an emergency.

    Most replacements get Type K copper or PEX. HDPE goes in on trenchless pulls. Copper lasts a very long time and resists punctures, and PEX flexes with the soil, tolerates freezing better and goes in faster. We recommend one based on your soil, line length, budget and what your local code allows in Newport News.

    For PEX we use Uponor and Viega, depending on the job. Buried copper is always Type K. Couplings and transition fittings come from makers like Mueller and Ford, and we stick with fittings rated for direct burial in the soil conditions we find. We do not use cheap knockoff fittings.
