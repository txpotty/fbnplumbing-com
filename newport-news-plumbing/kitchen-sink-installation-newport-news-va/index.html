    Stainless Steel Sink Installation in Newport News

    Stainless sinks come in gauges from 16 to 22, and a lower gauge means thicker steel. We connect the strainers, faucet and disposal either way.

    Double Bowl Sink and Continuous Waste in Newport News

    A double bowl drains both sides through one trap, using either a center outlet continuous waste or an end outlet tee. The layout depends on your disposal and dishwasher, because the disposal side changes where the tee sits and how much slope each bowl gets. We use heavy-wall fittings.

    Undermount Sinks on Stone in Newport News, VA

    Every fabricator handles undermount clips a bit differently. Some epoxy threaded studs into the stone, others use a rail system, and we work with whichever one your countertop shop prefers. The bowl is braced regardless. The trap does not get connected until the adhesive cures.

    Stainless Sinks With Sound Pads in Newport News

    Budget stainless sinks ring like a drum when dishes hit them or the disposal runs. Better sinks come with rubber pads and an undercoating, and if yours does not, we can add a coat of spray undercoating before it goes in. It is a small thing. After the sink is in place the underside is hard to get at, which is why we do it while the bowl is still sitting out.

    Composite Sink Installation in Newport News

    Granite and quartz composite sinks resist scratches on the surface but can chip at the drain hole if a strainer is overtightened. Hand tight comes first. Most composite brands want silicone rather than putty under the strainer because putty can stain them, so we go by the maker's instructions.

    New Construction Sink Rough-In in Newport News, VA

    Rough-in sets the drain height and the stops long before the cabinets arrive. We usually set the kitchen drain stub around 16 to 20 inches off the floor, then adjust for deep bowls or a disposal so the trap does not end up below the wall outlet. Venting happens at this stage. Having the sink and faucet specs early saves moving pipe later.
