    Slab Leak Detection and Repair in Newport News

    Slab leaks hide under concrete until something gives them away. Soils in Virginia range from sandy ground with a high water table near the coast to dense clay and rocky karst farther inland.     Polybutylene, the gray plastic pipe used for years in home construction, fails from the inside without warning.
