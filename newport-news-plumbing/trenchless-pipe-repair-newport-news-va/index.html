    Perma-Liner and Picote Solutions in Newport News

    Perma-Liner supplies the lining systems and Picote makes the cutters. Both matter. Before lining, Picote heads mill out roots and scale, and after curing they reopen the lateral connections so each drain flows. A clean pipe holds a liner best.

    Pipe Bursting Trenchless Repair in Newport News, VA

    Bursting works when the pipe has collapsed or is too broken to line. It also upsizes lines. Our crew sets an entry pit and an exit pit, then pulls a bursting head and new HDPE through on a hydraulic winch, which replaces the full run in a day or two for most homes. FBN Plumbing fuses the HDPE into one length.

    CIPP Lining for Clay and Cast Iron in Newport News

    Older Newport News homes often have clay or cast iron sewer pipe. Both fail with age. Roots get into clay joints and cast iron rusts from inside, and a liner seals over both with jointless pipe that should outlast the original. Only one or two access points are needed.

    Urgent Sewer Failures in Newport News

    Sewage backing into a tub is not a wait-and-see problem. Stop running water. Same day, we clear and scope the line, then schedule the liner or burst quickly and keep it flowing in the meantime.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe Lining in Newport News

    The old pipe gets cleaned first. After that, we insert the liner. A flexible liner saturated with epoxy resin is inflated against the pipe wall and cured with hot water, steam or UV light, and once it hardens, it forms a smooth, jointless pipe inside the old one. Lining works best for cracks, root intrusion and separated joints.

    Trenchless vs Traditional Costs in Newport News, VA

    On paper, the two methods can price out close. Then add restoration. A trench under a driveway or through an established garden means paying to replace concrete, plants and irrigation after the plumbing is done, and trenchless avoids most of those costs. We price both so you can compare.

    Sewer Lateral Lining in Newport News

    Laterals are the most common candidates for trenchless repair. Clay and Orangeburg fail often. Clay, cast iron and Orangeburg pipe crack, sag or fill with roots over time, so we jet the line clean, inspect it by camera and choose lining or bursting based on what we see. The new line restores full flow and resists roots.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Newport News

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. The resin cures in place. We pull or invert the tube into the cleaned line, inflate it against the pipe wall and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light, which leaves a jointless pipe inside the old one. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your Newport News yard.
