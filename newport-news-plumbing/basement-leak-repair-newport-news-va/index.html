    How We Repair Basement Leaks in Newport News, VA

    Waterproofing for Older Virginia Homes in Newport News, VA

    Older basements usually went in with block walls and little more than tar on the outside. Older neighborhoods in Newport News often have clay or cast iron sewer laterals that crack with age and root intrusion. Water collects inside hollow block cores, and weep holes at the base let it drain into a perimeter channel. Clay tile drains fail too.

    Drain and Sewer Pipe Leak Repair in Newport News, VA

    Cast iron drain stacks in older homes rust through from the inside over decades. We cut out cracked cast iron and splice in new pipe with shielded no-hub couplings. Plastic joints can fail too. A separated PVC joint usually means the pipe was not fully seated or not supported.

    Foundation Crack Repair in Newport News

    Hairline cracks in a poured wall are common and often stay dry, but a crack that seeps after rain needs attention. Expansive clays in some areas swell when wet and shrink when dry, which can crack foundations and shift buried sewer lines. We inject leaking cracks from the inside with polyurethane foam, which expands into the crack and stays flexible as the wall moves. Most injections take an hour or two.

    Emergency Basement Flooding Response in Newport News

    A burst pipe or a failed sump during a storm can put inches of water on the floor in minutes. Call us first, then move belongings off the floor. Stay away from wet outlets. If water is near the furnace, the water heater or any outlet, keep out of the room until the power to that area is off.

    Interior Drainage Systems in Newport News

    Where digging outside is not practical, an interior drain system does the same job from inside. We cut the slab along the perimeter, lay perforated pipe in washed stone, and slope it to a new or existing sump pit before patching the floor with fresh concrete. The wall gets a dimpled membrane. Seepage runs down behind it and into the pipe.

    Crack Injection for Basements in Newport News

    Injection works on poured concrete walls, not block. We set ports along the crack, seal the face with epoxy paste, and pump in polyurethane or epoxy from the bottom port up until resin shows at the next port. Epoxy bonds the two sides. Polyurethane is better for active leaks because it foams on contact with water.

    Sump Pump Repair and Installation in Newport News, VA

    Plenty of basements rely on a sump pit to handle runoff and groundwater. Saturated ground after a storm can push water into basements and back up sewer lines, making backflow valves and sump pumps worthwhile. Pumps from big-box stores often last five to seven years, and the first sign of trouble is usually a pump that runs constantly or hums without moving water. Same-day replacement is normal.
