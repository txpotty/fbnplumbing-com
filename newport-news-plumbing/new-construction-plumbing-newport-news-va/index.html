    Frequently Asked Questions About New Construction Plumbing in Newport News

    Most new single-family homes in the Newport News area cost between $12,000 and $25,000 to plumb, with larger custom homes running higher. A bathroom adds a few thousand on its own. Tankless heaters and gas runs add to it. We give a fixed bid per phase so you know the number before the slab is poured and do not get hit with surprise charges at trim.

    For supply, we use PEX-A or PEX-B in most homes, sometimes with a copper main. Drains are PVC or ABS. Some builders want cast iron in walls beside bedrooms, since it is far quieter than PVC when an upstairs toilet flushes.

    PEX-A is our default for water supply in Virginia homes. It tolerates hot water and avoids the pinhole leaks copper can develop in some water. We run drains in Schedule 40 PVC. Under slabs, we keep joints to a minimum and use long-sweep fittings at every change of direction so a cable or camera can get through later.

    It matters more than most owners expect. Corrections need a fast return trip. A plumber based an hour away may not come back until next week to add a missing strap, which can hold up your insulation inspection and every trade after it.

    Underground plumbing on a typical house takes one to three days. Plan on three to five days for top-out and a day or two for trim. The calendar time is longer, because each phase waits on concrete, framing, inspection or cabinets, so the plumber is on and off site over three to six months. Weather moves it too.

    Plumbing is usually covered under the general building permit. If the jurisdiction wants a separate plumbing permit, our license goes on it. Either way, we schedule the plumbing inspections, attend them, and handle any corrections without the builder having to chase us down about it.

    The term is written into your contract before work starts. Our labor and the fittings we provide are covered. Manufacturer warranties cover fixtures and heaters, and we register the water heater in your name at final.
