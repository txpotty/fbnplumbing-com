    Anode Rod Checks in Newport News

    The anode rod rusts so the tank does not. Once the rod is eaten down to its core wire, the water goes after the tank itself. We pull it and look. Replacing the rod is much cheaper than replacing the heater.

    Tankless Water Heater Service in Newport News, VA

    Tankless units need descaling. Scale inside the heat exchanger chokes flow until the unit throws codes or shuts off mid-shower. A pump circulates descaler through it. We also check the igniter, the fan and the exhaust vent for safe operation.

    Relief Valve and Safety Testing in Newport News, VA

    The relief valve is the tank's last line of defense. We test the valve, confirm it reseats and check that the discharge pipe is routed correctly. Leaky valves get replaced. Expansion tanks get an air charge check.

    Flushing a Tank Water Heater in Newport News

    Popping and rumbling mean sediment. We flush until it runs clear. Heavy buildup may take several cycles.
