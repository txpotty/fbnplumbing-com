    Grinding Noise Repair in Newport News

    Loud metal-on-metal sounds may mean a loose mount, a foreign object or worn impellers. After checking the chamber, snugging the mount and pulling debris, we run it empty to hear if the noise is coming from the motor. Bad bearings mean replacement.

    Preventive Care and Longevity in Newport News, VA

    Keep cold water running before, during and after use, and let it go about 15 seconds after the motor stops so the food clears the trap. Grease poured in with hot water coats the chamber and the pipe, while cold water keeps it solid so the impellers can break it up. Fibrous scraps jam it most. Celery, artichoke leaves and corn husks belong in the trash.

    Noisy Disposal Repair in Newport News

    Grinding, rattling or a hum that sounds rough all point to something inside. A loose mount will rattle. We check the mount, then look for debris, then run the motor empty, since a whine with an empty chamber means the bearings are going.

    Flange and Inlet Leak Repair in Newport News, VA

    The flange, dishwasher inlet, discharge tube and body are where leaks start. We trace the leak, reseal the flange with plumber's putty and replace gaskets or tubes as needed. A leak from the bottom of the unit usually means the internal seal has failed, and in that case replacement is the better option. It is not a repairable part.

    Commercial Disposal Repair in Newport News

    Restaurant disposals run 1 to 5 horsepower and often have a separate control panel and solenoid water valve. When one stops, the dish line backs up fast. We check the controls first. A bad solenoid or tripped overload in the panel kills the unit as fully as a jam does, but it gets fixed far faster.

    Leak Detection and Repair in Newport News, VA

    Disposal leaks come from four places: the sink flange, the dishwasher inlet, the discharge tube and the housing itself. We dry everything, run water with a little food coloring and watch to see which joint shows color first, since water runs down the outside of the unit and hides the real source. Most leak points can be repaired. A leak from the housing means it is time for a new unit.

    Jammed Disposal Repair in Newport News

    A jam usually comes from one hard object, like a peach pit, a chicken bone or a twist tie wrapped around the impeller. We free the flywheel from below with the hex key and pull the object with pliers, never a hand, then run it with water to make sure nothing else is stuck. We press the reset after clearing it. Check the trap arm if it jams again.

    Noise and Odor Diagnosis in Newport News

    A rattle that stops after a few seconds is usually a spoon or a pit bouncing in the chamber. A constant high whine means something else. A steady whine usually points to failing motor bearings, and the noise keeps building until the motor locks up. Odors usually trace to the splash guard. We pull it, clean the chamber walls and replace the guard if it is torn.
