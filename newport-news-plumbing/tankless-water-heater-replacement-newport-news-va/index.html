    Picking a Tankless for Your Newport News, VA House

    Commercial Tankless Racks in Newport News, VA

    Restaurants and salons run tankless racks hard. A rack of four units can draw more than 600,000 BTU, so we confirm gas pressure at the far unit with every burner firing. We work after hours when needed.

    Commercial Tankless Replacement in Newport News

    Cascade controls on a commercial rack decide which unit fires first, and after one unit is replaced they need rebalancing. Otherwise one unit does all the work. Descaling gets scheduled by usage.

    Residential Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Newport News

    Sizing a tankless starts with how many fixtures run at once and how cold the incoming water is, not the square footage of the house. Run two showers and the dishwasher together and you are near 8 GPM. The unit is chosen from that figure. Any gas, water or vent changes the new unit needs get done to current code.

    Navien Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Newport News, VA

    Navien NPE units are condensing, and the A models have a built-in recirculation pump and buffer tank that cut down on cold-water sandwiches. Their combi boilers handle both space heat and domestic water. Both are in our wheelhouse.

    Emergency Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Newport News

    No hot water from a tankless usually shows up as an error code on the controller, and the code tells us half of what we need to know. A photo of the error code helps a lot. A leaking heat exchanger means replacement, usually done same day or next day.

    Tankless-to-Tankless Replacement in Newport News

    Replacing an existing tankless with a new one is usually the simpler job, since the gas line, vent path and water connections are already there. We confirm they are sized right for the new unit. Old service valves often get replaced too.

    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Newport News, VA

    Exterior-mount tankless units hang on an outside wall and vent straight out the front, so there is no vent pipe to run. Sun and weather wear on the covers and wiring. Most Virginia winters bring several hard freezes, and cold snaps can burst pipes in crawlspaces, garages and exterior walls. We check the freeze protection settings and the pipe cover kit on every outdoor swap. They are usually the easiest swap.

    Rinnai Condensing Upgrades in Newport News, VA

    Rinnai Sensei condensing models replace older non-condensing Rinnai units in a lot of homes, but the venting changes from stainless to PVC. The vent swap is part of our job. Most of the plumbing lines up already.
