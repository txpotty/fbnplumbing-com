    Blower motors and pressure switches are the weak points on power vent tanks. When one fails twice on a tank that is already 8 or 9 years old, replacing the whole heater is usually cheaper over time. A dedicated outlet gets confirmed first.

    Bradford White tanks are sold only through plumbing wholesalers, not big-box stores, and plenty of Virginia plumbers install them for that reason. Replacement parts are easy to find. Installing to their specs keeps the tank warranty intact.

    Gas Water Heater Efficiency Ratings in Chesapeake

    Efficiency ratings on tank heaters are listed as UEF, uniform energy factor, and a higher number means less gas per gallon heated. A standard tank sits around 0.60 to 0.65. Condensing models top 0.80, and some qualify for utility rebates where those are offered.

    Tankless Gas Conversion in Chesapeake

    Going tankless usually means a bigger gas line, since a whole-house tankless unit can draw 199,000 BTU against the 40,000 a tank burner uses. Most homes need the line upsized from the meter. The venting changes too. Before quoting, we measure pipe runs and check the meter rating, which keeps the price from moving later.

    Choosing Between Gas Tank Brands in Chesapeake, VA

    Warranty length on a tank mostly tracks anode size and burner design, so a 12-year tank is a different build from a 6-year one. Self-cleaning dip tubes help on hard water. Powered anodes outlast standard rods. We price it against how long you plan to stay.

    Propane Gas Water Heater Replacement in Chesapeake, VA

    Propane heaters run at about 11 inches of water column instead of the 7 inches typical for natural gas, so the gas valve and orifice have to match the fuel. A natural gas unit does not go on propane unless the manufacturer allows a conversion. We confirm pressure with a manometer.

    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Chesapeake, VA

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust through 2 or 3-inch PVC, so they can sit far from a chimney. They need a 120-volt outlet nearby. We run the vent within the maker's length and elbow limits.
