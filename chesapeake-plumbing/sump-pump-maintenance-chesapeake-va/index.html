    Sump Pump Maintenance in Chesapeake, VA: Pits, Floats and Valves

    Home Sump Pump Maintenance in Chesapeake

    A typical house pit is about 18 inches across and two feet deep, and most of what goes wrong happens inside it. We pull the pump, clear the intake screen and scoop out the silt. After that, the pump gets a real water test. Expansive clays in some areas swell when wet and shrink when dry, which can crack foundations and shift buried sewer lines.

    Commercial Sump Pump Maintenance in Chesapeake, VA

    Commercial pits are often bigger and deeper, and many have two pumps on an alternating controller. Each pump gets tested, along with the alternator and the high-water alarm. Property managers get a written log of every reading we take, which helps at insurance renewal and during code inspections. Quarterly visits are common.

    Battery Backup System Testing in Chesapeake, VA

    Storms knock out power when the pit fills fastest. A backup pump exists for exactly that hour. We check the battery date, clean the terminals and confirm the charger is actually charging, since a dead charger quietly drains the battery to nothing over a few months.

    Check Valve and Discharge Pipe Repair in Chesapeake, VA

    A bad check valve can nearly double how often the pump starts. Every startup puts heat into the motor. Our plumbers replace worn valves with quieter spring-loaded types and check the pipe for cracks and loose couplings out to where it leaves the house. Discharge needs to land far enough from the slab or footings that it does not seep right back into the pit.

    Emergency Sump Pump Diagnostics in Chesapeake, VA

    Grinding, clicking and a hum with no water moving each point to a different failure. Our plumber works from the outlet and cord to the float and then the motor. A tripped GFCI is a quick fix. A seized motor calls for a new pump, so we carry replacements and never leave the pit unprotected overnight.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Maintenance in Chesapeake, VA

    Backup pumps sit in the pit next to the main pump and run off a 12-volt battery when the power drops or the primary fails. Our plumber load tests the battery instead of just reading its voltage. Then we unplug the main pump and let the backup empty the pit on its own. Alarms get tested too.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Service in Chesapeake, VA

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor up on a column above the water, so the wear shows up in the shaft, the bearings and the float rod. We check the rod for bends and the stops for slipping. Bearings get a listen. Rattling or a high whine at startup usually means the motor is near the end.

    Crawlspace and Window Well Pumps in Chesapeake, VA

    Not every sump sits in a basement. Crawlspaces and deep window wells often have small pumps that clear runoff after a storm. We clean their screens, test the floats and follow the discharge until it reaches daylight or an approved drain, because a line that dead-ends under the house just moves the water around. Disconnecting hoses, shutting off outdoor spigots and keeping crawlspace vents closed in winter are easy ways to prevent freeze damage.
