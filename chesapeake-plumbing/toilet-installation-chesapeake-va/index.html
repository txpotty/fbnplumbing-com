    High-Efficiency Toilet Installation in Chesapeake, VA

    High-efficiency toilets flush on 1.28 gallons or less, against 3.5 gallons or more for toilets from before the early 1990s. Look for the WaterSense label on the box.     Comfort height bowls stand about two inches taller than standard ones. Think about who will use it before you buy. Kids and shorter adults may not like their feet off the floor, but people with sore knees or a new hip usually love the extra height. Installation is the same as a standard bowl.

    Sloan and Zurn flushometer valves are the standard on commercial toilets for a reason. There is no tank to refill.
