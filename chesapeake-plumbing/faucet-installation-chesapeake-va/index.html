    Outdoor Faucet and Hose Bib Installation in Chesapeake

    Hose bibs get more abuse than any indoor faucet. Higher elevations in the west of Virginia freeze more often and harder than coastal areas, so pipe insulation matters more there. We match the hose bib to that, using a frost-free sillcock with its seat set back inside the heated wall wherever freezing is a real risk. Code calls for a vacuum breaker on every hose bib. Most frost-free models include one, so the backflow protection is built right into the body.

    Touchless Faucet Installation in Chesapeake

    Touchless faucets run on a solenoid valve. We mount the control box high on the cabinet wall, away from where water collects, and power it with batteries or a plug-in adapter. Scale is hard on solenoids, and we install the inline filter when the maker calls for one. We test the sensor range last.

    Garage and Workshop Faucets in Chesapeake

    Workshop and garage sinks get abused. We install faucets with metal handles and a threaded spout, anchored to the wall or the tub deck so a pulled hose does not twist the supply lines. We check the flow at full open. A slow tub drain gets its trap cleared at the same visit.

    Replacing a Leaky Faucet in Chesapeake, VA

    A faucet dripping once a second wastes a few thousand gallons a year. When the body itself is corroded or the brand stopped making parts, replacing it beats paying for a third cartridge swap. Out comes the old faucet. New supplies and a full leak check follow, and the whole visit usually takes about an hour.

    Restaurant and Office Faucets in Chesapeake, VA

    Restaurant pre-rinse units mount to the wall and the sink deck. The spring arm and spray valve take a beating, so we anchor the wall bracket into blocking and use check valves on the supply lines. Hand-wash sinks get wrist blades. We work after close so the line keeps running.

    Vessel Sink Faucets in Chesapeake, VA

    Vessel sinks sit high, so the faucet has to sit higher. A tall single-hole faucet or a wall-mount spout works, and we pick the height so the stream lands near the drain instead of the bowl rim. Reach matters as much as height. A short spout means knuckles against the bowl edge.

    High-End Faucet Installation in Chesapeake, VA

    High-end faucets ship with more parts in the box. Premium lines from Brizo and Grohe often use a separate valve body plus finish-matched trim that has to be assembled in a set order under the deck. We use padded wrenches on the finish. Brushed gold and matte black scratch easily, so nothing bare and metal touches the trim.

    Laundry Sink Faucet Installation in Chesapeake

    Laundry sinks get a faucet with a threaded spout. Deck or wall models go in with a built-in vacuum breaker, because a hose sitting in dirty wash water can siphon back into the supply. We clip the supply lines so nothing rubs.
