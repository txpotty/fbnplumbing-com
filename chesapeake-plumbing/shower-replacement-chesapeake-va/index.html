    Old bathtubs that nobody uses take up the best spot in the bathroom. We remove the tub, reroute the drain to the new pan location and install the valve at shower height, then set walls and a door or glass panel. Stepping in gets easier. That is a big deal for many households.

    Emergency Shower Leak Solutions in Chesapeake, VA

    Full replacement is rarely an emergency, but a major leak can be. We isolate a serious leak, stop it and make a safe temporary fix, which gives you time to plan the replacement. Then we assess. We tell you if full replacement is the right long-term fix.

    Old Shower Valve Replacement in Chesapeake

    Two- and three-handle valves from decades back often leak and lack scald protection. We open the wall from the back side when we can, remove the old valve and install a pressure-balance or thermostatic valve, then cover the old opening with an oversized trim plate. Usually no new tile. Safety improves a lot.

    Shower Valve and Fixture Upgrades in Chesapeake

    Sometimes the shower only needs its guts replaced. If the pan and walls are sound, we can swap an old valve, diverter and trim without tearing out tile, which fixes drips and temperature swings for a fraction of a full rebuild. It is a big upgrade. Half a day, usually.

    Prefab Shower Replacement in Chesapeake

    Prefab units past their useful life usually show it at the base. Fading, small cracks and a spongy floor mean water got through, so the subfloor and framing get checked after removal. New unit next. We set it on mortar and seal the drain.

    Aging-in-Place Shower Replacement in Chesapeake

    Aging in place often starts with getting rid of the tub. We remove it, adjust the drain and install a new shower valve at the right height, with blocking for grab bars and a handheld shower on a slide bar so the shower works seated or standing. No tall curb. It is one of the safest changes you can make.
