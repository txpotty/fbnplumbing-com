    Gas Appliance Connector Repair in Chesapeake, VA

    Weak flames or a smell near one appliance often point to the connector or shutoff behind it.     Pool Heater, Fire Pit and Grill Lines in Chesapeake, VA

    Backyard gas lines for pool heaters, fire pits and built-in grills face sun, weather and irrigation water that shorten their life. Repairs and new runs are tested, buried at code depth and marked with tracer wire.

    Gas Line Relocation and Upgrades in Chesapeake, VA

We calculate the full load first. The new pipe gets sized so every burner in the house still has full pressure at once.
