    Slab Leak Location and Repair in Chesapeake

    Slab leaks hide under concrete until something gives them away. Expansive clays in some areas swell when wet and shrink when dry, which can crack foundations and shift buried sewer lines. Our techs find them with ground microphones and thermal imaging, so the only concrete we break is the small patch right over the leak, not half the kitchen floor. After that, you choose the repair. Some leaks get a spot repair, others a reroute overhead that skips the slab entirely.
