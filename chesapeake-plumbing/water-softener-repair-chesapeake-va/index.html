    Resin Testing and Replacement in Chesapeake

    Resin beads trap hardness minerals and release them during regeneration. Chlorine and chloramine in city water slowly break resin down until it turns to mush. After a capacity test, the resin gets cleaned or replaced, and a good tank and valve with new resin performs like new. Mushy resin can clog the valve.

    Salt Usage and Settings Repair in Chesapeake

    Too much salt or water use usually means settings that do not match your hardness or household. Older timer units regenerate on a schedule no matter how much water you used. We retest hardness and reprogram the valve, or upgrade the head to a metered control that only regenerates when the resin is actually close to exhausted. Salt bills drop.

    Salt Bridge and Brine Tank Service in Chesapeake, VA

    The tank looks full but makes no brine. A hard crust has formed and is keeping the salt from dissolving. Bridges get broken up, mushy salt gets cleaned out and the float, brine well and safety valve get checked. Pellet salt bridges less in warm or humid spaces, so we may suggest switching if your softener sits in a hot garage.

    Softener Not Regenerating in Chesapeake

    Hard water coming back usually means the softener has stopped regenerating. A failed timer motor, a bad circuit board or a stuck meter turbine are the usual causes, along with settings lost in a power outage. We test each part. Then we program the valve for your household and hardness, and soft water returns by the next cycle.
