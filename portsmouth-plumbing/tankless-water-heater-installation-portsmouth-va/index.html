    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Portsmouth, VA

    Calcium scale is the most common reason tankless heaters fail. Private wells serve many rural Virginia homes, and some well water carries iron, manganese, low pH or sulfur that calls for treatment. Scale coats the heat exchanger, cuts efficiency and triggers error codes, and left alone it can crack the exchanger and end the warranty claim before it starts. A yearly flush prevents it. Service valves make it easy.
