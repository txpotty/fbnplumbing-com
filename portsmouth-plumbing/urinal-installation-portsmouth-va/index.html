    Rough-In Plumbing for Urinals in Portsmouth, VA

    Rough-in decides whether the finish goes smoothly. We set the carrier, run a 2 inch waste line to the outlet height on the spec sheet, stub the 3/4 inch supply where the flush valve will land, and tie the vent in before anyone hangs drywall. The rough gets inspected before cover. Then the walls close.

    Urinal Installation Cost Factors in Portsmouth, VA

    Carrier work is the biggest cost swing. A replacement on an existing carrier with good supply and drain lines costs far less than a new install that needs a carrier, a new 2 inch drain and a 3/4 inch supply run through finished walls. Touchless valves cost more than manual. We itemize each piece.

    Waterless Urinal Upgrades in Portsmouth

    Switching to waterless is mostly a fixture swap. The old flush valve comes off, the supply gets capped behind the wall, and the new waterless bowl goes on the same carrier with its cartridge housing tied into the existing drain. Many jurisdictions want a capped supply kept for later. That allows a future change back.
