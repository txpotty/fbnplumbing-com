    How We Keep Water Out of Walls in Portsmouth, VA

    Crawl Space Encapsulation in Portsmouth

    Encapsulation seals the whole crawl space, walls and floor, with a reinforced poly liner. We run 12 to 20 mil liner up the walls, tape every seam, seal around piers and pipe penetrations, and often add a dehumidifier to keep relative humidity below 60 percent. Frequent swings between mild days and freezing nights through winter put repeated stress on pipes and outdoor fixtures across Virginia. Wood rot slows to a stop. Drier air under the floor also cuts down on the musty smell in the house.

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Portsmouth

    Exterior waterproofing means digging down to the footing and coating the outside of the wall. It is the most reliable fix for a leaking basement, because the water never touches the block or concrete. We apply a rubberized asphalt or polymer membrane, add a dimple board to protect it, and set perforated drain pipe in gravel along the footing so water has somewhere to go. Soils in Virginia range from sandy ground with a high water table near the coast to dense clay and rocky karst farther inland. We backfill after the drain is tested.

    Foundation Crack Repair & Sealing in Portsmouth

    Hairline cracks in poured concrete walls leak more water than their size suggests. We inject them with low-pressure polyurethane foam, which expands to fill the crack from the inside face to the soil side, or with epoxy when the crack also needs structural bonding. Horizontal cracks are different. A horizontal crack often signals soil pressure pushing the wall in, so we check for movement with a level before we seal anything. Most vertical cracks get sealed in one visit.

    Waterproofing for Older Homes in Portsmouth, VA

    Houses built before modern drainage rules often have no footing drain at all, or a clay tile drain that collapsed decades ago. Many homes built from the late 1970s into the 1990s used gray polybutylene supply lines, a material known for leaks and sudden failure. Fieldstone and old brick foundations need breathable products. Sealing soft old brick with a rigid coating traps water inside it and makes the face spall off over a few seasons, so we use lime-compatible or vapor-open coatings and pair them with a new interior drain. Old mortar joints get repointed first.

    Sump Pump Installation and Repair in Portsmouth, VA

    A sump pump is the part of an interior system that actually moves the water. We set a lidded basin below slab level, install a cast iron pump sized for the inflow, and add a battery backup or water-powered backup for the night the power goes out during a storm. Check valves matter. Without one, the discharge water runs back into the pit and the pump short-cycles. The discharge line gets routed far enough out that it drains away from the house.
