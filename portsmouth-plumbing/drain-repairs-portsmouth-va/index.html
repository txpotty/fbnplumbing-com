    Most trap and fixture drain repairs take one to two hours. Hidden pipe adds time. Replacing drain pipe inside a wall or under a floor can take half a day to a full day, depending on how much finish material has to come out. The estimate includes the timeline.

    Repeat clogs come from something in the pipe catching debris. Usually it is buildup. Poor pipe slope, a missing vent or a cracked fitting with a rough edge catches debris over and over, even after the line is cleared. A camera shows which one you have.

    Call us if a plunger or a small hand snake has not cleared it. Repeat clogs too. Multiple slow drains, sewer smell or water coming up in a different fixture than the one you are using point to a blockage or defect past the reach of a home snake. Those call for professional equipment.
