    Water Softener Problems We Fix in Portsmouth, VA

    Control Valve Rebuild in Portsmouth

    Service, backwash, brine and rinse are the four cycles the control valve directs every time the softener regenerates, usually in the middle of the night. Hard water and chlorine wear out the seals, spacers and pistons. Leaks at the valve or water running to the drain are common signs. A matching rebuild kit fixes it.

    Resin Bed Replacement in Portsmouth

    Chlorine eats resin slowly, and after enough years the beads swell, break and stop exchanging the calcium and magnesium they are supposed to hold. This is common with older systems on treated city water. FBN Plumbing pulls the old resin and fills the tank with new media. The softener works like new.

    Softener Repair vs Replacement Cost in Portsmouth, VA

    Most softener repairs cost $150 to $450, while a resin replacement often runs $400 to $900 depending on tank size. A new quality softener installed typically costs $1,500 to $3,500. Sound tank and valve, repair wins. If the valve is obsolete and parts are scarce, replacing the head or the whole unit may make more sense than chasing used parts online.

    Resin Bed Cleaning and Replacement in Portsmouth

    Fouled resin is the usual reason a softener regenerates on schedule but still sends hard water to every faucet in the house. Iron and sediment coat the beads and block the exchange. Depending on the test, the resin gets cleaned or replaced. Fresh resin brings capacity back.

    Bypass and Fitting Leak Repair in Portsmouth, VA

    Bypass valves, the O-rings where the valve meets the tank, cracked fittings and the drain line are the usual leak points. A minor drip still wastes water and damages floors and cabinets. We replace O-rings or fittings and test under pressure. Mineral tank cracks cannot be fixed.

    Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Portsmouth, VA

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates the suction that draws brine into the resin tank. Its small ports clog with sediment and salt. Once it clogs, the brine tank fills with water instead of emptying, and the softener regenerates with plain water that does nothing to the resin. We clean or replace it and check the brine line for air leaks.

    Softener Not Regenerating in Portsmouth

    Hard water coming back usually means the softener has stopped regenerating. The usual causes are a dead timer motor, a bad board, a stuck meter or settings wiped by an outage. We test each part. Then we program the valve for your household and hardness, and soft water returns by the next cycle.

    Water Softener Leak Detection and Repair in Portsmouth, VA

    Cracked tanks, loose fittings and failed O-rings are behind most softener leaks. A small drip still wastes water and damages flooring. We find the source, whether it is the bypass, the valve-to-tank seal or the drain connection, and replace the seal or fitting that failed. Then it gets a pressure test.
