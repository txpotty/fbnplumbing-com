    Perma-Liner and NuFlow Trenchless Systems in Portsmouth

    Perma-Liner and NuFlow both make lining systems we use, each with its own resin chemistry and installation equipment. Both are built to ASTM standards for cured-in-place pipe. The brand matters less than the prep, because a liner bonded to a dirty, scaly pipe wall will not hold the way the manufacturer rates it. We clean to bare pipe first.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Lining in Portsmouth, VA

    CIPP stands for cured-in-place pipe. We wet out a felt tube with epoxy resin, invert it into the cleaned line with air pressure, and hold it against the old pipe wall until the resin hardens. Cure time runs a few hours. The finished liner seals cracks and leaking joints in the original pipe.

    Commercial Trenchless Sewer Repair in Portsmouth, VA

    Office and retail buildings often have a cast iron building drain under the slab that has rotted along the bottom. Lining it avoids cutting the floor. We coordinate around tenant hours. A typical commercial lining job runs one to three days, depending on footage and how many branch connections need to be reinstated with a cutter.

    Orangeburg and Clay Pipe Replacement in Portsmouth

    Orangeburg is bituminous fiber pipe that was laid in houses mostly from the late 1940s into the early 1970s. It goes oval and blisters, so a liner has nothing round to bond to and bursting is usually the better fix. Clay is different. Clay that still holds its round shape is a good candidate for a liner.

    Sewer Camera Inspections for Trenchless Repair in Portsmouth

    Every trenchless job starts with a push camera fed in from the cleanout or a pulled toilet. The screen shows the inside of the pipe live, and a sonde in the camera head lets us mark the exact spot on the ground above it. The locator also reads depth, which matters for pit planning. No guessing where to dig.

    Pipe Bursting in Portsmouth, VA

    Two pits are standard for a burst, one at the house end near the foundation and one out near the street or the city tap. Before pulling, the crew heat-fuses sections of HDPE into one continuous length on the ground. The winch or hydraulic puller then drags the bursting head and the new pipe through. It goes quickly.

    Perma-Liner Trenchless Solutions in Portsmouth

    Perma-Liner makes inversion drums and epoxy systems for lining 2- to 6-inch drain lines, which covers most of what we see in houses. The setup is compact enough to fit in a pickup bed and roll through a side gate. Compact gear matters on tight lots. The resin is mixed on site, so it does not start curing before the liner is in place.
