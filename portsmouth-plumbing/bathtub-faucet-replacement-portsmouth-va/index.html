    Dripping Tub Faucet Replacement in Portsmouth

    Drips at the spout come from inside the valve. On a single-handle faucet the cartridge seals have worn, and on a two- or three-handle faucet the stem washers and brass seats have worn, and either way the drip gets worse every month. New internals end the drip. Cracked bodies get a new valve.

    Upgrading Tub Faucets in Portsmouth, VA

    Plenty of people replace a working tub faucet for looks. A dated chrome cross-handle set can become a brushed nickel or matte black lever trim, and if the valve is a newer universal rough-in, the swap happens without opening the wall at all.     Thermostatic Tub Valve Installation in Portsmouth, VA

    Thermostatic valves hold the temperature you set. Unlike a pressure-balance valve, which only reacts to pressure changes, a thermostatic valve senses the actual water temperature and adjusts the mix, which matters for kids and older adults who cannot react fast to a hot spike. Many have separate heat and flow controls. They cost more than pressure-balance.

    Budget Tub Faucet Options in Portsmouth, VA

    Not every tub faucet needs a full valve. A new cartridge and a matching trim kit from the same brand can make a twenty-year-old single-handle faucet work and look nearly new for a fraction of what a valve replacement costs. We offer that option first. Three-handle faucets are the exception.

    Cartridge and Stem Replacement in Portsmouth, VA

    Most dripping tub faucets need internals, not a new valve. We replace Moen and Delta cartridges or old compression stems and seats, and we grease the O-rings with silicone plumber's grease so the handle turns smoothly for years. Old seats need a seat wrench. About an hour, usually.

    Thermostatic and Pressure-Balance Valves in Portsmouth, VA

    Pressure-balance valves react to pressure drops. When someone flushes a toilet, the valve cuts hot and cold flow in proportion so the mix stays steady, and a thermostatic valve goes further by holding a set temperature even as the water heater cools. Both meet anti-scald code. Thermostatic costs more.

    Private wells serve many rural Virginia homes, and some well water carries iron, manganese, low pH or sulfur that calls for treatment. Corrosion shows up as green crust at the handle or a stem you cannot turn by hand. At that stage the trim threads are often too weak and the valve body is pitted where the cartridge seals. A new valve makes more sense.
