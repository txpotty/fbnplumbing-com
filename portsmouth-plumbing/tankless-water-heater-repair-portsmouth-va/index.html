    Ignition Failure Repair in Portsmouth, VA

    No ignition codes have four usual causes: a dirty flame rod, a bad igniter, low gas pressure or a blocked intake. Undersized gas pipe is the one people miss. A tankless burner can need 150,000 to 199,000 BTU, and the half-inch line that fed a 40,000 BTU tank often cannot carry that, so the unit lights at low flow and fails when two showers run.

    No Hot Water Tankless Repair in Portsmouth

    No hot water at all usually points to ignition, gas supply or a heat exchanger choked with scale. We start with the error history in the service menu, then test the igniter, flame rod and gas pressure in that order, because a weak flame signal and low pressure can look identical on the display. Venting gets checked too. Common igniters and flame rods ride on the truck.

    Noritz Tankless Water Heater Repair in Portsmouth, VA

    Noritz heaters hold up well, but their flow sensors and vent connections still wear out. A code 90 points to combustion trouble, often a dirty fan or a restricted intake. We clean the fan and burner, check the vent joints for gaps, and test the flow sensor's output against the spec sheet. Gaskets get replaced if they leak.

    Electric Tankless Element Repair in Portsmouth, VA

    Electric units fail in their own way. Stiebel Eltron and EcoSmart models use two to four heating elements in separate chambers, so a single burned element leaves the heater behind at high flow, while a failed flow switch means no heat at all. We check each element's resistance, measure voltage under load and check amperage on each leg, then confirm the panel and wire size match the unit's rating.
