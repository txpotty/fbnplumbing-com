    Kinds of Toilet Installation in Dale City, VA

    New Construction Toilet Installation in Dale City

    On new builds we set toilets after the finish floor is down, never before. The flange should sit on top of the finished floor. An early set means the tile goes around the flange, leaving it low, and that calls for an extender ring later. Each bowl gets a fresh seal.

    Standard Toilet Installation in Dale City

    A standard floor-mount toilet is a two-hour job when the flange is sound. We shut off the stop, drain the tank and pull the old bowl. After scraping the old wax off, we check the flange for cracks, set new stainless bolts, and lower the new toilet straight down onto a fresh seal so it compresses evenly. The old one leaves with us.

    Smart Toilet and Bidet Seat Installation in Dale City

    Bidet seats like the TOTO Washlet and Bio Bidet bolt onto most existing elongated bowls. Water comes from a T-valve at the fill line. Heated seats with a warm-air dryer need a nearby GFCI outlet, and when there is none we bring in an electrician first. Non-electric seats take about thirty minutes.

    Commercial Toilet Installation in Dale City, VA

    Commercial restrooms take a beating, so we install vitreous china bowls with flushometer valves rather than tank toilets. Flushometers want a 1 inch supply line and steady pressure. Before setting fixtures in an office or shop, we check that the building supply can deliver the flow the valve is rated for, often 20 to 25 gallons per minute at the fixture. Fixture counts follow code.

    High-Efficiency Toilet Installation in Dale City, VA

    High-efficiency toilets flush on 1.28 gallons or less, against 3.5 gallons or more for toilets from before the early 1990s. Look for the WaterSense label on the box. A MaP rating of 800 grams or better is what we look for, since a weak low-flow toilet that needs double flushing saves no water at all. Dual-flush models add a lighter half flush.

    Smart Toilet Installation in Dale City, VA

    Smart toilets with heated seats and built-in bidets need two things the old toilet did not: power and a dedicated water connection. Most require a GFCI outlet within reach of the cord. We run the water side and mount the unit, and if a new outlet is needed we bring in a licensed electrician rather than splicing anything ourselves. Then we test every button.

    Rough-In Toilet Installation in Dale City, VA

    Rough-in is the plumbing that goes in before walls and floors close up. On a toilet, that is a vented 3 inch drain and a cold water stub. The drain goes 12 inches out from the finished wall line, which means we add the drywall thickness to the framing measurement. Then the inspector sees it open.

    Wall-Hung Carrier Systems in Dale City

    In-wall carriers from Geberit and Grohe each mount a little differently, and the instructions matter. We follow the maker's frame height chart exactly. Set the frame too low and the bowl ends up at child height, too high and nobody's feet touch the floor, and neither can be fixed once the wall is tiled. Measure twice here.
