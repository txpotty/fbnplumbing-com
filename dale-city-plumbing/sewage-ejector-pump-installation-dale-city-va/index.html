    What Goes Into an Ejector System in Dale City, VA

    Duplex and Commercial Ejector Systems in Dale City, VA

    Commercial buildings, multi-unit properties and busy homes often need duplex systems with an alternating control panel. The panel switches the lead pump each cycle so wear is shared, and if the lead pump fails or the flow is heavy, the second pump starts automatically and the panel trips an alarm so someone knows to call for service. Redundancy matters here.

    Zoeller Ejector Pump Installation in Dale City, VA

    Zoeller has made ejector pumps for decades, and its cast iron models are the ones we see still running in older basements. We install Zoeller for both new basins and drop-in replacements, and its standard models often fit existing pits and discharge piping without changes. Floats and switches are stocked locally.

    Residential Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Dale City

    Residential ejector systems in Dale City serve basement bathrooms, lower-level suites and any fixture that sits too low to drain by gravity to the sewer. The setup is a sealed basin, a pump, a float switch, a check valve and a vent. Simple parts, exact install. Most problems come from the install, not the hardware, so we take our time on the vent and the lid seal.

    Grinder Pump Installation in Dale City

    Grinder pumps chop waste into slurry before pumping it, which lets them push through small pipe over long distances. A grinder makes sense when the sewer is distant, uphill or pressurized, such as a pressure sewer where each home pumps into a shared main. Grinders run pricier. For a basement bath near the drain, a standard ejector usually works.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Basin Installation in Dale City, VA

    The basin is where the system lives, and a 30-gallon size is common for a single basement bathroom. A larger basin means longer, less frequent cycles that spare the motor and switch, so we go bigger when the pit takes a laundry or several fixtures. Lids must be gas-tight.

    Commercial Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Dale City, VA

    Businesses in Dale City, VA with restrooms, kitchens or break rooms below grade need ejector systems that keep up with heavier, steadier use than a house. A duplex system with an alternating panel is common, so if one pump fails the other keeps things running. Closed restrooms cost business. We size both pumps to carry the full load.

    Liberty Pumps Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Dale City, VA

    Liberty builds simplex and duplex packages that arrive with the basin, pump, lid and fittings matched. Matched packages speed up the install, and Liberty's float design is less likely to snag on the basin wall, a common reason older pumps fail to start. Switch failures cause many calls.
