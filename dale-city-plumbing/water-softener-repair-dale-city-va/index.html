    Salt Bridge & Brine Tank Repair in Dale City

    Salt bridges form when a hard crust builds across the brine tank and the salt below it stops dissolving into the water. The tank looks full, but the softener cannot make brine. We break the bridge, clean out the mushy salt and check the float and brine well. Cracked tanks get replaced.

    Softener Repair vs Replacement Cost in Dale City, VA

    Most softener repairs cost $150 to $450, while a resin replacement often runs $400 to $900 depending on tank size. A new quality softener installed typically costs $1,500 to $3,500.     Softener Not Regenerating in Dale City

    Hard water coming back usually means the softener has stopped regenerating. The usual causes are a dead timer motor, a bad board, a stuck meter or settings wiped by an outage. We test each part. After the repair, the valve is programmed for your household size and hardness so you have soft water by the next cycle.

    No-Salt Water Conditioner Repair in Dale City, VA

    No-salt conditioners do not remove minerals, they change how scale forms, but they still need service. If scale comes back or pressure drops, the media or the prefilter is usually the problem. FBN Plumbing repairs these units in Dale City, VA. Media lasts a few years.

    Resin Bed Cleaning and Replacement in Dale City

    Fouled resin is the usual reason a softener regenerates on schedule but still sends hard water to every faucet in the house. Iron and sediment coat the beads so they cannot grab hardness. Depending on the test, the resin gets cleaned or replaced. Fresh resin brings capacity back.
