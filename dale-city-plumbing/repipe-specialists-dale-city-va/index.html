    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Dale City, VA

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside, and over decades the inside diameter closes down to a fraction of what it was. Pressure drops slowly. Rust-colored water in the morning, poor pressure upstairs and dripping threaded joints all point to failing galvanized. New PEX or copper takes its place. Owners usually notice stronger flow the first day.

    Drain Line Repiping in Dale City

    Drain pipe wears out too, especially old cast iron that cracks and scales up inside. Slow drains in several fixtures, sewer odors and repeated clogs in the same line are common signs of a failing drain system. We camera the lines first.     Repiping for Hard Water in Dale City, VA

    Private wells serve many rural Virginia homes, and some well water carries iron, manganese, low pH or sulfur that calls for treatment. Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium scale inside pipe, and over years it narrows the bore and speeds up corrosion in metal lines. Pressure suffers. In hard water homes, PEX-A is often the better pick since it resists scale, and a softener loop or filter can be added during the job. Adding the loop now costs less than later.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Dale City, VA

    PEX tubing resists freezing damage, corrosion and scale better than most metal pipe. It is quieter too. Flexible PEX soaks up some of the pressure spike behind water hammer, and its long fitting-free runs leave fewer weak points. Attic PEX gets kept away from flues and out of UV.

After multiple leaks, we will show you why a full repipe may cost less over the next ten years.
