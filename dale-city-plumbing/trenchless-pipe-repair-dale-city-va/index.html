    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Dale City

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. It hardens inside the old pipe. Once inside the cleaned pipe, it gets inflated against the wall and cured with heat or UV, forming a new pipe with no joints. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your Dale City yard.

    Pipe Bursting Services in Dale City, VA

    Bursting replaces a pipe that is too far gone to line. The old pipe gets split. A winch pulls a bursting head through the line, cracking the old pipe outward into the soil while new HDPE follows right behind it, so the replacement pipe ends up in the same path. HDPE does not rust or leak at joints in Dale City, VA soil.

    Pipe Bursting in Dale City, VA

    Bursting is used when the old pipe is too damaged to line or needs a larger diameter. You'll see an entry and exit pit. The head fractures the old pipe as it travels, with new HDPE trailing behind, fused into one length with no joints.
