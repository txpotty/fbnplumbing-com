    How We Install Kitchen Sinks in Leesburg, VA: The Details

    Top-mount Sink Installation in Leesburg

    Drop-in sinks are common in Virginia homes with laminate or tile counters. The rim holds the sink up. We check the cutout, run a bead of silicone under the lip and tighten the mounting clips evenly around the bowl so the rim seats flat. Then the drain and faucet get connected and tested.

    Drop-In Sink Replacement in Leesburg

    Removing an old drop-in begins by slicing through the old caulk and backing off the clips from below. Old clips are often rusted solid, so we bring a reciprocating saw and cut them off rather than prying on the counter. Laminate chips if you pry. The new sink needs to cover the old cutout fully.

    New Construction Sink Rough-In in Leesburg, VA

    The rough-in decides where the drain and valves end up long before the cabinets arrive. We usually set the kitchen drain stub around 16 to 20 inches off the floor, then adjust for deep bowls or a disposal so the trap does not end up below the wall outlet. Vents go in now. Having the sink and faucet specs early saves moving pipe later.

    Commercial Kitchen Sink Installation in Leesburg, VA

    Three-compartment sinks, prep sinks and hand sinks each have their own health code rules. Prep sinks usually need an indirect drain to a floor sink with an air gap, while the three-compartment sink often runs through the grease trap. The local rule gets checked first. Work is scheduled before or after service so the kitchen does not close.

    Undermount Kitchen Sink Installation in Leesburg

    An undermount depends on its clips and adhesive to stay attached to granite or quartz. We mount the bowl, seal the rim with silicone and brace it while the adhesive sets, which usually takes overnight before the sink can hold a full load of water. Then come the strainers and trap.

    Restaurant Sink Installation in Leesburg

    Restaurants, cafes and commissary kitchens in Leesburg get their commercial sinks installed by FBN Plumbing. Hand sinks need hot and cold. We install three-compartment sinks, prep sinks and hand sinks with the indirect drains, floor sinks and grease trap connections the health inspector will look for.

    Apron-Front Sinks in Leesburg, VA

    Apron sinks need a cabinet cut to fit the front panel, and the bowl must be set before the counter gets measured. We set the sink and frame early, so the fabricator measures from the actual bowl instead of a spec sheet. It prevents a crooked reveal. A cast iron apron sink can weigh 150 pounds or more, so two of us show up.

    Undermount Sink Installation in Leesburg, VA

    Undermounts hang from clips anchored into the underside of the stone, with silicone run around the rim. We support the bowl from below with a brace while the silicone cures, typically for a full day, before loading it with water. Skipping the cure time leads to sagging. A sink that drops even an eighth of an inch opens a gap where water and crumbs collect along the counter edge.
