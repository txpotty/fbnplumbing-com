    Low Shower Pressure Repair in Leesburg, VA

    Low pressure at the shower with good pressure at the sink narrows it down fast. That points to the head, the flow restrictor or the cartridge, not the main or the water heater, and it is usually something we can fix within an hour. We start with the head. If every fixture is weak, we check the regulator and the main.

    Weak Shower Spray Repair in Leesburg, VA

    Most weak showers come down to the head. Minerals fill the nozzles and the screen behind them, and the spray fades so gradually you barely notice until it is a mist. Cleaning often works. A clogged cartridge gets replaced, then we test the flow.

    Mixing Valve Repair in Leesburg

    Mixing valves blend hot and cold inside the wall. Over time the cartridge or spool sticks, and you get a shower that will not stay at one temperature or a handle that turns past where it should before the water warms up. We replace the cartridge. If the valve is too old for parts, we talk through a replacement valve and what it means for the tile.

    Same-Day Shower Leak Repair in Leesburg, VA

    Shut off the main first if water is getting where it should not.     Dripping Showerhead Repair in Leesburg

    Drips from the head itself almost always come from the valve, not the head. If the head drips with the valve closed, water is slipping past the cartridge, and a new head will not change that. The fix is in the valve. Drips from the arm threads just need fresh tape and a snug turn.

    Hot Water Pressure Problems in Leesburg, VA

    Weak hot water with strong cold usually rules out the showerhead. The problem is on the hot side, either sediment in the water heater outlet, a partly closed valve at the heater or a cartridge whose hot port is packed with scale. We test both sides. Good hot flow at the sinks points us straight at the shower cartridge.
