    Culligan Water Softener Installation in Leesburg

    Culligan systems are common in homes around here, and we install and connect them. We follow Culligan's requirements for the drain, bypass and electrical, and we program the system for your tested hardness so it does not over-regenerate. We handle all of it. Existing loops make it faster.

    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Leesburg

    On a whole-house system, the softener goes on the main line after the shutoff and before the water heater. We add a bypass valve so you can isolate the softener for service, and we install a pressure regulator if your street pressure runs above 80 psi. Every tap gets soft water. Outdoor taps can be left off.

    Fleck Water Softener Installation in Leesburg, VA

    Fleck valves, especially the 5600SXT and 7000SXT, are on a lot of the softeners we install. Their parts are easy to find and the valves can be rebuilt instead of replaced, so the system lasts. Settings match your test. We confirm the setup before leaving.

    Softener Loop Installation in Leesburg

    No loop? A loop is the plumbing that routes the main line through a softener, and many older homes were built without one. We add the loop. Where we can, we keep hose bibs and irrigation on hard water to save salt. Future service gets easier too.

    Water Softener Replacement in Leesburg, VA

    Older softeners lose ground as resin wears out, valve seals leak or injectors clog. We remove the old unit and install a new metered model, then check the loop and drain line while everything is apart. Salt use drops. Performance comes back right away.

    Water Softener Replacement & Upgrades in Leesburg, VA

    Upgrading an old timer-based softener is one of the easiest ways to cut salt use. Modern units meter water use, send salt reminders by app and come in larger capacities for growing families. The savings add up. We swap it out in a few hours.

    Residential Water Softener Installation in Leesburg

    Hard water costs homeowners money in shorter appliance life and extra cleaning. Our residential installs start with a test and a few questions about water use, then we pick a grain capacity that regenerates about once a week for your household. Weekly is the sweet spot. One visit covers the full install.

    Combination Softener and Carbon Filter Systems in Leesburg, VA

    City water often carries chlorine or chloramine that affects taste and smell. Putting a carbon tank in front of the softener takes out chlorine and keeps it from wearing down the resin. Taste improves fast. We install twin-tank setups or dual-stage units.
