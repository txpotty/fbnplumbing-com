    Large Outdoor Grease Interceptor Cleaning in Leesburg

    Indoor Grease Trap Cleaning in Leesburg

    Indoor traps usually sit under the three-compartment sink or in a recessed pit near the dish station. Space is tight back there.     Grease Line Jetting in Leesburg

    Grease that slips past a full trap coats the 4-inch line out to the main. The buildup forms rings that are thickest along the bottom. A snake cuts a narrow path through and the line plugs again within weeks, while a jetter with a rotating nozzle peels the grease off the whole pipe wall. A camera confirms it afterward.

    Commercial Grease Trap Cleaning in Leesburg

    Hydro-Jetting for Grease Lines in Leesburg, VA
