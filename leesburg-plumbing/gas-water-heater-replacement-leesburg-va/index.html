    Choosing Between Gas Tank Brands in Leesburg, VA

    Warranty length on a tank mostly tracks anode size and burner design, so a 12-year tank is a different build from a 6-year one. Some have self-cleaning dip tubes for sediment. Powered anodes outlast standard rods. We price it against how long you plan to stay.

    High-Efficiency Gas Water Heater Upgrades in Leesburg, VA

    Condensing gas tanks run around 90 percent efficient or better and vent through PVC, while a standard atmospheric tank sits closer to 60 percent. You will need a condensate drain and a nearby outlet. Big households see payback sooner.

    Bradford White Tank Replacement in Leesburg

    Most of the tanks on our trucks are Bradford White, partly because their Defender combustion system and ICON gas valve are simple to service. Model choice follows your hot water use. The install meets current code.

    Propane Gas Water Heater Replacement in Leesburg, VA

    Propane heaters run at about 11 inches of water column instead of the 7 inches typical for natural gas, so the gas valve and orifice have to match the fuel. We never swap a natural gas tank onto propane without the right conversion. Pressure gets checked with a manometer.

    Gas Water Heater Efficiency Ratings in Leesburg

    Efficiency ratings on tank heaters are listed as UEF, uniform energy factor, and a higher number means less gas per gallon heated. Basic tanks land near 0.60 UEF. Condensing models top 0.80, and some qualify for utility rebates where those are offered.

    Forty and fifty gallon gas tanks are the workhorse in most Virginia homes, and a leak at the bottom seam means the tank is finished. Current tanks are often taller and wider. We check clearance before we order. Plan on about two to three hours.
