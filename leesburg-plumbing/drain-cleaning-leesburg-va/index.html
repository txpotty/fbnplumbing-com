    Main Line Drain Cleaning in Leesburg

    Toilets bubbling while the washer drains? The main line is the likely spot.     Hydro Jetting for Drains in Leesburg

    Jetting does what a cable cannot. Water at around 3,000 to 4,000 PSI scours the whole pipe wall. Cabling opens a path, but jetting strips the buildup off the walls. It works best on a kitchen or main line that keeps clogging every few months no matter how often it gets cabled.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Cleaning in Leesburg

    Most clogs in Leesburg cost $150 to $500 to clear. Where the clog sits matters more than anything. A main line that needs a camera run or jetting usually falls between $400 and $800, depending on access and how heavy the buildup is, and you get the number in writing before work starts.

    Water backing up into a different fixture is the giveaway. It points to a branch or the main. A single slow sink is usually a trap or a short branch, but a toilet that bubbles when the tub drains means the blockage is farther down.

    Slow drains are the first sign. After that you may hear gurgling or smell sewer gas. Standing water in a tub or water coming up in one fixture when another drains means the clog is past the trap and into a shared line, which is the point where a plunger will not help.

    Professional equipment from RIDGID, Spartan Tool and Gorlitz. They are built for daily use. These machines run all day in the field and handle clogs from a bathroom sink to a 4 inch main without overheating.
