    Frequently Asked Questions About Basement Leak Repair in Harrisonburg

    Price depends on the source. A supply line or fitting repair usually runs a few hundred dollars. Crack injection and sump work cost more, and perimeter drains or exterior waterproofing can run several thousand. We quote after testing.

    Most plumbing leak repairs in a basement are done in one visit. With wall access, plan on two to four hours. Seepage repairs vary more, from half a day for an injected crack to most of a week for a full interior perimeter drain, which includes cutting and removing the slab edge, setting pipe and stone, and pouring new concrete.

    Crack repairs use two-part polyurethane or epoxy injection resin, depending on whether the crack is actively wet. Polyurethane handles wet cracks. Our interior drains use rigid perforated pipe, which keeps its slope and can be jetted later.

    Yes, labor is covered by our workmanship warranty and parts by the manufacturer. Most pumps carry a multi-year warranty. Model and serial numbers stay on file with us, so a warranty claim does not require hunting for receipts.

    Look for damp spots on walls or floors, a musty smell, and white chalky deposits on concrete. Those deposits are efflorescence, left behind when water evaporates. A rising water bill or rust at the water heater base points toward a plumbing leak. Smell often comes first.

    A pipe or fitting repair usually takes an hour or two. Crack injection takes about half a day. Sumps take most of a day, and perimeter drains take two to five days depending on wall length.

    Tightening a loose fitting or replacing a washer hose is a reasonable DIY task. Cast iron stack cracks are another matter. Hidden slab leaks and sump wiring also belong with a pro, since a mistake can flood the basement or leave an outlet energized in a wet space, and those repairs often need a permit.

    Gutters and downspouts do most of the work. Keep them clear and extended well away from the foundation. Test the sump each spring and swap rubber washer hoses for braided ones every five years.
