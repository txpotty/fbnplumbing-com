    Moen Tub Faucet Replacement in Harrisonburg

    Moen Posi-Temp valves use the 1222 cartridge. That cartridge wears out and causes drips or a handle that will not reach full hot, and swapping it with a cartridge puller takes about an hour when the valve body is in good shape. Moentrol valves use a different part. We carry both.

    Dripping Tub Faucet Replacement in Harrisonburg

    Drips at the spout come from inside the valve. On a single-handle faucet the cartridge seals have worn, and on a two- or three-handle faucet the stem washers and brass seats have worn, and either way the drip gets worse every month. Replacement parts stop it. Cracked bodies get a new valve.

    Three-Handle to Single-Handle Conversion in Harrisonburg

    Three-handle faucets are worth converting. The old valve comes out, a pressure-balance valve goes in, and a remodel plate covers the extra holes. We usually work from behind the wall. Tile around the plate stays intact.

    Hard Water Tub Faucet Replacement in Harrisonburg

    Even moderate hardness in Harrisonburg can leave scale in water heaters over time, so regular flushing helps them run longer and more efficiently. Scale packs into cartridges and spout diverters until handles stiffen and the diverter will not lift. Soaking and cleaning buys time on a lightly scaled valve, but once the brass is pitted or the cartridge has fused to the body, replacing the faucet is the only fix that lasts. One cartridge pull tells us.

    Thermostatic and Pressure-Balance Valves in Harrisonburg, VA

    Pressure-balance valves react to pressure drops. When someone flushes a toilet, the valve cuts hot and cold flow in proportion so the mix stays steady, and a thermostatic valve goes further by holding a set temperature even as the water heater cools. Both meet anti-scald code. Thermostatic costs more.

    Tub Valves in Older Houses in Harrisonburg, VA

    Cast iron tubs and plaster walls change the plan. Plaster cracks wider than drywall when you cut it, so we open plaster carefully with a multi-tool and often go in from an adjacent closet, and the heavy tub stays put while we work on the valve above it. Patching plaster is slow. Access panels avoid it.

    Tub Faucet Repair vs Replacement Costs in Harrisonburg, VA

    A cartridge costs much less than a valve. A new valve makes sense for discontinued parts, a cracked body or an old faucet with no scald protection. You see both prices. Most people choose repair first.

    Replace or Keep Repairing in Harrisonburg

    Repeated repairs add up. A valve on its third cartridge in five years is probably worn inside, so one replacement beats more repairs. Your service history helps us decide. Newer valves often come with lifetime cartridge warranties.
