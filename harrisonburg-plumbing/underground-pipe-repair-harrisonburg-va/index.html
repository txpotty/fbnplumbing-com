    Water Main Underground Repair in Harrisonburg

    Galvanized and Polybutylene Line Replacement in Harrisonburg

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside until little water gets through. Polybutylene cracks without warning.     Underground Sewer Line Repair in Harrisonburg

    Buried sewer damage shows up as odor, slow drains or a soggy patch over the line. Video shows the break.     Pool and Outbuilding Supply Line Repair in Harrisonburg, VA

    Pool auto-fills, guest houses, outdoor kitchens and detached garages often have their own buried water lines. These runs are long.     Pipe Bursting Services in Harrisonburg, VA

    Pipe bursting pulls a new pipe through the old one while breaking the old pipe outward. New pipe follows the old path. A bursting head on a cable splits clay, cast iron or brittle plastic, and fused HDPE follows right behind it, often at the same size or one size larger than the original. Collapsed sections are fine. Lining cannot do that.

    Storm Drain and Lateral Repair in Harrisonburg, VA

    Storm laterals carry roof and yard water away from the foundation. Broken ones leave puddles. We jet the line, camera it for crushed or separated sections and replace the damaged corrugated or PVC pipe so water reaches the outlet instead of soaking the ground around your footings. The outlet gets cleared too.

    Trenchless Underground Pipe Repair in Harrisonburg

    Digging a trench across a mature yard is expensive to restore. Trenchless avoids most of it. We open small access pits at each end, then pull new pipe through the old path or line the old pipe in place, so the expense of putting the yard back together shrinks a lot. Faster, too.
