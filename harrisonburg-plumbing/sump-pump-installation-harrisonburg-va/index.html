    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Harrisonburg, VA

    Backup systems we install use sealed AGM batteries that do not need water. A smart charger keeps the battery topped off and sounds an alarm if the battery weakens, the primary fails or the water rises past the backup float. Expect a battery swap every four to five years. Finished basements get the most out of them.

    Primary Sump Pump Installation in Harrisonburg

    A primary sump pump is the one doing the daily work, kicking on every time the float rises in the pit after rain or snowmelt. We size it from pit diameter and vertical lift. Most homes need 1/3 or 1/2 HP. We usually pair that with a 1-1/2 inch discharge line.

    Liberty Pumps Installation in Harrisonburg, VA

    Liberty builds quiet, well-made submersible pumps with good switch options. Their 250 and 280 series cover most residential lifts, and the vertical float version fits narrow pits where a wide tethered float would rub. Replacement switches are easy to get. We drill a 3/16 inch weep hole in the discharge to stop air lock.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Installation in Harrisonburg, VA

    Zoeller has built cast iron sump pumps for decades, and parts are easy to get. The M53 and the M98 are the two we set most, depending on lift and inflow, and both come with mechanical float switches that hold up for years. Backup and combo units are available too. We help you pick the model that fits the pit.

    Emergency Sump Pump Replacement in Harrisonburg, VA

    A dead pump during a storm means the water is already climbing. Our trucks carry 1/3 and 1/2 HP submersibles, check valves and fittings, so in most homes we can pull the old pump and set a new one in one visit. Most swaps take about two hours. We test it with a full pit before leaving.

    Sump Pump Replacement in Harrisonburg, VA

    Floats stick and motors seize when a pump sits idle for months between storms. When we replace a pump we also check the basin for silt, look at the check valve flapper and run water through the discharge line to find any clogs. If the old one could not keep up, we size up. Testing is done with a full pit.

    Crawl Space Sump Pump Installation in Harrisonburg, VA

    Crawl spaces with standing water breed mold and rot sill plates within a few seasons. We set a basin at the lowest point, run perforated drain pipe to it where needed and seal the vapor barrier around the lid so the space stays dry. Access is often tight. The discharge exits through the rim joist and drains away from the house.
