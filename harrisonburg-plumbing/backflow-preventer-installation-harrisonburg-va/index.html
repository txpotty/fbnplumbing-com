    Pressure Vacuum Breaker (PVB) Installation in Harrisonburg, VA

    Pressure vacuum breakers are the low-cost answer for lawn irrigation in Harrisonburg, VA.     Backflow Testing and Certification in Harrisonburg

    Testable assemblies need certification at install and usually once a year after that.     Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in Harrisonburg

    Double check assemblies work for low-hazard connections such as many fire lines and some irrigation. They have two spring-loaded checks and test cocks for annual certification, and since they do not discharge water, they can go in a vault or below grade where local rules allow, sized so the pressure loss stays small. Flow drives the size.

    Double Check Valve Assembly (DCVA) Installation in Harrisonburg

    A double check valve assembly, or DCVA, uses two spring-loaded checks in series with four test cocks. It is the usual pick for low-hazard lines such as many fire sprinklers, and because it does not discharge water, it can go in a vault where local rules allow it, sized so it does not rob your system of pressure in Harrisonburg. Test cocks stay reachable.
