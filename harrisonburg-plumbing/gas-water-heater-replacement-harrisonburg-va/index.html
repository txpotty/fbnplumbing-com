    Picking a Gas Water Heater for Your Harrisonburg, VA House

    Rheem Gas Water Heater Replacement in Harrisonburg, VA

    Rheem Performance and Performance Platinum tanks cover most of the range we install, from basic 6-year models to 12-year tanks with better anodes. The longer warranty usually means a bigger anode. Choosing is simple.

    Choosing Between Gas Tank Brands in Harrisonburg, VA

    Warranty length on a tank mostly tracks anode size and burner design, so a 12-year tank is a different build from a 6-year one. Some have self-cleaning dip tubes for sediment. Powered anodes last longest. Your plans for the house help decide it.

    Propane Gas Water Heater Replacement in Harrisonburg, VA

    Propane heaters run at about 11 inches of water column instead of the 7 inches typical for natural gas, so the gas valve and orifice have to match the fuel. We never swap a natural gas tank onto propane without the right conversion. Pressure gets checked with a manometer.

    40 and 50 Gallon Tank Replacement in Harrisonburg

    Forty and fifty gallon gas tanks are the workhorse in most Virginia homes, and a leak at the bottom seam means the tank is finished. Current tanks are often taller and wider. We check clearance before we order. Most of these swaps take two to three hours.

    Bradford White Tank Replacement in Harrisonburg

    Most of the tanks on our trucks are Bradford White, partly because their Defender combustion system and ICON gas valve are simple to service. We help you match model to demand. Then it gets installed to code.

    Tankless Gas Conversion in Harrisonburg

    Going tankless usually means a bigger gas line, since a whole-house tankless unit can draw 199,000 BTU against the 40,000 a tank burner uses. Most homes need the line upsized from the meter. The venting changes too. We check meter capacity and pipe length before quoting so there are no surprises halfway through the job.

    Gas Water Heater Efficiency Ratings in Harrisonburg

    Efficiency ratings on tank heaters are listed as UEF, uniform energy factor, and a higher number means less gas per gallon heated. A standard tank sits around 0.60 to 0.65. Condensing models top 0.80, and some qualify for utility rebates where those are offered.

    Atmospheric Vent Gas Water Heater Replacement in Harrisonburg

    Atmospheric vent heaters last 10 to 12 years on average, and the end usually shows up as rusty hot water or a slow drip from the bottom. The new tank ties into your existing gas line and flue. Closets need enough combustion air. We confirm a closet install has louvers or ducted air before we light the burner.
