    High-Capacity Sump Pump Upgrades in Harrisonburg

    If your pump runs every 30 seconds during a storm and still loses ground, it is undersized for the water coming in. We time the cycles and measure how fast the pit refills.     Pedestal Pump Service in Harrisonburg, VA

    Louder, yes, but a pedestal motor sits high and dry, so in a narrow pit it often outlasts a submersible by several years. Narrow pits favor pedestals.     Yard Drainage Pit Pumps in Harrisonburg, VA

    Yard drainage pits and window well pumps can sit dry from fall to spring and then seize the first time a big storm fills them. We swap in a corrosion-resistant pump and clear out leaves and silt. We route the outlet away from the house. Test them each spring.

    Submersible Sump Pump Replacement in Harrisonburg

    A submersible pump sits at the bottom of the pit, sealed in an oil-filled housing, and it runs quieter than anything else you can put down there. Out comes the old pump, out comes the muck at the bottom of the basin, and the new one goes in on a brick to stay clear of silt. Then a new check valve goes on. A worn valve lets water fall back and makes the pump run twice as often.
