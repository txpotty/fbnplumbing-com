    Plumbing Leak Behind Walls in Harrisonburg

    A drip from a shower valve cartridge, a sweated copper stub or a loose drain slip nut can soak a wall cavity for months before paint bubbles. The bad fitting or section of pipe gets replaced. Wet insulation comes out, and fans dry the cavity before we close it up.

    Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in Harrisonburg

    Bare dirt in a crawl space releases gallons of water vapor a day into the air under your floor. A 10 mil or heavier polyethylene barrier, lapped 12 inches at the seams and taped, cuts that off and keeps the joists and subfloor dry. We run it up the foundation walls and hold it in place with termination strip. Cheap fix, big difference.

    Foundation Crack Repair in Harrisonburg

    Crack injection takes about two hours per crack. We set injection ports every 8 to 12 inches along the crack, seal the surface with epoxy paste, and pump in resin starting at the lowest port and working up until it pushes out the next port. Polyurethane flexes a bit if the wall shifts, and epoxy bonds the two sides back together. The surface paste gets ground off once it cures.

    Crawl Space Encapsulation in Harrisonburg

    Encapsulation seals the whole crawl space, walls and floor, with a reinforced poly liner. We run 12 to 20 mil liner up the walls, tape every seam, seal around piers and pipe penetrations, and usually add a dehumidifier to hold humidity under 60 percent. Humid summers and moderate winters are typical for most of Virginia, though mountain areas see colder temperatures and longer heating seasons. Wood rot slows to a stop. Drier air under the floor also cuts down on the musty smell in the house.

    Basement Wall Sealing in Harrisonburg, VA

    Concrete block is porous, and its hollow cores fill with water like a sponge.     Sump Pump Installation and Repair in Harrisonburg, VA

    A sump pump is the part of an interior system that actually moves the water. Check valves matter. A missing check valve lets the discharge line drain back into the pit and the pump short-cycles. The discharge line gets routed far enough out that it drains away from the house.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Walls Waterproofing in Harrisonburg

    Most jobs fall between $3,000 for interior crack sealing and $15,000 or more for exterior excavation with new footing drains.     It depends on the wall and the site. For showers, we use Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban.

    Interior systems manage water after it has come through the wall, with drain tile along the footing and a sump to pump it out. Exterior waterproofing stops water before it reaches the wall, which means digging down to the footing and applying a membrane to the outside face. The exterior route costs more.

    Bubbling paint is a big one. We can confirm it with a meter in a few minutes.

    It removes the water mold needs, so new growth slows down. It does not kill mold that is already there. Any mold already growing needs to be remediated before or alongside the waterproofing, and once the space is dry and sealed it should stay that way.

    Schluter Kerdi, Custom RedGard and Laticrete Hydro Ban cover most of our showers. Sheet membranes like Kerdi suit tight schedules because there is nothing to cure, while liquids like RedGard and Hydro Ban go on in two coats and need to dry between them. We follow each maker's steps exactly.

    Patching one small crack yourself is fine. A full system involves excavation, digging equipment, proper membrane thickness and knowing how water drains around a footing, plus local code on drainage and backfill. Done wrong, it can trap water against the wall and cause more damage.
