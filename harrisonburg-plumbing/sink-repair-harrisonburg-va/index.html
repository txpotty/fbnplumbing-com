    Pop-Up Stopper Repair in Harrisonburg, VA

    Stoppers that will not hold water have a linkage problem. The clevis strap slips on the lift rod, the pivot ball loses its seal, or hair wraps the stopper so tightly it cannot seat on the flange. Cleaning fixes half of them. The other half get a new pop-up drain in a matching finish.

    Supply Line and Angle Stop Replacement in Harrisonburg, VA

    Braided supply lines have a rubber core that ages. In a warm cabinet that core can fail after five to ten years and burst with no warning, and the multi-turn stop above it often will not close when you need it. We swap both in one visit. Quarter-turn stops close in one motion.

    Low Water Pressure at the Sink in Harrisonburg, VA

    Unscrew the aerator before anything else. Most single-faucet pressure problems are a packed aerator screen, and after that we look at the cartridge and the angle stop for debris. Low pressure everywhere points to a different problem. We test the pressure regulator in that case.

    P-Trap Repair and Replacement in Harrisonburg, VA

    The trap holds a plug of water that keeps sewer gas out of the room. Chrome traps that have rusted thin get swapped for PVC or brass.
