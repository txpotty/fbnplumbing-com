    Grease Interceptor Clog Removal in Harrisonburg

    Grease does not stay liquid for long once it cools in a pipe. Within a few feet of the sink it hardens like candle wax along the top of the pipe. Chemical drain openers soften it for a day and then it re-forms farther downstream, sometimes past the trap entirely, where it becomes a sewer problem instead of a kitchen problem. A jetter actually takes it out of the line.

    Residential Grease Trap Repair in Harrisonburg, VA

    Homes rarely need a grease trap, but some have one, usually a small plastic unit under the kitchen sink on a house with a septic system or a home bakery. These leak at the lid or the threaded inlet. We replace the gasket, reset the fittings with fresh thread sealant, and clear any grease plug in the outlet. Small job, usually under an hour.

    Flow Control Device Repair in Harrisonburg

    Hydromechanical traps are rated by flow, like 20 or 50 gallons per minute, and the flow control fitting is what holds the water to that rate. Take it out and a three-compartment sink dump blows grease right past the baffles. Inspectors look for it. We also clear the air intake, since a plugged intake on the flow control fitting lets the trap siphon.

    Inlet and Outlet Tee Repair in Harrisonburg, VA

    Tees are cheap parts with an outsized job. The inlet tee sends flow down below the grease layer. The outlet tee draws water from low in the tank, well under the grease cap, so if it breaks off or someone removes it during pumping, floating grease walks right out to the sewer. New tees go in as Schedule 40 PVC, cut to the drop length the manufacturer or local code specifies.

    Grease Trap Repair vs Replacement Cost in Harrisonburg, VA

    Age and material decide most repair versus replace calls. A 25-year-old steel trap with rust holes is done. A plastic or fiberglass trap with a broken baffle is usually worth fixing, and a concrete interceptor with sound walls and a bad riser almost always is, since replacing a buried tank means excavation and several days closed. You get repair and replacement priced side by side, in writing.

    Hydro-Jetting for Grease Trap Repair in Harrisonburg

    A cable machine punches a hole through grease. Jetting actually scours the pipe wall clean. We run a commercial jetter at 3,000 to 4,000 PSI through the line feeding the trap and the line leaving it, working from the cleanout toward the main. On old cast iron we lower the pressure and watch the camera so we do not open up a thin spot.

    Preventative Grease Trap Maintenance & Repair in Harrisonburg, VA

    Most trap repairs we do trace back to pumping intervals that stretched too long. The common rule is to pump when grease and solids fill a quarter of the liquid depth. After a repair, we measure the grease cap and solids with a sludge judge and suggest a pumping schedule based on how fast your trap actually fills. A kitchen running two fryers can hit that mark in a couple of weeks.
