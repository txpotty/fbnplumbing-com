    PEX Water Line Repair in Fredericksburg, VA

    PEX itself rarely fails underground, but the joints and the first few feet near the house take a beating. Rodents chew it.     Galvanized Pipe Water Line Repair in Fredericksburg, VA

    Galvanized steel service lines rust from the inside out. By the time one leaks, the rest of the pipe is close behind.     Pipe Bursting and Pulling in Fredericksburg

    Pipe bursting means splitting the old line in place while the new one follows behind it. Straight runs are ideal.
