    Descaling Tankless Units in Fredericksburg

    Error codes often mean scale. Tankless heaters are especially sensitive to hard water, and once scale coats the heat exchanger, flow drops, the unit overheats and the control board shuts it down to protect itself. We descale through the service valves. Most makers recommend descaling at least yearly with hard water.

    Electric Water Heater Tune-Up in Fredericksburg

    Electric tanks fail at the lower element. Sediment buries the lower element until it overheats and fails, leaving you with a short supply of hot water. We test both elements. With power off, we check thermostats and connections.

    Tune-Up Versus Replacement in Fredericksburg, VA

    A tune-up costs a fraction of a new heater. Maintenance helps a tank reach its full rated life and helps tankless units last much longer. Sometimes replacement makes sense. We give you real numbers for both.

    Electric Element Service in Fredericksburg, VA

    Gas Burner and Venting Checks in Fredericksburg

    A blue flame is what we want to see. Yellow or lazy flames mean poor combustion, so we clean the burner and combustion chamber, check the thermocouple or flame sensor, and confirm the vent has a proper draft and tight connections. Bad venting is dangerous. We test the gas connection too.
