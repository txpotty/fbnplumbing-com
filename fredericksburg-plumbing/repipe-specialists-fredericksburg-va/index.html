    What Our Repipe Crews Do in Fredericksburg, VA

    Galvanized Pipe Removal in Fredericksburg

    Old galvanized lines shed rust flakes that clog aerators, faucet cartridges and toilet fill valves. Clogged aerators are a clue. Replacing galvanized with PEX or copper ends the rust, brings pressure back and stops the leaks that start at the threaded joints where galvanized is thinnest. Old pipe stays in walls when pulling it would wreck finished surfaces.

    Kitec and Polybutylene Replacement in Fredericksburg

    Kitec, an orange or blue PEX-AL-PEX pipe with brass fittings, and gray polybutylene are both known for failures. Many homes built from the 1980s through the early 2000s have one or the other. The pipe at the water heater usually tells us which one you have, and we replace the full system so the home is easier to insure and to sell.

    Urgent Repipes After Repeated Failures in Fredericksburg, VA

    A widespread pipe failure sometimes forces a repipe sooner than you planned. We make the house safe and usable first, with temporary repairs that get you water, then schedule the full repipe as soon as the crew and permit allow. We move fast. Most emergency repipes still finish within the normal two to four day window once work starts.

    Whole-Home Copper Repiping in Fredericksburg

    Copper is the traditional choice, and Type L copper has a thicker wall than the Type M that went into many older homes. It holds up well, does not mind sunlight in an exposed run and has a long track record behind it. It costs more. Our plumbers solder joints with lead-free solder or use press fittings, support the pipe at the spacing the code calls for and pressure test the system before any wall is closed.

    PEX Versus Copper Repipe Pricing in Fredericksburg, VA

    PEX repipes usually cost less than copper because the material is cheaper and installation goes faster. Copper costs more. Copper commonly costs 20 to 40 percent more than PEX, and some owners prefer it anyway for its long history. Our quotes for either include permit, fittings, patching and cleanup.

    Slab Leak Repiping in Fredericksburg

    A slab leak shows up as a warm spot on the floor, a running meter or a wet patch of carpet with no visible cause. Basements are common in northern and western parts of Virginia, while crawlspaces and slabs are more typical in the east and south. Instead of breaking concrete to patch one pipe, we reroute new supply lines overhead through the attic and down interior walls, then abandon the old lines in the slab. No jackhammer needed. That stops the current leak and removes the chance of the next one under the same floor.

    Commercial Repipe Services in Fredericksburg

    Older commercial buildings in Fredericksburg often have galvanized risers, copper that has pinholed or plastic pipe that no longer meets code. Our crews swap out risers, branches and drains with as little interruption to tenants as the building allows. Shutoffs get scheduled ahead. We plan the work by zone and handle keys, access and quiet hours with your manager.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Fredericksburg

    When one line under the slab leaks, a full repipe is not always the first step. Rerouting one branch overhead and leaving the bad pipe in the slab is often cheaper than both a repipe and a jackhammer repair. This works well for a first leak. If the house has had several leaks, we will walk through why a full repipe usually costs less over time.
