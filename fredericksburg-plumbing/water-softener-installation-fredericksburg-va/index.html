    Combination Softener and Carbon Filter Systems in Fredericksburg, VA

    City water often carries chlorine or chloramine that affects taste and smell. A carbon filter placed ahead of the softener removes those chemicals and protects the resin, since chlorine slowly breaks resin beads down over the years. You will taste it. Setups can be two tanks or one dual-stage tank.

    Water Hardness Testing and Programming in Fredericksburg

    Testing is what makes the settings right. Our crew checks hardness, iron and chlorine, then sets the regeneration and salt dosage to match. Iron adds to the setting. You get a walkthrough of the settings.

    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Fredericksburg

    Reverse osmosis pushes water through a fine membrane that removes most dissolved solids, including the sodium a softener adds. We install RO units under the kitchen sink with a dedicated faucet, and many can also feed a refrigerator water line or an ice maker. It complements a softener. We can handle filter changes.

    Softener Loop Installation in Fredericksburg

    No loop? A softener loop is a pair of capped stubs on the main line, usually in the garage, that lets a softener be connected without cutting pipe later. We cut it in for you. Outdoor taps can stay on hard water, which saves salt. Future service gets easier too.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Installation in Fredericksburg

    Most Fredericksburg, VA softener installs land between $1,500 and $4,000. The price usually includes the equipment and labor, with extra plumbing shown as its own line. Capacity affects price. We give written pricing up front.

    Standard residential softener installs often need no separate permit. Major main line or drain changes can need one, and we let you know. Permits are on us.

    Control valves usually carry about 5 years, and tanks often 10 or more. We also warranty our installation work.

    Softeners remove hardness minerals using salt. Conditioners alter the minerals instead, and softeners are stronger for very hard water.

    On an existing loop, installs usually take two to three hours. Adding a new loop can take half a day or more.

    Salt use depends on hardness, water use and how efficient the softener is. On average a household of four goes through 40 to 80 pounds a month, less with a metered unit. Metered valves save most.

    Some homeowners try it, but we recommend a plumber. Cutting into the main, setting the bypass, running the drain to an air gap and programming the valve all have to be right, or you get leaks and poor softening. It also protects the warranty.

    Sized and installed correctly, a softener should not noticeably drop your pressure. If pressure falls after the install, it usually points to an undersized valve or a clogged pre-filter, which our plumbers can fix.
