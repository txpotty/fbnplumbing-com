    Softener Systems We Replace in Fredericksburg, VA

    Replacing Fleck Water Softeners in Fredericksburg, VA

    Fleck control valves, the 5600SXT and 9100SXT in particular, are on a large share of the softeners we see in Virginia homes. An old Fleck with discontinued parts usually gets replaced, valve and tank together. Current Fleck valves fit standard tanks.

    Brine Tank Replacement in Fredericksburg

    Salt bridges, crusty layers of salt that form over a hollow, stop the brine from making contact with the water and the softener quits softening. We break up the crust and scoop out the mushy salt underneath. We replace cracked tanks and bad safety floats. Pellets bridge less than rock salt.

    Metered Softener Replacement in Fredericksburg

    Timer softeners regenerate every few days whether you used 50 gallons or 500. A metered valve, Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1, tracks gallons and regenerates when the resin actually needs it. Salt bills usually drop in the first month.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Replacement in Fredericksburg, VA

    Salt-free conditioners, sometimes sold as descalers, change how hardness minerals behave without removing them, so the water still tests hard. They do keep scale from sticking inside pipes and water heaters. They need no drain line. We can replace one or switch you to a true softener.

    Salt-Based Softener Swaps in Fredericksburg, VA

    Swapping a salt-based softener in Virginia homes means disconnecting the old resin and brine tanks, cutting in new fittings if the loop is corroded, and setting the new valve. We pressurize and check every joint. The drain line gets an air gap.

    Dual Tank Softener Systems in Fredericksburg

    Twin-tank systems keep one tank in service while the other regenerates, so you never get hard water during a middle-of-the-night regeneration. Kinetico runs them without electricity, using water flow to drive the valve. The price is higher.

    Commercial Softener Replacement in Fredericksburg

    Espresso machines and commercial dishwashers scale up fast on hard water, and the service bills for descaling them are steep. We size commercial softeners to flow rate and daily gallons. A carbon prefilter may go first.

    Softener Loop Installation in Fredericksburg, VA

    Softener loops are the pair of stubbed-out lines near the garage wall or water heater where a softener ties into the main. Homes without one get a loop cut in, with shutoffs and a bypass. Hose bibs stay off it. We run the drain to a standpipe or floor drain through an air gap.
