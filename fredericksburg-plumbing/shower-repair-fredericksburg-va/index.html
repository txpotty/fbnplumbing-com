    Shower Repairs We Handle in Fredericksburg, VA

    Leaking Shower Cartridge Repair in Fredericksburg

    Cartridges wear from the inside. Mineral grit scores the sealing faces and flattens the O-rings until the cartridge can no longer stop the flow. A new cartridge fixes it. We use genuine parts and coat the new one with silicone grease so the handle turns smoothly.

    Showerhead Repair and Replacement in Fredericksburg

    Showerheads clog from the inside out. Scale builds behind the face plate and in the flow restrictor, so the spray goes uneven, shoots sideways or drops to a trickle even when the valve is wide open. A vinegar soak can work. When it does not, we install a new head with soft rubber nozzles that you can wipe clean with a thumb.

    Leaky Shower Valve Repair in Fredericksburg

    One drip every few seconds adds up to hundreds of gallons a year. Single-handle showers drip from a worn cartridge or O-rings, while old two-handle valves drip from a washer and a pitted seat. The parts are on the truck. Most valves we open take a Moen, Delta, Kohler or Pfister cartridge, which means a same-visit fix.

    Mixing Valve Repair in Fredericksburg

    Mixing valves blend hot and cold inside the wall. Over time the cartridge or spool sticks, and you get a shower that will not stay at one temperature or a handle that turns past where it should before the water warms up. A new cartridge usually fixes it. Valves with no parts left get a replacement plan and an honest look at the tile.

    Clogged Shower Drain Clearing in Fredericksburg, VA

    Hair and soap scum cause nearly every slow shower drain. A hand auger through the strainer clears most of them, and a drum machine handles clogs that have moved past the trap. Drain cleaner usually misses the clog. A hair catcher over the strainer keeps the next one from forming.

    Shower Diverter Repair in Fredericksburg, VA

    Diverters send water up to the head instead of out the tub spout. A worn spout gate or a tired diverter stem lets water keep running from the spout, and the head pressure drops off. Usually a new spout fixes it. It is a short job.

    Weak Shower Spray Repair in Fredericksburg, VA

    Most weak showers come down to the head. Scale packs the nozzles and the restrictor screen behind them, and the spray drops off a little at a time until one day it feels like a garden mister. A soak can bring it back. If the cartridge is clogged too, we replace it and test the flow again.

    Emergency Shower Leak Repair in Fredericksburg

    Water coming through a ceiling below a bathroom needs fast action. Shut the main valve, catch the drip in a bucket and keep people out from under any sagging drywall, since a soaked ceiling can let go without warning. Then call us. We find the source, stop it and dry out what we can that day.
