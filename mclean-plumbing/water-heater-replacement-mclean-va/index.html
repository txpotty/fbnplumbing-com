    Hybrid Water Heater Installation in McLean, VA

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters pull heat from the air around them, using a fraction of the electricity a standard electric tank uses. They need space and airflow, often a room of about 700 cubic feet or a louvered door, plus a condensate drain, and they work best in a garage or basement that stays above freezing. They also cool the room.

    Emergency Water Heater Replacement in McLean, VA

    A leaking tank can empty itself onto the floor faster than most people expect.     Electric Water Heater Replacement in McLean

    Electric water heaters in McLean usually fail from a burned-out heating element, a bad thermostat or a tank that has finally rusted through at the bottom. Elements are cheap to swap. A leaking tank cannot, so if water is coming from the tank body itself, we replace the heater and match the voltage, wattage and breaker size to what your panel already supplies. Most swaps take a few hours.

    Upsizing Your Water Heater in McLean

    Running out of hot water usually means the house outgrew the tank. A bigger tank or a higher first-hour rating handles more back-to-back showers, and sometimes a 50-gallon high-recovery unit matches a 75 in the same footprint. Recovery rate counts too.

    Tankless Water Heater Upgrades in McLean

    Going tankless when the old tank fails is a popular move, but it is a bigger job than a tank swap. Since tankless can pull four times the gas of a tank, the meter line usually gets bigger, and the old vent is swapped for stainless or PVC. We price both options.
