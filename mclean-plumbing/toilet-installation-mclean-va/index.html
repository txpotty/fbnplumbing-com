    Floor-Mount and Wall-Hung Toilet Installs in McLean, VA

    Standard Toilet Installation in McLean

    A standard floor-mount toilet is a two-hour job when the flange is sound. The stop gets shut, the tank drained, and the old bowl lifted out. Once the old wax is scraped off, the flange gets checked for cracks, new stainless bolts go in, and the new bowl is lowered straight onto a fresh seal. The old one leaves with us.

    Wall-Hung Toilet Installation in McLean, VA

    Wall-hung toilets need a wall at least 2 by 6 deep, or a furred-out chase built in front of an existing 2 by 4 wall. The floor underneath stays open. That makes mopping easier and lets you set the bowl height anywhere within the carrier's range, which is handy for tall households or anyone who wants a comfort-height seat without a tall bowl. Service happens through the flush plate.

    Wall-Hung Carrier Systems in McLean

    In-wall carriers from Geberit and Grohe each mount a little differently, and the instructions matter. We follow the maker's frame height chart exactly. Set the frame too low and the bowl ends up at child height, too high and nobody's feet touch the floor, and neither can be fixed once the wall is tiled. Measure twice here.

    ADA-Compliant Toilet Installation in McLean

    ADA toilets put the seat 17 to 19 inches off the floor, and the centerline sits 16 to 18 inches from the side wall. It is set that way so a grab bar fits. Before anything is ordered we measure the drain, because shifting it to meet the ADA centerline means cutting into the floor. Elongated bowls are standard here.

    Commercial Flushometer Toilets in McLean

    Sloan and Zurn flushometer valves are the standard on commercial toilets for a reason. They recover instantly between flushes. We install manual and sensor-operated valves on floor-mount and wall-hung bowls, and we set the supply stop so the valve delivers its full rated volume without splashing or running on. Sensor valves cut down on touch points.

    Smart Toilet Installation in McLean, VA

    Smart toilets with heated seats and built-in bidets need two things the old toilet did not: power and a dedicated water connection. Most need a GFCI outlet close enough for the cord. The water connection and mounting are ours, and when an outlet has to be added we schedule a licensed electrician instead of doing that wiring. Then we test every button.

    Comfort-Height and ADA Toilets in McLean

    Aging in place usually starts in the bathroom. A taller toilet and a grab bar go a long way. While the wall is open, we add 2 by 6 blocking between the studs at grab bar height, so the bar can later be screwed into solid wood instead of drywall anchors that pull loose. We also center the bowl to ADA spacing.

Plastic flanges crack from overtightened bolts, and a stainless repair ring screwed over the old one usually gives a solid anchor again. Seal goes on last.
