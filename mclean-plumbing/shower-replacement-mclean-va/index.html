    Frequently Asked Questions About Shower Replacement in McLean

    Shower valve replacement usually costs $450 to $1,200. A new pan is usually $1,500 to $4,000, while a full tiled rebuild can reach $8,000 to $20,000. Valve swaps cost the least.

    Trim and showerheads are DIY-friendly. Valve, pan and drain replacements are hidden work where mistakes cause leaks, so a licensed plumber is the better choice.

    Staining and cracks on the pan or walls are early signs. Fixtures that drip constantly, mold that will not clean off and poor pressure from old plumbing all suggest replacement over repair, as does a need for better accessibility. We check before recommending.

    For most homes, Moen or Delta valves are hard to beat. For the unit or the base, Sterling and American Standard make durable acrylic and fiberglass options, and Kohler makes plenty of both valves and bases. We fit it to your budget.

    Watch for leaks that return after repairs. Cracked pans or units, soft floors near the shower, mold that keeps coming back and old valves without scald protection are the other signs.

    Replacement keeps the footprint. A replacement stays in place, and a remodel can move walls and change the layout. It is the smaller job.

    New valves and fixtures carry manufacturer warranties, often lifetime for the original owner. Our workmanship is guaranteed on every job.

    Valve and pan replacements may need one, depending on the city and scope. We check requirements and handle permits as needed.
