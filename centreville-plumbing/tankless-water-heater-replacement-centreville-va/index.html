    Electric Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Centreville, VA

    Electric tankless water heaters skip the gas line entirely, but a whole-house model can need 120 to 160 amps on two to four double-pole breakers. Many panels cannot spare that. We check panel capacity and wire gauge before quoting.

    Commercial Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Centreville, VA

    Commercial tankless setups in restaurants and offices often use two or more units on a manifold, with a controller that splits the load. One bad unit can come out while the others keep running. Hand sinks and dish stations have to hit the temperatures health code requires.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Centreville

    A gas tankless unit at full fire can pull 199,000 BTU, roughly five times what a 40 gallon tank burner uses. The gas line usually has to be 3/4-inch or larger. We check meter capacity and pipe length against the manufacturer's sizing chart, then confirm pressure with the unit running. Starved units throw error codes.

    Commercial Tankless Replacement in Centreville

    Cascade controls on a commercial rack decide which unit fires first, and after one unit is replaced they need rebalancing. Skip that and the new unit wears out first. We also set a descaling schedule.

    Leaking Tankless Replacement in Centreville

    A leaking tankless can drip inside the cabinet for weeks before anyone notices the puddle. Shut the gas and cold isolation valve now. We then find out whether it is a fitting or the heat exchanger, since one is cheap and the other is replacement.

    Tank to Tankless Conversion in Centreville, VA

    Converting from a tank to tankless takes a full day in most homes. The work usually includes a larger gas line, new venting through a wall or roof, and a new spot on the wall for the unit. We cap or remove the old flue.

    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Centreville, VA

    Exterior-mount tankless units hang on an outside wall and vent straight out the front, so there is no vent pipe to run. Covers and wiring take a beating outdoors. Disconnecting hoses, shutting off outdoor spigots and keeping crawlspace vents closed in winter are easy ways to prevent freeze damage. On every outdoor swap, we check freeze protection and the pipe cover. Often the simplest job we do.

    Gas Line Testing on Tankless Installs in Centreville, VA

    Every gas connection on a tankless gets a soap test and a manometer reading at full fire before we leave the house. Static pressure means little if it drops when the burner opens up. Both readings get taken.
