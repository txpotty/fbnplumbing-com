    Tub and Shower Combo Repair in Centreville, VA

    Tub and shower combos share one valve, one drain and a diverter between them. That means a worn diverter steals pressure from the head, a clogged tub drain slows the shower and a leaky valve shows up at both the spout and the head. Each part gets tested. We also check the overflow gasket, a frequent hidden leak.

    Weak Shower Spray Repair in Centreville, VA

    Most weak showers come down to the head. Minerals fill the nozzles and the screen behind them, and the spray fades so gradually you barely notice until it is a mist. Cleaning often works. If the cartridge is clogged too, we replace it and test the flow again.

    Commercial Shower Repair in Centreville, VA

    Gyms and apartment buildings run showers dozens of times a day. We repair commercial pressure-balance and thermostatic valves with heavy-duty cartridges, and we can work early or late so a locker room or a unit is not out of service during peak hours. We stock tamper-resistant heads. Limit stops are set to code on each valve.

    Mixing Valve Repair in Centreville

    Mixing valves blend hot and cold inside the wall. A sticking cartridge or spool shows up as temperature that drifts or a handle that has to go much farther than it used to. A new cartridge usually fixes it. Valves with no parts left get a replacement plan and an honest look at the tile.

    Commercial Shower Service in Centreville

    Hotels and dorms cannot wait days on a shower. With commercial cartridges on board, we fix rooms one at a time so the rest stay open. We can work nights. Each repaired valve gets its limit stop set and tested before we move on.

    Moen and Delta Shower Repair in Centreville, VA

    Moen 1222 and Delta RP46074 cartridges sit inside a huge share of the showers we open. The Moen 1222 runs the Posi-Temp line, and the Delta RP46074 runs the Monitor 13 and 14 series, and both wear out on a schedule you can almost set a calendar by. Both ride on our trucks. Many of these carry a lifetime warranty to the original owner.

    Hot Water Pressure Problems in Centreville, VA

    Weak hot water with strong cold usually rules out the showerhead. The problem is on the hot side, either sediment in the water heater outlet, a partly closed valve at the heater or a cartridge whose hot port is packed with scale. Each side gets tested. Good hot flow at the sinks points us straight at the shower cartridge.

    Leaky Shower Valve Repair in Centreville

    One drip every few seconds adds up to hundreds of gallons a year. In a single-handle shower the cause is almost always the cartridge or its O-rings, and on an old two-handle valve it is the stem washer and the seat. We carry the common parts. Most valves we open take a Moen, Delta, Kohler or Pfister cartridge, which means a same-visit fix.
