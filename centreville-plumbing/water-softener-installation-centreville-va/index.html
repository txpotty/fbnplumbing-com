    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Centreville

    On a whole-house system, the softener goes on the main line after the shutoff and before the water heater. We add a bypass valve so you can isolate the softener for service, and we install a pressure regulator if your street pressure runs above 80 psi. Every tap gets soft water. Outdoor taps can be left off.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Centreville

    Salt-free conditioners do not soften water, and we say so up front. They change the minerals into crystals that do not stick to pipe walls and heating elements, so scale drops off without adding sodium or using a drain line. Maintenance is light. They suit moderate hardness.

    Combination Softener and Carbon Filter Systems in Centreville, VA

    City water often carries chlorine or chloramine that affects taste and smell. Putting a carbon tank in front of the softener takes out chlorine and keeps it from wearing down the resin. You will taste it. Setups can be two tanks or one dual-stage tank.

    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Centreville

    Reverse osmosis pushes water through a fine membrane that removes most dissolved solids, including the sodium a softener adds. We install RO units under the kitchen sink with a dedicated faucet, and many can also feed a refrigerator water line or an ice maker. It complements a softener. We can handle filter changes.

    Water Softener Replacement & Upgrades in Centreville, VA

    Upgrading an old timer-based softener is one of the easiest ways to cut salt use. Newer valves meter actual water use, some connect to Wi-Fi for salt alerts and higher-capacity tanks regenerate less often for larger households. The savings add up. We swap it out in a few hours.

    Fleck Water Softener Installation in Centreville, VA

    Fleck valves, especially the 5600SXT and 7000SXT, are on a lot of the softeners we install. Their parts are easy to find and the valves can be rebuilt instead of replaced, so the system lasts. Settings match your test. We confirm the setup before leaving.
