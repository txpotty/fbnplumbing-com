    Zoeller Sump Pump Installation in Virginia Beach, VA

    Zoeller has built cast iron sump pumps for decades, and parts are easy to get. The M53 and the M98 are the two we set most, depending on lift and inflow, and both come with mechanical float switches that hold up for years. Backup and combo units are available too.     Emergency Sump Pump Replacement in Virginia Beach, VA

    A dead pump during a storm means the water is already climbing. Our trucks carry 1/3 and 1/2 HP submersibles, check valves and fittings, so in most homes we can pull the old pump and set a new one in one visit. Most swaps take about two hours. We test it with a full pit before leaving.

    Liberty Pumps Installation in Virginia Beach, VA

    Liberty builds quiet, well-made submersible pumps with good switch options. Their 250 and 280 series cover most residential lifts, and the vertical float version fits narrow pits where a wide tethered float would rub. Replacement switches are easy to get. A small weep hole in the discharge pipe keeps the pump from air locking.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Virginia Beach, VA

    Backup systems we install use sealed AGM batteries that do not need water. The charger keeps the battery topped off. Batteries need replacing every few years. Finished basements get the most out of them.

    Sump Pump Replacement in Virginia Beach, VA

    Floats stick and motors seize when a pump sits idle for months between storms. When we replace a pump we also check the basin for silt, look at the check valve flapper and run water through the discharge line to find any clogs. Undersized pumps get upsized. Testing is done with a full pit.

    Primary Sump Pump Installation in Virginia Beach

    A primary sump pump is the one doing the daily work, kicking on every time the float rises in the pit after rain or snowmelt. We size it from pit diameter and vertical lift. Most homes need 1/3 or 1/2 HP. A 1-1/2 inch discharge line handles the flow from either size without straining the motor.

    Combination Sump Pump System Installation in Virginia Beach, VA

    Two pumps on one riser cover both kinds of failure, a dead motor and a dead outlet. The primary runs on house power and handles the everyday inflow, while the battery unit waits above it on a separate float. People with carpet downstairs buy these. They are worth it anywhere a few inches of water would mean new drywall.

    Crawl Space Sump Pump Installation in Virginia Beach, VA

    Crawl spaces with standing water breed mold and rot sill plates within a few seasons. A small sump basin at the low point fixes that. Clearance is usually under two feet. The discharge exits through the rim joist and drains away from the house.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Installation in Virginia Beach

    A pump swap in an existing pit typically costs $600 to $1,500. New pits, with saw cutting, excavation and a concrete patch, run about $1,500 to $4,000. Add $500 to $1,500 for a backup.

    Manufacturer warranties usually run three to five years, longer on premium models. We also back our workmanship.

    A submersible sits in the pit under water, so it runs quieter and takes no floor space. The pedestal type mounts the motor on a column above the water, which makes it easier to reach for service and usually cheaper to buy, though you will hear it. Either can do the job. We help you pick based on your basement.

    Submersibles are quieter and stronger, so they suit heavy use. A pedestal pump costs less and you can reach the motor without lifting it out, but it makes more noise and moves less water.

    Discharge should end at least ten feet from the foundation, on ground that slopes away. Most cities do not allow sump water in the sanitary sewer.

    We install Zoeller, Wayne, Liberty and a few other dependable brands. We recommend a model after we see the pit and how fast water comes in.

    Permits depend on the scope of the job and the local building department. Virginia uses a uniform statewide building code that incorporates the International Plumbing Code, enforced by local building officials. FBN Plumbing pulls any permit the job needs in Virginia Beach.
