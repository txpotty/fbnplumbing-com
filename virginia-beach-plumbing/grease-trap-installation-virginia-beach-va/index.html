    Grease Trap Sizing and Placement in Virginia Beach, VA

    Sizing starts with fixture drain rates and ends with where the trap can physically go. A trap 40 feet from the sink lets grease cool and stick in the pipe before it ever reaches the unit. Closer is better. For outdoor tanks we measure the hose run from where a pump truck can park.

    Commercial Kitchen Grease Trap Installs in Virginia Beach

    Every commercial install starts with a fixture list. The tech walks the kitchen and writes down every fixture that drains to the trap and how fast each one empties.     Hydro-Mechanical Grease Interceptor Installation in Virginia Beach

    Inside a hydromechanical interceptor, baffles do the separating while a flow control fitting with an air intake holds incoming water to the rated flow. The air intake has to be vented to code, not left open in the kitchen. Get that wrong and the trap siphons. A trap that sits out of level loses capacity, so we shim it flat before connecting anything.
