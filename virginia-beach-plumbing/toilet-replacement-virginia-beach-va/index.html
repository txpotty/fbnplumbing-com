    Replacing Pressure-Assisted Toilets in Virginia Beach, VA

    Frequently Asked Questions About Toilet Replacement in Virginia Beach

    A standard toilet replacement usually runs $400 to $800 with the new toilet and labor included, depending mostly on the model you choose. Flange or subfloor repairs add to it. We quote those separately before doing them, so the base number does not move.

    About two hours for a standard swap. In that time we remove the old toilet, inspect the flange, set the new one and test it, with the old fixture loaded on the truck at the end. Flange repairs add time.

    Rough-in is the distance from the finished wall behind the toilet to the center of the drain, which you can find by measuring to the closet bolts. Twelve inches is standard. Older homes sometimes have 10 or 14 inch rough-ins, and a toilet made for the wrong one either sticks out or will not fit.

    Replacement wins when repair costs start to approach the price of a new toilet, or when the toilet is old enough to waste water every time it flushes. Cracks settle it. Frequent clogs and a rocking base point the same way.

    Even moderate hardness in Virginia Beach can leave scale in water heaters over time, so periodic flushing helps them run longer and more efficiently. Hard water leaves calcium scale in the rim jets and on the flush valve seat, which slowly weakens the flush and makes flappers leak. A fully glazed trapway resists scale better. Our picks for hard water have glazed trapways and fill valves with fewer small ports.

    It is doable for handy homeowners, but old toilets are heavy and flange problems are common once the bowl comes off. A pro install avoids cracked china and slow leaks.

    A handle that needs jiggling, a tank that refills on its own, a hiss after the flush or a flush that stops halfway all point to worn tank parts. Those can be replaced one at a time. On a toilet past 25 years, replacing the whole fixture often makes more sense.

    Plan on two to four hours if the flange or subfloor needs work, and closer to two for a clean swap with a solid flange. The bathroom is back in service the same day. Testing happens before we pack up.
