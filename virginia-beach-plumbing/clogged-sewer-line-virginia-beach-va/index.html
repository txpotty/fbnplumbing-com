    Tree Root Sewer Clogs in Virginia Beach

    Clay and older cast iron sewer pipe have a joint every few feet. Each joint is an opening. Roots from trees and shrubs push into the gaps and fill the pipe, then we cut them out and camera the line for damage. Ongoing root problems may call for a treatment schedule or lining.

    Sewer Belly and Sagging Pipe Clogs in Virginia Beach, VA

    Expansive clays in some areas expand when wet and shrink when dry, which can crack foundations and shift buried sewer lines. A section of sewer pipe that sinks forms a belly. Waste settles in the low spot. Over time that standing water collects paper and grease, and the line clogs at the same spot every few months no matter how often it gets cleaned. Our camera and locator show where the belly is and how deep it sits. A spot repair restores the slope.

    Main Line Hydro Jetting in Virginia Beach

    A jetter cleans the full diameter of the pipe, not just a hole through the middle. That is the difference. Water at thousands of psi cuts roots, grease and scale, and the rear-facing spray carries all of it downstream as the nozzle pulls itself along the line. We camera the pipe first so jetting only goes into lines that can take it. Results often last years.

    Preventative Sewer Line Maintenance in Virginia Beach

    Lines that clog from roots once a year can be cleaned before they clog. That beats a 2 a.m. backup. FBN Plumbing sets up scheduled cutting or jetting for homes in Virginia Beach with older clay or cast iron, usually every 12 to 24 months depending on how fast the roots come back.     Hydro-Jetting Services in Virginia Beach, VA

    Jetting sends water at thousands of psi through a nozzle that sprays backward and drives itself down the pipe. The pipe gets cleaned all the way around. A cable punches a hole through a grease plug, and a jetter clears the coating that starts the next clog. The camera goes in before the jetter. Badly cracked pipe gets a gentler approach.
