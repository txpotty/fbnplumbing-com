    Picking a Tankless for Your Virginia Beach, VA House

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Virginia Beach

    A gas tankless unit at full fire can pull 199,000 BTU, roughly five times what a 40 gallon tank burner uses. The gas line usually has to be 3/4-inch or larger. We check meter capacity and pipe length against the manufacturer's sizing chart, then confirm pressure with the unit running. Starved units throw error codes.

    Condensing Tankless Replacement in Virginia Beach

    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE or Rinnai Sensei pass 90 percent efficiency by pulling extra heat out of the exhaust. They vent through PVC. The condensate is acidic, so we install a neutralizer cartridge and run the line to an approved drain. Summer humidity leads to condensation on cold water lines and in crawlspaces, which can speed up corrosion and invite mold. Showers and laundry run all year, so the gas savings do too.

    Tankless-to-Tankless Replacement in Virginia Beach

    Replacing an existing tankless with a new one is usually the simpler job, since the gas line, vent path and water connections are already there. We still check sizing against the new model. Corroded service valves get swapped while we are there.

    Point-of-Use Electric Tankless in Virginia Beach

    Point-of-use electric tankless heaters under a sink are a different animal from whole-house units. They run on a single 20 or 30 amp circuit and work well for a far bathroom or an office sink. Swaps take an hour or two.

    Emergency Tankless Replacement in Virginia Beach, VA

    A tankless that will not ignite or leaks from the heat exchanger leaves you with no hot water at all. Call (434) 230-6997 any time it quits. We stock common models for same-day replacement. A stopgap repair can sometimes bridge the gap until the right unit arrives.

    Gas Line Testing on Tankless Installs in Virginia Beach, VA

    Every gas connection on a tankless gets a soap test and a manometer reading at full fire before we leave the house. A line that holds pressure at rest can still sag under full load. We test both.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Virginia Beach, VA

    Electric tankless water heaters skip the gas line entirely, but a whole-house model can need 120 to 160 amps on two to four double-pole breakers. Many panels cannot spare that. Panel size and wire gauge get checked first.
