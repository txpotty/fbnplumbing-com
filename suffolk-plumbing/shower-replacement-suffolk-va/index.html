    Shower Valve Replacement in Suffolk

    Old valves waste water and swing temperature.     Leaking Shower Pan Replacement in Suffolk, VA

    Leaking pans soak the subfloor and the ceiling below. Prefab or tiled, the failed pan comes out, the subfloor gets repaired and the new pan is flood-tested for 24 hours. Drains get replaced too. It ends the cycle.

    Shower Pan Replacement in Suffolk, VA

    Cracked pans damage subfloors, walls and nearby rooms. With slab floors, water can move under flooring into the next room, so we trace it first. Then we remove the pan. We install the new pan with slope and waterproofing, then flood-test it.

    Fiberglass Shower Unit Replacement in Suffolk

    Narrow doorways change which unit we can use. A one-piece fiberglass shower often will not fit through a standard bathroom door, so we usually install a multi-piece unit that goes in as panels and seals at the seams. Both last a long time. We check the framing and drain before setting it.

    Emergency Shower Leak Solutions in Suffolk, VA

    Full replacement is rarely an emergency, but a major leak can be.     Walk-In Shower Conversion in Suffolk, VA

    Walk-in conversions are one of the most requested bathroom jobs we do.     Old Shower Valve Replacement in Suffolk

    Two- and three-handle valves from decades back often leak and lack scald protection. Tile often stays put. Safety improves a lot.
