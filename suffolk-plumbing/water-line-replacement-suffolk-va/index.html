    Polybutylene Water Line Replacement in Suffolk, VA

    Polybutylene is the gray or blue plastic pipe installed from roughly the late 1970s to the mid 1990s. It degrades from the inside when exposed to chlorinated water. The failures tend to be sudden splits rather than slow drips, and some home insurers refuse to cover homes that still have it. Worth replacing.

    Main Water Line Repair vs. Replacement in Suffolk

    One leak on a twenty-year-old copper line? We will usually just fix that spot. Three leaks on a galvanized line from the 1950s is different, because the pipe is thin everywhere and the next leak is already forming, so the full line costs less than a string of repairs. We tell you which camp you are in and show you the pipe we cut out.

    Damage Assessment Before Replacement in Suffolk

    We start at the meter. With every tap closed, the leak indicator should sit still. From there we listen along the line with an acoustic ground mic, rule out a running toilet or water softener, and sometimes push a camera into the line when we suspect root damage or a crushed section, so the hole goes in the right spot.

    Trenchless Bores Under Driveways in Suffolk

    Directional boring drills a path under the obstacle and pulls the pipe back through it. It does not need the old pipe as a guide. We use it when the new line has to take a different route, say around a new addition or under a driveway poured after the house was built, leaving just two small pits in the yard. Heavy rock is the exception.

    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Upgrades in Suffolk, VA

    Street pressure varies a lot from one neighborhood to the next. Plumbing code caps house pressure at 80 psi. If your new line comes in above that, we add a pressure reducing valve right after the main shutoff, set it around 55 to 65 psi, and swap the old gate valve for a full-port ball valve. A quarter turn and the water is off.

    Traditional Water Line Excavation in Suffolk

    Sometimes the open trench wins. A collapsed line, a route with sharp bends, or solid rock can rule out trenchless. We cut the sod and set it aside, dig to depth with a mini excavator or by hand near other utilities, set the pipe on clean bedding, and backfill in layers with compaction so the trench does not sink after the first good rain. Sod goes back on top.

    Commercial Water Service Replacement in Suffolk

    Strip centers, churches and small apartment buildings each come with their own wrinkles. A single meter may feed six or eight units. We map where each branch goes before cutting anything, bring the utility in for the meter side, and handle the permit, backflow test and inspection so the owner gets one point of contact. Paperwork included.

    PEX Water Line Replacement in Suffolk

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster, which matters on a long run. Scale and corrosion are not a problem with it. We use PEX-a or PEX rated for direct burial, use only fittings listed for burial, and lay tracer wire on top so a locator can find it later, since plastic pipe does not show up on a metal detector. Cheap insurance.
