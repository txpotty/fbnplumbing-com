    Control Valve Diagnostics and Repair in Suffolk

    Leaks at the valve body, water running to drain around the clock, a motor that hums without moving or a display stuck on one cycle tend to mean the seal and spacer stack is worn out.     Brine Tank Cleanout and Inspection in Suffolk, VA

    Brine tanks collect a gray sludge of salt fines and insoluble grit, and it builds up faster with cheap rock salt. We empty, scrub and rinse the tank. Then we look for cracks around the brine well and overflow fitting and refill with fresh salt.

    Commercial Twin-Tank Maintenance in Suffolk

    Salons, car washes, hotels and restaurants put far more gallons through a softener than any house does, and a hard-water day can mean spotted glassware or a scaled ice machine. Twin-tank and progressive-flow systems need closer checks on the meter and the tank changeover. Visits run monthly or quarterly, depending on volume.

    Softener Upkeep for Restaurants and Shops in Suffolk

    Ice machines, espresso machines, dish machines and boilers all scale up fast on hard water, and equipment warranties often require treated water. Each visit includes a hardness test, logged for your warranty file. Brine tank cleanouts happen more often on commercial units.

    Scale coming back is the giveaway. Dull laundry and dry skin after a shower point the same way. Higher salt use, a brine tank full of water, a grinding noise from the valve head or lower pressure at the faucets can also mean the softener needs service.
