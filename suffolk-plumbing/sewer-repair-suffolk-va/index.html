    Sewer Pipe Relining in Suffolk, VA

    Relining builds a new pipe inside the old one. We clean the line with a jetter first. A flexible liner saturated with epoxy is pulled or inverted into place, inflated against the old wall and left to cure, which seals cracks and root entry points in a single continuous tube. The finished pipe loses a little diameter. On a four-inch residential line, that rarely matters.

    Tree Root Invasion Solutions in Suffolk

    Roots in a sewer line are not the tree's fault. The pipe was leaking first. Warm, wet, nutrient-rich air escaping from a loose joint draws roots toward the line, and once they get in they thicken until they crack the pipe or separate the joint completely. We cut them out, then talk about the long-term fix. Lining or a joint replacement keeps them from coming back.

    Pipe Burst & Collapse Repair in Suffolk, VA

    A collapsed sewer pipe stops all flow at the point of failure. Every drain backs up. Collapses usually come from corroded cast iron, crushed clay under a driveway or tar-paper pipe that has finally flattened, and the camera tells us exactly where the pipe gave way and how deep it sits. We dig at the mark and replace the broken length with PVC. Lining cannot fix a full collapse.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Suffolk, VA

    Trenchless repair means we fix the pipe without opening a trench along its full length. Usually two small pits. Pipe bursting pulls a new HDPE line through the old one and splits the old pipe outward, while cured-in-place lining pushes a resin-soaked felt tube into the old pipe and hardens it into a new wall. Either way, the driveway and the trees stay put. We tell you which one your pipe can take.
