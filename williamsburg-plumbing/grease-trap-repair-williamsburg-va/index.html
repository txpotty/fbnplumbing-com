    Grease Trap Fixes We Handle in Williamsburg, VA

    Damaged Baffle and Lid Repair in Williamsburg, VA

    Lids crack when someone sets a stockpot on them or a dolly rolls across a floor-mounted unit. Once cracked, it leaks smell out and lets mop water into the trap. We replace the lid and gasket with the manufacturer part, and on in-floor units we check that the lid sits flush so nobody catches a toe or a cart wheel on it. Baffle damage often shows up at the same visit.

    Leaking Grease Trap Repair in Williamsburg, VA

    A wet floor around an under-sink trap usually points to a failed lid gasket or a loose inlet coupling. Steel trap bodies also rust through at the bottom seams after enough years of hot, acidic water. We dry the area, fill the trap and watch where the water shows up. Sometimes it is a $15 gasket. If the seam itself has opened, patching rarely holds and we will tell you so before you pay for it.

    Inlet and Outlet Tee Repair in Williamsburg, VA

    Tees are cheap parts with an outsized job. On the inlet side, the tee directs incoming water down under the floating grease. The outlet tee draws water from low in the tank, well under the grease cap, so if it breaks off or someone removes it during pumping, floating grease walks right out to the sewer. We replace broken tees in Schedule 40 PVC and set the drop to the depth the code or the manufacturer calls for.

    Residential Grease Trap Repair in Williamsburg, VA

    Homes rarely need a grease trap, but some have one, usually a small plastic unit under the kitchen sink on a house with a septic system or a home bakery. These leak at the lid or the threaded inlet. We replace the gasket, reset the fittings with fresh thread sealant, and clear any grease plug in the outlet. Small job, usually under an hour.

    Emergency Grease Trap Services in Williamsburg, VA

    Friday night, full dining room, and the floor drain by the dish station starts gurgling up gray water. That is the call we answer after hours. If the right part is not on the truck, we get you running safely tonight and come back for the permanent repair.

    Damaged Baffle Repair in Williamsburg

    Baffles are the walls inside the trap that slow the water down long enough for grease to float and solids to sink. Once one cracks or falls loose, the trap is just a box that grease passes through. We pump it out and pull the old baffle. On plastic units we order the factory part for that model number. Steel ones often need a fabricated baffle or a new trap.

    Hydro-Jetting for Grease Trap Repair in Williamsburg

    A cable machine punches a hole through grease. A jetter scrubs the whole pipe wall instead. We run a commercial jetter at 3,000 to 4,000 PSI through the line feeding the trap and the line leaving it, working from the cleanout toward the main. On old cast iron we lower the pressure and watch the camera so we do not open up a thin spot.

    Lid, Gasket and Riser Repair in Williamsburg, VA

    Outdoor risers take a beating from garbage trucks and the occasional forklift. A cracked riser lets surface water and grit into the interceptor. We replace damaged risers and set traffic-rated covers, H-20 rated where vehicles cross, and seal the riser joints with butyl rope so the tank stays tight. Pedestrian lids in a drive lane are a liability.
