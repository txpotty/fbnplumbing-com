    Backflow Testing and Certification in Williamsburg

    Each backflow test takes about 30 minutes per assembly and requires a short water shutoff to that line. Plan for a brief outage. Our tester opens each test cock in order, records the check valve and relief valve readings on a calibrated gauge and compares them against the pass limits printed on the test form. We tag every passing device. We file the report with your water provider and keep a copy on our side for next year.

    Grease Trap Installation in Williamsburg

    A new restaurant or a kitchen expansion usually needs a grease interceptor sized from the fixtures that drain to it.     Grease Interceptor and Line Service in Williamsburg, VA

    Slab Leak Detection for Businesses in Williamsburg

    A slab leak under a store or office floor often shows up as a warm spot, a running water meter with everything off, or a crack in floor tile. We confirm it with a meter test and pressure test, then use acoustic listening gear and thermal imaging to pinpoint the leak within a foot or two. Then we decide. A spot repair through the floor makes sense for some, and an overhead reroute makes more sense for others. We price both so you can compare.
