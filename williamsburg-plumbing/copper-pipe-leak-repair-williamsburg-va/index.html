    Every Kind of Copper Leak, Repaired in Williamsburg, VA

    Copper Pinhole Leak Repair in Williamsburg

    Pinholes form when the inner wall of copper pipe erodes or pits. Hot water speeds it up. They often appear in thin Type M pipe and on hot water lines where velocity and temperature are higher, which we fix by replacing that length with heavier pipe. More pinholes often follow.

    Commercial Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Williamsburg

    Commercial buildings often run larger diameter copper for domestic water. Water-off time hurts. We repair copper supply lines in offices, restaurants and apartment buildings, and we use press fittings on bigger pipe so the repair goes faster and needs no open flame near occupied spaces. Early or late work is available.

    Corrosion-Related Leak Repair in Williamsburg

    Corrosion thins copper from the inside. Private wells serve many rural Virginia homes, and some well water carries iron, manganese, low pH or sulfur that calls for treatment. Look for blue-green spots. On exposed pipe you may see blue-green deposits at fittings, while hidden pipe usually announces itself with a small leak or a stain. We replace the corroded length. We explain what drove it.

    Copper Slab Leak Repair in Williamsburg

    Many slab homes have copper water lines running under the concrete. Listen for running water. When those lines leak, you may notice warm spots on the floor, the sound of running water or a meter that turns with every fixture off, and we locate the leak electronically before choosing a spot repair through the slab or a reroute. We price each option.

    Preventive Copper Pipe Inspection in Williamsburg, VA

    An inspection catches thin spots before they leak. Plan on an hour. We look at exposed copper for green crust and pitting, test static pressure, check the bond wire at the water heater and main, and run a thermal scan on walls where pipe runs if the house has had leaks before. We write up what we find.

    Underground Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Williamsburg

    Buried copper service lines can leak for months before anyone notices. The meter gives it away. We locate the leak with acoustic gear and tracer gas, and either spot repair it or swap in a new service line with minimal digging. Every repair gets a pressure test.

    Copper Pipe Joint Leak Repair in Williamsburg, VA

    Joints leak from poor original soldering, thermal expansion or a house that settles. Basements are common in northern and western parts of Virginia, while crawlspaces and slabs are more typical in the east and south. Look for white crust. We inspect each joint near the leak for weeping or mineral buildup, then clean, reflux and resolder the joint, or replace the fitting entirely with a new sweat or press fitting. Every joint gets tested.

    Burst Copper Pipe Repair in Williamsburg, VA

    Copper in an exterior wall or attic can split when the water inside freezes. Disconnecting hoses, shutting off outdoor spigots and keeping crawlspace vents closed in winter are simple ways to prevent freeze damage. Shut the main first. The split often does not leak until the ice thaws, so a pipe that froze overnight may start spraying the next afternoon once the house warms up. We cut out the split and solder in new pipe. Insulation goes on after.
