    What Goes Into a PEX Job in Williamsburg, VA

    PEX for Remodels & Additions in Williamsburg, VA

    Opening a wall for a kitchen remodel is the cheapest time to swap out the old pipe. While the studs are exposed we can run PEX back to a manifold or a sound trunk, set fresh stub-outs for the sink and dishwasher, and add a fridge line. An addition gets sized on its own. A new bath at the far end of the house may need a three-quarter inch feed to keep pressure up.

    Manifold and Home-Run PEX Layouts in Williamsburg, VA

    Think of a manifold like an electrical panel, but for water lines. A central header feeds each fixture on its own line with its own valve, which lets you shut off one toilet and leave the kitchen running. It uses more pipe. The trade-off is almost no fittings hidden in the walls and steadier pressure when two showers run at once.

    Full Home PEX Repiping in Williamsburg

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside out. Give it four or five decades and the inside is so packed with rust that a half-inch pipe barely flows, and the first draw in the morning runs brown. A full PEX repipe replaces all of it. You get real pressure back, and the discolored water goes away with the old pipe.

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Williamsburg

    PEX-B is stiffer, is cheaper by the roll, and joins with copper crimp rings or stainless cinch clamps over brass or poly-alloy fittings. Since a crimp is only as good as the tool that made it, every ring gets a go/no-go gauge check, and any ring that fails gets cut off and redone. It is a five-second check. Skipping it is how leaks start.

    Remodel and Addition PEX Rough-In in Williamsburg

    Rough-in heights matter more than people think. A shower valve set two inches too low or a vanity stub that misses the cabinet back means opening the wall again after the tile is up, which is expensive and slow. We confirm heights with your contractor first. The test happens before drywall goes up.

    Whole-Home PEX Repiping in Williamsburg

    Low pressure at every faucet, across the whole house, usually points at the pipe and not the fixtures. In older homes it is rust-choked galvanized or polybutylene with failing fittings, and swapping a faucet cartridge will not help. A whole-home repipe will. We replace every supply line from the main valve to each angle stop and test the system before closing anything.

    Commercial PEX Pipe Installation in Williamsburg

    Commercial PEX work in Williamsburg gets scheduled around your hours. In a small office, a restaurant restroom or a retail space, we can work after hours and shut water down only when nobody is there to need it. Larger pipe sizes use the same fittings. Larger jobs may call for one-inch or bigger trunk lines and commercial-grade manifolds.

    Hydronic Heating with PEX in Williamsburg

    Radiant floor loops are PEX too, though not the same pipe you drink from. Heating loops need oxygen-barrier PEX, which has a coating that keeps oxygen from migrating through the wall and rusting the boiler, the pump and any iron in the system. Loops have length limits. Run them too long and the last stretch never warms up.
