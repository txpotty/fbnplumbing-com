    High-Efficiency Toilet Installation in Williamsburg, VA

    Toilets made before 1994 often use 3.5 gallons a flush, and some from the 1970s use five or more. Savings show up fast. Check with your water provider about rebates.

    Toilet Flange Repair During Replacement in Williamsburg

    Pulling the old toilet is the only chance to see the flange, and a cracked plastic ring or a rusted cast iron flange will ruin a new install within a year. We check it every time. For a cracked flange, a stainless repair ring is often enough, while a flange that sits below the finished floor gets a spacer or extender so the seal compresses properly.

    Toilet Brands We Replace in Williamsburg

    Brand matters less than model, because every major maker sells a great toilet and a mediocre one at nearly the same price. We look at the flush valve size and MaP score. Parts availability counts too. Proprietary flappers and fill valves mean that ten years from now, a simple fix becomes a special order.

    Comfort-Height Toilet Replacement in Williamsburg

    Going from a standard bowl to a comfort-height model raises the seat two or three inches, which matters a lot for seniors and anyone recovering from knee or hip surgery. The drain location stays the same. Pair it with a grab bar if you can, and we can anchor that into a stud during the same visit.

    Water-Saving Toilet Upgrades in Williamsburg, VA

    Older toilets are one of the largest indoor water users in a house, and replacing a 3.5 gallon model with a 1.28 gallon one cuts toilet water use by more than half. Dual-flush models go lower for liquids. We recommend models with high MaP scores so the savings do not come with double flushing.

    Cracked Bowl or Tank Replacement in Williamsburg

    Sometimes only the tank is cracked, and if the model is still in production, a matching tank costs less than a whole new toilet. We check the model number first. A cracked bowl always means a full replacement, since the bowl carries the seal to the floor and cannot be patched.

    Wall-Hung Toilet Replacement in Williamsburg

    Wall-hung toilets mount to a steel carrier inside the wall, so replacing one means checking the carrier bolts, the gasket on the outlet and the condition of the wall finish around it. Some carriers need new bolts. The new bowl gets matched to the carrier already in the wall whenever possible, which avoids opening the wall.
