    Espresso machines and commercial dishwashers scale up fast on hard water, and the service bills for descaling them are steep. We size commercial softeners to flow rate and daily gallons. Sometimes a carbon filter goes ahead of it.

    Salt-Based Softener Swaps in Blacksburg, VA

    Swapping a salt-based softener in Virginia homes means disconnecting the old resin and brine tanks, cutting in new fittings if the loop is corroded, and setting the new valve. We pressurize and check every joint. We set an air gap on the drain line.

    Dual Tank Softener Systems in Blacksburg

    Twin-tank systems keep one tank in service while the other regenerates, so you never get hard water during a middle-of-the-night regeneration. Kinetico's version uses water pressure, not power, to switch tanks. They cost more.

    Replacing Fleck Water Softeners in Blacksburg, VA

    Fleck control valves, the 5600SXT and 9100SXT in particular, are on a large share of the softeners we see in Virginia homes. If the valve is old enough that parts are hard to find, a new valve on a new tank is the smarter buy. Newer Fleck heads drop right in.

    Softener Loop Installation in Blacksburg, VA

    Softener loops are the pair of stubbed-out lines near the garage wall or water heater where a softener ties into the main. Homes without one get a loop cut in, with shutoffs and a bypass. Outdoor spigots stay on hard water. We run the drain to a standpipe or floor drain through an air gap.
