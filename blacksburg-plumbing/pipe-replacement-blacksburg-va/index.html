    Whole-Home Repiping in Blacksburg

    Repiping sounds bigger than it is. Most homes stay livable during the job. We work room by room, restore water most evenings, and keep dust down with plastic sheeting and drop cloths, then patch every opening before we leave. Clean finish.

    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Blacksburg

    Polybutylene is gray or blue plastic installed from roughly the late 1970s to mid 1990s. It degrades from the inside. Failures tend to be sudden splits at fittings rather than slow drips, and some insurers refuse to cover homes that still have it. Replace it.

    Burst Pipe Repair and Replacement in Blacksburg, VA

    Most Virginia winters bring several hard freezes, and cold snaps can burst pipes in crawlspaces, garages and exterior walls. Frozen pipes split when water inside expands. Exposed pipe in crawlspaces, garages and outside walls is most at risk, and when it bursts we replace the damaged section with PEX or copper and add insulation or reroute it away from the cold. Then we test it.

    Galvanized Pipe Removal in Blacksburg, VA

    Removing every piece is rarely needed. It is disconnected and capped, while new lines run beside it, which keeps drywall damage low. Only accessible pipe comes out.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Blacksburg, VA

    Galvanized steel was common before the 1960s. Inside, rust builds once the coating fails. Over decades the opening shrinks, pressure drops and the water turns brown after sitting, while threaded joints corrode through first. Replacing it is the only fix.

    Copper Pipe Replacement in Blacksburg, VA

    Copper still has fans. It handles heat well and lasts decades. We install Type L copper for most interior supply lines, sweat or press the joints, and support every run with hangers at the spacing code requires, so the new pipe does not rattle or sag. Costs more than PEX.

    Corroded Pipe Replacement in Blacksburg, VA

    Corrosion shows up as green or white crust on copper, rust on steel, or pinholes that drip. Some water is harder on pipe. We replace corroded sections or the whole system depending on how widespread the damage is, and we check the water heater connections and shutoffs since they often corrode too. Look at the joints.

    Slab Leak Pipe Repair and Replacement in Blacksburg

    Water under a slab shows up as a warm spot on the floor, a sound of running water, or a meter that spins with everything off. We locate the leak first. Then you have a choice between breaking the slab to fix that spot or rerouting the line through walls or the attic, which skips the jackhammer. We explain both.
