    Hydro-Jetting Services in Blacksburg

    A hydro jetter sends water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full pipe wall. Unlike a cable, the jet cleans the whole pipe wall, not just a hole through the clog. Restaurants need it most. A camera check comes first to confirm the pipe can take it.

    French Drain Installation in Blacksburg

    A French drain is a gravel-filled trench with perforated pipe that collects groundwater and carries it away. We wrap the gravel in filter fabric so silt does not clog it. The pipe needs at least a 1 percent slope to a daylight outlet, a dry well or a sump, and the outlet end needs a grate so animals do not nest in it.

    A typical French drain or downspout system takes one to three days. Larger projects run longer. Dry wells, long trenches or saw-cut concrete can push it toward a week.

    Drain cleaning usually means a cable machine with a cutting head that bores through the clog. It works fast and costs less. Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to scrub the whole pipe wall clean of grease, scale and roots, so it lasts longer but costs more and needs a pipe that is in good enough shape to take the pressure.

    Clearing a clog does not need a permit. Pipe repairs often require one. Replacing a section of sewer, adding a cleanout, or connecting a new drain to the sewer generally requires a permit and an inspection, and we handle that paperwork and schedule the inspector as part of the job.

    Snaking is the right first step for most single clogs. It costs less and takes less time. Jetting wins for grease, scale and recurring roots since it cleans the whole wall.
