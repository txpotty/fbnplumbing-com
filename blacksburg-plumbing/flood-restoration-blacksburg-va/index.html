    Frequently Asked Questions About Flood Restoration in Blacksburg

    Smaller flood cleanups with extraction and drying often cost $2,500 to $6,000. Larger projects with contamination, mud removal and rebuild work can run $10,000 to $30,000 or more, especially in finished basements with carpet and built-ins. Insurance may help.

    Extraction, drying and basic repairs usually run 3 to 7 days. Bigger floods with mold, structural damage or a finished basement to rebuild can take several weeks from the first pump-out to the last coat of paint. You get a timeline.

    Drywall and flooring replacement usually do not need permits. Heater swaps, electrical and structural work often require them. We handle plumbing permits when they are required.

    Submersible pumps handle deep standing water, and truck-mounted extractors pull it out of carpet and floors. For drying, we bring high-velocity air movers, LGR and desiccant dehumidifiers, HEPA air scrubbers and meters to track moisture in the framing. Every truck carries it.

    We remove saturated porous materials, apply antimicrobial to the framing and dry the structure with commercial dehumidifiers and air movers. Moisture readings confirm materials are dry before rebuild. Speed beats mold.

    Basic extraction and drying generally do not need permits. Major structural repairs, electrical work or plumbing replacement usually do, and the Blacksburg building department sets the rules for each type of work. Permits are handled by us.

    Mold needs moisture and time. Fast pump-out, tear-out, antimicrobial and drying inside the 24 to 48 hour window remove both. Speed is everything.

    Outside surface water is usually excluded from standard homeowners policies. Storm runoff usually needs a flood policy. Damage from a burst pipe or appliance failure is more often covered under the regular policy as sudden and accidental.
