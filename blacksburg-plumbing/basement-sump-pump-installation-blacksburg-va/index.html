    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Blacksburg

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the water. That keeps it dry and easy to reach, which is why they suit narrow pits under 14 inches across, and why they often outlast cheap submersibles in Blacksburg homes. Expect more noise.

    Sump Pit Installation in Blacksburg, VA

    Pit size controls how often the pump starts. A small 15 inch pit makes the pump cycle every minute in heavy flow, so we usually install an 18 by 22 inch or 18 by 30 inch basin with a solid bottom and perforated sides wrapped in filter fabric. Fewer starts mean a longer life.

    Primary Sump Pump Replacement in Blacksburg

    Pumps wear out, usually somewhere between seven and ten years. We pull the old unit and inspect the pit, the check valve and the float clearance, then install a new pump on fresh PVC so you are not hanging a new motor on an old cracked fitting in Blacksburg. Most swaps take two hours.

    Residential Basement Sump Pump Installation in Blacksburg

    A typical house pit holds an 18 by 22 inch basin with a 1/3 horsepower pump, and that covers most homes. Our plumbers size the unit to the water coming in and the height it has to climb, not to the sale sticker. In Blacksburg, we set the basin at the low corner, bed it in washed gravel and run 1-1/2 inch PVC out through the rim joist. Discharge gets carried far enough out that it cannot soak back toward the wall.

    High-Capacity Sump Pump Systems in Blacksburg, VA

    Some lots sit in a high water table and fill a standard pit every few minutes during spring. For those, we step up to a 1/2 or 3/4 horsepower unit in a bigger basin, all sized after we time how fast your pit refills. We use a stopwatch.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Blacksburg

    Outages and heavy rain tend to show up together. A battery backup keeps a second pump running without house power, and a fully charged 75 amp-hour battery can run a typical backup pump for several hours of on-and-off cycling, longer on a light night. We test it under load.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Blacksburg, VA

    Battery backups earn their keep during outages. Saturated ground after a storm can push water into basements and back up sewer lines, making backflow valves and sump pumps worthwhile. We add a second pump in the same pit with its own float, wired to a sealed AGM battery on a smart charger that tests itself and beeps when the battery gets weak. The main pump keeps doing the daily work.

    Sump Basin and Discharge Line Installation in Blacksburg

    The basin sets the ceiling on what the pump can do. We place it at the lowest point of the floor, bed it in washed stone, and run the discharge line with a slight pitch to the exit point so nothing sits in the pipe in Blacksburg. Nothing should drain back toward the wall.
