    Burner Cleaning and Repair in Roanoke, VA

    Yellow flame tips, soot on the burner door or a roaring sound at startup all mean the burner is not getting clean air. We pull the burner, brush the ports and blow out the orifice.     Gas Water Heater Noise Troubleshooting in Roanoke

    Rumbling or popping from the bottom of the tank is water boiling under a layer of sediment. Hard water and years without a flush both make it worse.     Flammable Vapor Sensor Repair in Roanoke

We clean the screen and the chamber. We replace any tripped thermal switch.

    A thermocouple is a small copper-sheathed rod that sits in the pilot flame and makes a tiny electric current. That current holds a magnet in the gas valve open. No flame, no current. When the pilot goes out, the magnet lets go and the valve shuts off gas to both the pilot and the burner, which is the safety that keeps raw gas from filling a closet. Weak thermocouples cause more pilot outages than anything else.

    Most repairs take one to three hours. Pilot and thermocouple jobs usually wrap up in under an hour.
