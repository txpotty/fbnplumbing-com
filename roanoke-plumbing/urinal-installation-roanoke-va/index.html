    Waterless Urinal Installation in Roanoke

    Waterless urinals save the most water and need the most discipline. A cartridge or sealing liquid blocks sewer gas while letting urine through, and it must be replaced on schedule. Cleaning staff need a quick lesson. We show them how.

    Old Urinal Removal and Replacement in Roanoke

    Old urinals come off harder than they went on. Rusted bolts and seized valves often have to be cut, so we protect the tile while working. We never reuse the old gasket. The flange gets cleaned to bare metal.

    Residential Urinal Installation in Roanoke, VA

    Basement and garage bathrooms are where most home urinals go. A 2 inch drain, a vent tie-in and a supply sized to the valve go in, with blocking or a compact carrier. Many use 0.125 gallons per flush. That barely touches the water bill.

    Flushometer Urinal Installation in Roanoke

    Manual flushometers are still the most common setup. Manual valves go on a 3/4 inch line with the control stop tuned and the vacuum breaker at the correct height. Handles can go left or right. ADA units need the handle within reach.

    Flush Valve Urinal Installation in Roanoke, VA

    Flush valve urinals are the commercial standard. A flushometer from Sloan or Zurn delivers a measured volume from a 3/4 inch supply with no tank, and it recovers instantly for the next user, which matters in a stadium or school restroom. The volume matches the fixture rating. Wrong settings stain bowls.

    Urinal Upgrades and Water Savings in Roanoke, VA

    Older urinals often use 1.5 gallons or more per flush. A low-flow bowl and matching valve on the existing carrier cut water use sharply. Some utilities offer rebates. We provide the model numbers you need.

    American Standard and Kohler Urinal Installation in Roanoke

    American Standard and Kohler make the china we install most. Models like the Washbrook and the Bardon come in versions from 0.125 to 1.0 gallons per flush, and each one needs a flush valve matched to that rating or the bowl will either splash or not rinse clean. Matching is simple with the spec sheet. We order both together.

    Sensor Urinal Installation in Roanoke, VA

    Sensor valves stop missed flushes and double flushes. We install battery or hardwired units, set the sensing range so the valve does not fire when someone walks past, and turn on a sanitary flush cycle if the restroom sits empty for long stretches. Expect years between battery changes. Hardwired units need an electrician first.
