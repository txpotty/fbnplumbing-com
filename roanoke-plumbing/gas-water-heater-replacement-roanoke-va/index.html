    Gas Water Heater Efficiency Ratings in Roanoke

    Efficiency ratings on tank heaters are listed as UEF, uniform energy factor, and a higher number means less gas per gallon heated. Basic tanks land near 0.60 UEF. Condensing models top 0.80, and some qualify for utility rebates where those are offered.

    High-Efficiency Gas Water Heater Upgrades in Roanoke, VA

    Condensing gas tanks run around 90 percent efficient or better and vent through PVC, while a standard atmospheric tank sits closer to 60 percent. They cost more and need a condensate drain and an outlet. Big households see payback sooner.

    40 and 50 Gallon Tank Replacement in Roanoke

    Forty and fifty gallon gas tanks are the workhorse in most Virginia homes, and a leak at the bottom seam means the tank is finished. Current tanks are often taller and wider. We check clearance before we order. Plan on about two to three hours.

    Commercial Gas Water Heater Replacement in Roanoke, VA

    Commercial gas heaters in restaurants and offices often run 75 to 100 gallons at 75,000 BTU or more, and they need a gas line sized for that load. Code is stricter here. We size by peak demand and recovery, not tank volume alone.

    Standard Gas Water Heater Replacement in Roanoke
