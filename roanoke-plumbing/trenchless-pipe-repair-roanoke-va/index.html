    Sewer Lateral Lining in Roanoke

    Laterals are the most common candidates for trenchless repair. Old materials crack and sag. Over time, clay, cast iron and Orangeburg pipe crack, sag or fill with roots, so we jet, scope and then pick lining or bursting. The new line restores full flow and resists roots.

    Trenchless vs Traditional Costs in Roanoke, VA

    On paper, the two methods can price out close. Then add restoration. A trench under a driveway or through an established garden means paying to replace concrete, plants and irrigation after the plumbing is done, and trenchless avoids most of those costs. We price both side by side.

    Lateral and Spot Lining in Roanoke

    Not every pipe needs lining end to end. A few bad joints need a short fix. When damage is limited to a few joints or a short section, a sectional liner costs less than a full-length one and goes in quickly, but we confirm the rest of the line is sound by camera before recommending it.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Roanoke

    Tree roots, corrosion and soil movement wreck most sewer lines. Basements are common in northern and western parts of Virginia, while crawlspaces and slabs are more typical in the east and south. Small cracks get a sectional liner. Longer damage gets a full-length liner, and a fully collapsed line gets burst and replaced, all without trenching across the yard or cutting the driveway at your home.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Roanoke

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. It hardens inside the old pipe. Once inside the cleaned pipe, it gets inflated against the wall and cured with heat or UV, forming a new pipe with no joints. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your Roanoke yard.

    Pipe Bursting Services in Roanoke, VA

    Bursting replaces a pipe that is too far gone to line. A cone-shaped head breaks the old pipe. As a winch drags the head through, the old pipe fractures into the soil and new HDPE is pulled into the same path behind it. HDPE does not rust or leak at joints in Roanoke, VA soil.
