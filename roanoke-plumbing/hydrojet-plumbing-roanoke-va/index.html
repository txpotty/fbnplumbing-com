    Preventive jetting pays off for lines that clog more than once a year. Camera findings set the interval. Grease-heavy lines may need six-month service, while root-prone sewers can often go 18 to 24 months.

    Water-Only Drain Cleaning in Roanoke, VA

    Hydrojetting uses only water, so nothing caustic goes into your pipes or septic tank. Chemical cleaners can damage pipe.     Kitchen Grease Line Jetting in Roanoke

    Kitchen drains collect grease that cools and hardens along the pipe wall, especially in long horizontal runs under a slab. Cabling only cuts a narrow channel. A spinning nozzle removes the grease all around the pipe so it flushes away cleanly.
