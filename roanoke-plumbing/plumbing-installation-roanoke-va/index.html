    Natural Gas and Propane Lines in Roanoke, VA

    Natural gas and propane run at different pressures, and the pipe sizing changes with them. Our licensed plumbers size each run from the appliance BTU ratings and the developed length, then choose black iron or CSST to suit the route. Code requires CSST to be bonded. Without that bond, a lightning strike nearby can arc through the tubing and start a fire.

    Backflow Assembly Testing and Installation in Roanoke

    Fire lines and irrigation systems account for most of the backflow assemblies we install. A certified tester checks each assembly on install day and the results go to the water provider.     PEX Repiping in Roanoke

    Copper repipes cost more and take longer, which is why most whole-house repipes today use PEX-A or PEX-B tubing with brass or poly fittings. It runs quieter than copper, with less water hammer banging in the walls. It also handles a freeze better. Most Virginia winters bring several hard freezes, and cold snaps can burst pipes in crawlspaces, garages and exterior walls. Fittings are matched to the tubing manufacturer.

    Tankless Water Heater Installation in Roanoke, VA

    Navien and Rinnai tankless units hang on a wall and free up the floor space a tank used to take. Most gas units need upsized gas piping and a new vent run in stainless or PVC. Electric models need amperage. We size by gallons per minute and the temperature rise from your incoming water. Summer humidity leads to condensation on cold water lines and in crawlspaces, which can speed up corrosion and invite mold. Isolation valves go on every unit for descaling.

    Expansion Tank and Sewage Ejector Installs in Roanoke, VA

    A closed water system has nowhere for heated water to expand, so pressure climbs every single time the heater cycles. An expansion tank absorbs that swing and keeps the relief valve from dripping. Homes with a PRV usually need one. For basement baths and guest suites below the sewer, we also install ejector systems with a sealed basin, check valve and vent.

    PEX Pipe Installation and Repiping in Roanoke

    Pinhole leaks in copper keep coming back once the pipe walls start to thin. Repiping in PEX stops the cycle. The layout is a manifold with home runs for balanced pressure, or trunk and branch when the framing makes home runs a fight. We cut small, clean access holes and patch them, or hand off to your drywall contractor if you prefer.
