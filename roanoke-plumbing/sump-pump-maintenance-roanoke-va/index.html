    Commercial Building Sump Pumps in Roanoke, VA

    Parking garages, mechanical rooms and loading docks often drain to large pits with pumps rated at a horsepower or more. They need checking more often than anything in a house. We test the controls, the alarm panel and both pumps in a duplex setup, then log the amp draw on each one so trends show up over time. Downtime costs money.

    Pre-Storm Season Pump Testing in Roanoke

    Saturated ground after a storm can push water into basements and back up sewer lines, making backwater valves and sump pumps worthwhile. We test the pump a few weeks before the wet season by filling the pit with a hose until the float trips. Then we time how fast it pumps out and listen to the bearings. A struggling pump can then be replaced on a calm day instead of in the middle of a storm.

    Submersible Sump Pump Service in Roanoke

    Submersible pumps sit at the bottom of the pit, which keeps them quiet but hides the problems. We lift the unit out to look at the seals and the cord. A cracked cord usually means a new pump. We also clean the intake screen and check the housing for corrosion, which shows up fast on pumps that sit in standing water for years.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Maintenance in Roanoke, VA

    Backup pumps sit in the pit next to the main pump and run off a 12-volt battery when the power drops or the primary fails. Our plumber load tests the battery instead of just reading its voltage. Then we unplug the main pump and let the backup empty the pit on its own. Alarms get tested too.

    Battery Backup System Testing in Roanoke, VA

    Storms knock out power when the pit fills fastest. That is the moment a backup pump earns its keep. We check the battery date, clean the terminals and confirm the charger is actually charging, since a dead charger quietly drains the battery to nothing over a few months.

    Check Valve and Discharge Pipe Repair in Roanoke, VA

    A bad check valve can nearly double how often the pump starts. Every startup puts heat into the motor. Our plumbers replace worn valves with quieter spring-loaded types and check the pipe for cracks and loose couplings out to where it leaves the house. Discharge needs to land far enough from the slab or footings that it does not seep right back into the pit.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Service in Roanoke, VA

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor up on a column above the water, so the wear shows up in the shaft, the bearings and the float rod. We check the rod for bends and the stops for slipping. Bearings get a listen. Rattling or a high whine at startup usually means the motor is near the end.

    Home Sump Pump Maintenance in Roanoke

    A typical house pit is about 18 inches across and two feet deep, and most of what goes wrong happens inside it. We pull the pump, clear the intake screen and scoop out the silt. Then we run a full water test. Soils in Virginia range from sandy ground with a high water table near the coast to dense clay and rocky karst farther inland.
