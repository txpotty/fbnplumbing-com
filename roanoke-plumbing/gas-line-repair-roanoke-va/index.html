    What Our Gas Line Plumbers Fix in Roanoke, VA

    Pool Heater, Fire Pit and Grill Lines in Roanoke, VA

    Backyard gas lines for pool heaters, fire pits and built-in grills face sun, weather and irrigation water that shorten their life. Pool heaters are the big load, often 250,000 to 400,000 BTU, so a line that was fine for a grill can starve the heater and the house appliances at the same time. Sizing is the first thing we check. New and repaired lines get tested, buried to the required depth and laid with tracer wire.

    Gas Appliance Connector Repair in Roanoke, VA

    Weak flames or a smell near one appliance often point to the connector or shutoff behind it. Older uncoated brass connectors can crack at the flare nut, and they are no longer accepted by most codes or utilities. We replace them with coated stainless connectors. We also add a reachable shutoff and a drip leg where needed, and confirm the new connector is rated for that appliance's BTU input.

    CSST Repair and Bonding in Roanoke, VA

    Newer homes often have corrugated stainless steel tubing running through the attic. Nails, rodents and lightning can all damage CSST, and older yellow-jacket tubing must be bonded to the home's grounding system to lower arc risk. It takes about an hour. We repair damaged sections with manufacturer-approved fittings and verify bonding before we leave. Mountain areas face flash flooding when heavy rain runs off steep slopes and into narrow valleys.

    Permits and Code Compliance for Gas Work in Roanoke, VA

    Most gas piping work beyond a simple connector swap needs a permit in Roanoke, VA. The inspector checks pipe sizing, material, support spacing, shutoff locations, sediment traps and the pressure test result before signing off. We handle the paperwork. Doing it this way keeps your insurance clean and gives you a record of the repair if you sell the house later.

    Gas Leak Detection in Roanoke

    A rotten egg smell, hissing near a line, dead grass in a stripe across the yard or a gas meter that moves with everything off are the common leak signs. Trust your nose. Our plumbers isolate the system section by section, sweep it with an electronic detector and confirm the exact spot with soap solution. After we locate the leak, we price the repair in writing.

    Buried Yard Line Repair and Replacement in Roanoke, VA

    Many older homes have steel gas lines running from the meter to the house or out to a pool heater. The wrap fails eventually. After the coating breaks down, corrosion starts, and constant moisture from irrigation or poor drainage makes it worse. We call 811 before digging. We lay polyethylene with tracer wire or coated steel where exposed, then backfill and clean up after the test passes.

    Propane Gas Line Service in Roanoke, VA

    Propane systems run at different pressures than natural gas and use a two-stage regulator setup between the tank and the house. Regulators fail too. Our work covers propane yard lines, regulator replacement and fitting repairs, plus a check that every appliance has its propane orifices installed. Wrong orifices make a burner run dirty and unsafe. We test the system the same way we test natural gas piping.

    Gas Leak Repair at Fittings and Valves in Roanoke

    Most residential gas leaks are at a threaded joint, a valve stem or a flexible connector, not in the middle of a pipe. That keeps repairs small. We back out the leaking joint, clean the threads, apply gas-rated thread sealant and reassemble it, or replace the fitting or valve if the threads are damaged. Every repair gets a soap test and a system pressure test before we leave.
