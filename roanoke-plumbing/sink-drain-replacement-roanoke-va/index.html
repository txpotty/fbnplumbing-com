    Vessel and Grid Drain Replacement in Roanoke

    Vessel sinks use a different drain. Most glass and stone vessel bowls have no overflow hole, so they need an unslotted grid or umbrella drain, and a slotted drain in one of those bowls leaks out of the slots right into the counter hole. Mismatched drains are common. Swapping it takes under an hour.

    Code Updates During Drain Replacement in Roanoke

    Old drains sometimes break current rules. S-traps and drum traps were common decades ago, and when we replace the drain we bring it up to a vented P-trap with the right trap arm length. An AAV may be allowed where a vent is missing. Local rules decide.

    Push-Button Drain Installation in Roanoke, VA

    Push-button drains get rid of the lift rod. They open and close with a press on the cap, and we check that the sink has an overflow before choosing a slotted body, since a slotted drain in a bowl without an overflow leaks out the slots. Cleaning is simpler with no linkage. Hair still collects, so pull the cap now and then.

    Commercial Lavatory Drain Replacement in Roanoke

    Public restroom drains take constant use. Offset grid drains and heavy traps go in, with insulated covers where accessibility rules apply. Work can happen after hours. Inspectors get a clean, labeled job.

    Emergency Sink Drain Service in Roanoke

    A trap that falls apart sends a sink of water into the cabinet. Turn off the faucet, set a bucket under it, and stop using that sink, and if the drain is backed up into a second fixture, stop using water elsewhere in the house too.     Utility and Basement Sink Drain Replacement in Roanoke

    Basement sinks often drain to a pump. Sinks below the sewer line drain through a pump, so we check its valve and discharge line during the job. Pumps fail quietly. A gurgling pit is a clue.

    Corroded Drain Replacement in Roanoke, VA

    Drain cleaners speed up corrosion. Caustic products sit in the bottom of a metal trap between uses and eat at the chrome plating and the thin brass underneath, which is why so many old traps we pull have pinholes right at the bottom of the bend. PVC does not have that problem. We recommend it for most cabinets.
