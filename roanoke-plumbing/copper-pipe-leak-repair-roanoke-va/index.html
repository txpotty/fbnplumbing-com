    Commercial Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Roanoke

    Commercial buildings often run larger diameter copper for domestic water. Shutdowns cost money. We repair copper supply lines in offices, restaurants and apartment buildings, using press tools on large lines to shorten the shutdown and skip the torch. Early or late work is available.

    Copper Pinhole Leak Repair in Roanoke

    Pinholes form when the inner wall of copper pipe erodes or pits. Hot lines get it worst. They often appear in thin Type M pipe and on hot water lines where velocity and temperature are higher, so we cut out the affected section and replace it with Type L copper or a press fitting. More pinholes often follow.

    Solder Joint Leak Repair in Roanoke, VA

    Older solder joints can fail from poor original work, thermal expansion or vibration. Look for white or green crust. A leaking joint usually weeps for a while before it drips, so we either resolder it or replace the fitting and a short length of pipe. The joint gets tested.
