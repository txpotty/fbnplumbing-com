    Brine Tank Cleanout and Inspection in Roanoke, VA

    Service for Most Softener Brands in Roanoke, VA

    Culligan, Kinetico, EcoWater, Morton and the store brands all use different valves, and parts for some of them only come through the dealer. Seal kits for the common valves ride on the truck.     Regeneration Programming and Hardness Testing in Roanoke, VA

    Plenty of softeners still run factory settings. The installer never changed them, or the household grew and nobody updated the valve. We enter your raw hardness and household size, then set a metered valve to regenerate by gallons used instead of every few days on a timer.

    Resin Rebedding in Roanoke

    Chlorine and chloramine in treated water slowly swell and soften resin beads, and iron or sediment can coat them until capacity drops off. We try a resin cleaner first. If the bed is spent, we drain the tank, pull the old resin and refill it with fresh high-capacity resin, which runs far less than a new softener.

    Hardness Testing at the Tap in Roanoke

    We test with a titration kit, counting drops until the sample changes color, because test strips are too rough for setting a valve. We take the raw sample from an unsoftened hose bib or with the softener on bypass. Softened water comes from the kitchen tap.

    Bypass Valve and Drain Line Repairs in Roanoke, VA

    A bypass valve that does not seat fully lets raw water blend into the softened supply, and the hardness test is usually the first clue. We rebuild or replace the bypass and check the drain line for kinks. The drain line has to end above its receptor with an air gap.
