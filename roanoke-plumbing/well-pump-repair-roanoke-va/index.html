    Well Pump Repair vs Replacement Cost in Roanoke, VA

    Switch and control repairs often cost $150 to $500, while a pressure tank replacement typically runs $600 to $1,500.     Pressure Switch Replacement in Roanoke

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Most switches fail from pitted contacts, a plugged sensing tube or insects in the housing. We replace the switch, clean or replace the tube and set the cut-in and cut-out pressures to match your tank, typically 40 and 60 psi. One of the cheaper well repairs.

    Submersible Well Pump Repair in Roanoke

    Submersible pumps hang inside the well casing on drop pipe, often 100 feet or more below the wellhead. The usual signs are no water at all, weak pressure or a hum with no flow. We test the motor from the surface first. When a pull is needed, a hoist brings the pipe up safely so we can replace the motor, the impellers or the check valve.

    Franklin Electric Well Pump Service in Roanoke, VA

    Franklin Electric motors and control boxes are in a lot of local wells. We work on Franklin motors, QD control boxes and SubDrive units regularly. We carry the common Franklin parts. Replacing a failed motor usually costs less than a rewind once shipping is counted.

    Control Box and Surge Repair in Roanoke, VA

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and a relay. Saturated ground after a storm can push water into basements and back up sewer lines, making backflow valves and sump pumps worthwhile. Lightning and power surges damage those parts more than anything else. After testing the capacitor, relay and overload, we replace the bad part and suggest a surge arrestor if the box lacks one. An arrestor costs little.

    Above-Ground Jet Pump Repair in Roanoke

    Lost prime is the number one jet pump complaint. These pumps can also leak at the shaft seal or wear out an impeller. We reprime the pump, replace the seal and check the foot valve and suction line for leaks, since one tiny air leak will undo the whole repair. Everything is above ground.
