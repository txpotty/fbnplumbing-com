    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Roanoke, VA

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water. Paper and solids settle there. Expansive clays in some areas swell when wet and shrink when dry, which can crack foundations and shift buried sewer lines. We measure the depth of standing water on camera, dig out that section, re-bed the pipe on compacted gravel and set it back to a quarter inch of fall per foot, which is the typical code minimum for a four-inch line. Not every sag needs digging, and we will tell you which yours is.
