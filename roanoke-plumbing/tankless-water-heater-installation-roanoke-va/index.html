    Tankless Water Heater Relocation in Roanoke

    Moving the heater is common on a tankless job because the old tank spot is not always the best place. Mounting near the gas meter on a garage or outside wall shortens the gas line and venting, though it can add distance to the bathrooms. We weigh both. A far bath may need recirc.

    Upgrading from Tank to Tankless in Roanoke, VA

    Switching from a tank takes more than unbolting one and hanging the other. A 40-gallon tank burns about 40,000 BTU, but a whole-home tankless unit may need 150,000 to 199,000, so the meter line nearly always gets bigger. Venting changes too. Both are on the estimate.

    Tankless vs Tank Cost Comparison in Roanoke, VA

    Upfront, a tankless install in Roanoke, VA costs more than a standard tank because of the unit price, the gas line upgrade and the venting changes. Over the long haul, the math narrows. Less standby loss, a life of up to 20 years versus roughly 10 for a tank, and replaceable parts all count over the years. Ask for both prices.

    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Installation in Roanoke, VA

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Installation in Roanoke

    Gas tankless units in Roanoke are the common pick for whole-house hot water because a single 199,000 BTU burner can feed two showers and a sink at the same time without losing temperature. Gas is the catch. Most homes need the line upsized from the meter, often from half-inch to three-quarter inch or larger. We size it and pressure test it before the unit is lit.

    Commercial Tankless Water Heater Services in Roanoke, VA

    Businesses in Roanoke, VA with steady hot water use, like salons, restaurants and small gyms, often get more out of two or three tankless units racked together than one big tank. One unit can fail and the rest still carry the load. We size the rack by peak demand. Commercial installs usually need a larger gas service and a mechanical permit.

    Residential Tankless System Upgrades in Roanoke

    Upgrading from a tank in Roanoke begins with your current setup: tank size, vent type, gas line size and where the unit sits.     Electric Tankless Water Heater Installation in Roanoke, VA

    Electric tankless heaters make sense in Roanoke, VA homes without gas, or for a single sink or a guest bath far from the main heater. Whole-house electric units pull a lot of current. Some models need two or three 40 to 60 amp double-pole breakers, more than a 100-amp panel can usually spare alongside the range and AC. Panel capacity gets checked up front.
