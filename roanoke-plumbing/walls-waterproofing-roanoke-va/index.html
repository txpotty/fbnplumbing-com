    Drainage System Integration in Roanoke, VA

    Water has to go somewhere once a wall is sealed. Exterior footing drains, interior drain tile and surface French drains all do the same job at different depths. We match the drain to where the water comes from, which might be a high water table, a yard that slopes toward the house, or a downspout emptying right at the foundation. We extend downspouts at least four feet from the wall.

    Below-Grade and Basement Wall Waterproofing in Roanoke

    Housing in Virginia spans centuries, from historic homes with cast iron and galvanized pipe to newer subdivisions plumbed with PEX. Any wall below grade sits in soil that gets saturated after heavy rain or snowmelt, and that water pushes against the wall with real hydrostatic pressure. We inject cracks, coat the wall inside or out, and add drain tile wherever water collects at the footing. A sump goes in if groundwater sits high. Some basements never need a sump.

    Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in Roanoke

    Bare dirt in a crawl space releases gallons of water vapor a day into the air under your floor. A 10 mil or heavier polyethylene barrier, overlapped a foot at every seam and taped, cuts that off and keeps the joists and subfloor dry. We run it up the foundation walls and hold it in place with termination strip. Cheap fix, big difference.

    Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Roanoke, VA

    Wet baseboards outside the shower or a stain on the ceiling below usually point to the pan. We plug the drain, fill the pan with an inch or two of water, and mark the level and wait 24 hours. A drop means a leak. Rebuilding means a mortar pre-slope of a quarter inch per foot, a new PVC liner or bonded membrane, and a two-piece clamping drain set on the liner. We flood test the new pan before any tile goes down.

    Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in Roanoke, VA

    White crust on block or stem walls is efflorescence, salt left behind as water moves through the masonry and dries. The usual cause is a sprinkler head spraying the wall instead of the plants, or soil graded toward the foundation. We fix the water first. Then we clean the wall, let it dry and apply a breathable masonry sealer or elastomeric coating.

    Basement Wall Sealing in Roanoke, VA

    Concrete block is porous, and its hollow cores fill with water like a sponge.     Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Roanoke

    Interior systems manage water after it has come through the wall, using drain tile under the slab edge and a sump pump. Exterior waterproofing stops water before it reaches the wall, which means digging down to the footing and applying a membrane to the outside face. Outside costs more.

    Anywhere from an afternoon to over a week. Interior crack sealing might take a day or two, while exterior excavation with new footing drains might run several days or longer than a week depending on the size of the house. Rain delays exterior work.

    Look low on the wall first. Damp patches, a musty smell, mold on the bottom of the drywall, white powdery efflorescence on block or brick, and puddles after rain all point to water coming through. Bowing walls and stair-step cracks need a closer look too.

    A shower wall job usually takes two to four days from demolition to finished membrane, counting the drying time between coats. Exterior block sealing often wraps up in a day. Larger foundation jobs can run several days, and weather can stretch that.
