    Drainage and Venting for Urinals in Alexandria, VA

    Urinal drains clog with scale, not paper. Venting stops gurgle and odor. We tie in within code distance.

    Residential Urinal Plumbing in Alexandria, VA

    Home urinals use the same rules as commercial ones. The drain is usually 2 inch, the trap is built into the china, the fixture needs a vent within code distance, and the flush valve needs either a 3/4 inch supply or a lower-flow residential valve on 1/2 inch. Your city may want a permit. We check that first.

    Home Gym and Garage Urinals in Alexandria, VA

    A garage urinal saves tracking dirt into the house. Unheated garages need freeze planning. Ask us about the layout.

    Flushometer Urinal Installation in Alexandria

    Manual flushometers are still the most common setup.
