    Emergency Faucet Repair in Alexandria

    A faucet that will not shut off, or one with a cracked body spraying under the sink, needs the water off before anything else happens. Turn the two small valves under the sink clockwise. If they will not turn, use the main shutoff for the house. We come fast for these.

    Kitchen Sprayer and Diverter Repair in Alexandria, VA

    Spray heads on kitchen faucets get clogged with scale, and side sprayers lose pressure when the diverter inside the faucet body wears out. Diverters are cheap parts. Touchless faucets add a solenoid and a battery pack to the list, and we test those before replacing anything.

    Leaky Faucet Repair in Alexandria

    Most drips come from the part that seals the water inside the faucet, which is a rubber washer on older two-handle faucets and a cartridge or ceramic disc on newer ones. We figure out which type you have first. It matters. With the right part on the truck, most drips are fixed in one visit.

    Delta and Moen Faucet Repair in Alexandria, VA

    Delta and Moen are the faucets we repair most, and both back their cartridges with lifetime warranties for the original owner on many models. We can check whether yours qualifies. Kohler, Pfister and American Standard each use proprietary cartridges, and we stock the common ones for each brand.

    Low Water Pressure Faucet Fix in Alexandria, VA

    Weak flow at one faucet usually points to a clogged aerator or a cartridge packed with scale, not a problem with the house pressure. Water hardness in Virginia varies by region, with more mineral-heavy water in the western limestone valleys and softer water closer to the coast.     Cracked Faucet Body Repair in Alexandria

    Cracked faucet bodies, usually from freezing or an old brass casting that finally gave out, cannot be repaired and need a replacement faucet. Shut the stops first. We can usually install a new faucet the same day, and we will check the supply lines and stops while the water is off.

    Commercial Faucet Repair in Alexandria, VA

    Restaurant and office faucets cycle hundreds of times a day, so cartridges, handle springs, metering valves and sensor solenoids wear out far faster than they do in a house.
