    Tub-to-Shower Conversion in Alexandria, VA

    Shower Pan Installation in Alexandria

    Shower pans fail quietly. Pinholes and loose drain clamps let water into the subfloor slowly, which is why tiled pans sit flooded for a full day before tile. We level prefab pans carefully. Both kinds get slope toward the drain.

    Shower Upgrades Without a Full Remodel in Alexandria

    Shower Stall Installation in Alexandria

    Barrier-Free Walk-In Showers in Alexandria, VA

    Barrier-free entries need careful drain placement. The membrane and slope get planned with the tile setter, so water reaches the drain instead of spilling out the open side.
