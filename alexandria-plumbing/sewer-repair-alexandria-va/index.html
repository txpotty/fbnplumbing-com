    Tree Root Invasion Solutions in Alexandria

    Roots in a sewer line are not the tree's fault. A leaking joint invited them in. Warm, wet, nutrient-rich air escaping from a loose joint draws roots toward the line, and once they get in they thicken until they crack the pipe or separate the joint completely. We cut them out, then talk about the long-term fix. Lining or a joint replacement keeps them from coming back.

    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Alexandria

    Some lines are too far gone for a liner. A fully collapsed section is one example. When the camera shows a collapse, a large offset or a belly deep enough to hold several inches of water, we open a trench, remove the old pipe and lay new Schedule 40 PVC on proper bedding at a quarter inch of fall per foot. Older homes often need this on at least one section. We stage the work so you lose sewer service for a few hours, not days.

    Restaurant and Retail Sewer Repair in Alexandria

    Restaurants, retail centers and multifamily buildings load a lateral far beyond a single house. Grease is the usual culprit.     Emergency Sewer Repair in Alexandria

    Raw sewage on a basement floor is not a next-week problem. We answer at night and on weekends.     Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Alexandria

    Orangeburg is wood fiber soaked in coal tar. It flattens with age. PVC goes in, usually end to end. Trenchless bursting is often an option.

    Sewer Pipe Relining in Alexandria, VA

    Relining builds a new pipe inside the old one. The old pipe gets jetted clean before anything else. A flexible liner saturated with epoxy is pulled or inverted into place, inflated against the old wall and left to cure, which seals cracks and root entry points in a single continuous tube. The finished pipe loses a little diameter. For most houses the flow difference is negligible.
