    Slab Leak Detection in Alexandria

    Concrete slab homes hide supply lines under the floor, and a pinhole there can run for months. Warm floor spots are the classic sign. FBN Plumbing isolates the hot and cold sides with pressure gauges, then listens through the slab with a ground microphone and confirms with a thermal camera, which lets us mark the spot before cutting anything.

    Commercial Water Leak Detection in Alexandria, VA

    Offices, shops and apartment buildings in Alexandria have long supply runs that hide leaks. Closures are expensive. FBN Plumbing isolates each zone and tests risers, mains and branch lines with pressure gauges, then locates leaks with acoustic and thermal tools, working around your schedule so business keeps going.

    Hidden Wall and Ceiling Leaks in Alexandria, VA

    Stained drywall, peeling paint and a musty smell usually mean water inside the wall. Shower valves cause many of these. Our techs scan with thermal cameras and confirm with a moisture meter, checking fittings, old galvanized pipe and shower pans until the source is clear, so the drywall cut stays small.

    Water Meter Leak Testing in Alexandria

    The meter is the first and simplest leak test. Everything off, then watch. If the leak indicator spins with every fixture and appliance off, water is escaping somewhere, and the flow rate gives a rough count of gallons lost per day. Then we shut off zones one at a time.

    Underground Water Line Leaks in Alexandria

    The buried line from the meter to the house may leak for months before showing up. Look for green stripes in a dry lawn. FBN Plumbing traces the pipe with an electronic locator, listens for the leak with a ground microphone and switches to tracer gas on plastic lines, so we dig one targeted hole instead of trenching the whole yard.

    Urgent Leak Calls in Alexandria, VA

    Burst lines need quick action. Close the main. Our techs arrive with detection tools and repair parts, find the failed section and stop the loss, then go over the repair choices and costs before any permanent work begins in your Alexandria home.

    Water Heater and Softener Leaks in Alexandria

    A softener stuck in regeneration sends water down the drain nonstop. That shows up on the bill. Water heaters leak at fittings, drain valves and relief valves, and a dripping relief valve often means high pressure or a failed expansion tank. We check both units on every visit.

    Main Service Line Leaks in Alexandria, VA

    The service line carries full street pressure and may leak below ground for months. Soft spots in the lawn may be the only sign. We trace the line, listen for the leak and mark it for a targeted repair, and we often recommend replacing old galvanized or polybutylene service pipe rather than patching it again.
