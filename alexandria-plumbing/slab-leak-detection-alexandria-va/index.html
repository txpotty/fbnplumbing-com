    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Alexandria, VA

    Cold-side leaks leave no warm spot. They are harder to see. They usually show up as a running meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards, which is why we isolate the cold line, trace its route and listen along it with acoustic gear.

    Residential Slab Leak Detection in Alexandria

    Single-family homes and townhouses across Alexandria get the same step-by-step process. Meter test first. We isolate, trace and listen, then confirm with a thermal scan or tracer gas, then explain what we found and give you written repair prices.

    Thermal Scans on Large Floors in Alexandria, VA

    Big open floor plans hide leaks well. A thermal camera scans an entire living area in a few minutes, showing temperature differences of a degree or less across tile or concrete, which lets us rule out large areas before acoustic work starts. That saves real time in bigger houses across Alexandria, VA.

    Thermal Imaging for Slab Leaks in Alexandria, VA

    A leaking hot line heats the concrete over it. A thermal camera shows that warm bloom through tile, vinyl and even thin carpet, often spreading outward from the leak point in a recognizable shape. It only works on hot lines. We use it to narrow the search before the acoustic check.

    Post-Tension Slab Leak Detection in Alexandria

    A lot of newer houses are built on post-tension slabs. These slabs have steel tendons stretched under tens of thousands of pounds of force, and cutting one by mistake is dangerous and can damage the foundation. We scan for tendons with ground-penetrating radar or a rebar locator before opening anything, or recommend a reroute that never touches the concrete.

    Supply and Drain Leaks Under the Slab in Alexandria, VA

    A supply leak and a drain leak show different signs. Supply leaks are pressurized, so they move the meter, warm the floor on the hot side and run whether or not anyone uses water. Drain leaks only leak when water goes down, and they show up as odors, slow drains or damp floors near bathrooms. Each needs its own test in your Alexandria, VA home.

    Slab Leaks in Older Homes in Alexandria, VA

    Older homes in Alexandria, VA often have soft copper or galvanized steel under the slab, and both corrode with age. Copper fails at bends and where it rubs concrete, while galvanized rusts from the inside until it splits. Our techs know where these fail. That knowledge points the search at the likeliest spots first.
