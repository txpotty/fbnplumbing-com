    Sump Pump Types We Replace in Alexandria, VA

    Elevator and Garage Pit Pumps in Alexandria

    Elevator pits and parking garage sumps often require oil-sensing controls that stop the pump from sending hydraulic fluid or motor oil into the storm drain. Pumps and oil-sensing panels get replaced to code. Two alternating pumps on one controller mean one can fail without flooding the pit. Some insurance carriers ask for test records.

    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Alexandria

    Stuck floats and leaking check valves cause more sump failures than dead motors do. When the check valve leaks, the water in the pipe drops back into the basin and the pump has to move it twice, which shortens its life. New valve every time. Vertical switches suit tight pits, and tethered floats suit wide ones.

    Storm-Night Sump Pump Replacement in Alexandria, VA

    Most emergency sump calls come in at night, during the heaviest rain of the year, when the pump has been running nonstop for hours. Those calls go to the top of the schedule. Our tech shows up with a pump and valve on the truck, and in most cases water is leaving the pit within an hour. Then we find out why it failed.

    Pedestal Pump Service in Alexandria, VA

    Louder, yes, but a pedestal motor sits high and dry, so in a narrow pit it often outlasts a submersible by several years. Narrow pits favor pedestals. Worn units get replaced, and we reset the float rod stops so the cycle is right. A submersible is worth a look when the pit is wide enough.

    Submersible Sump Pump Replacement in Alexandria

    A submersible pump sits at the bottom of the pit, sealed in an oil-filled housing, and it runs quieter than anything else you can put down there. We pull the old unit, scoop the gravel and silt out of the basin, and set the new pump on a flat brick so it does not suck up debris. Then a new check valve goes on. Skip that part and the pump short cycles.

    Cast Iron Submersible Pumps in Alexandria

    Cast iron sheds motor heat into the water around it far better than a plastic housing does, which is a big part of why a Zoeller M53 or Liberty 250 outlasts a hardware-store pump. Most swaps take one to two hours. Before we set it, we make sure the float can swing freely without catching the wall.

    Yard Drainage Pit Pumps in Alexandria, VA

    Yard drainage pits and window well pumps can sit dry from fall to spring and then seize the first time a big storm fills them. We clean the debris out and install a pump built to handle grit. Discharge goes away from the foundation. Test them each spring.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Replacement in Alexandria, VA

    Zoeller cast iron pumps, the M53 and M63 in particular, are what we pull off the truck most often because they take heat and hard cycling better than plastic housings. An existing Zoeller can usually be swapped for a current model with the same 1.5-inch outlet and float style. Need more lift? The M98 steps up to half horsepower.
