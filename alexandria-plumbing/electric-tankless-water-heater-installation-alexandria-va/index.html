    Electric Tankless Water Heater Sizing and Selection in Alexandria

    Sizing comes down to two numbers, flow rate and temperature rise. If your groundwater comes in at 50 degrees and you want 120 at the shower, that is a 70 degree rise, and a 27 kW unit delivers about 2.6 gallons per minute at that rise. Warmer water means more flow. Panel and pipe layout matter as well.

    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Alexandria

    Hard water builds scale on tankless heating elements, which cuts efficiency and can trigger error codes. Even moderate hardness in Alexandria can leave scale in water heaters over time, so regular flushing helps them run longer and more efficiently. A softener or scale filter helps, and the service valves we install make yearly descaling simple. It is a short service.
