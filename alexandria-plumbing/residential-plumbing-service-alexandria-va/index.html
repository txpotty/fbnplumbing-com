    Leak Detection and Repair in Alexandria, VA

    Water stains on a ceiling rarely sit right under the leak. Expansive clays in some areas expand when wet and shrink when dry, which can crack foundations and shift buried sewer lines.     Pipe Repair and Repiping in Alexandria, VA

    Pinhole leaks and rust-colored water from the tap usually point to supply pipe near the end of its working life in an older house. We patch single leaks with a copper repair or PEX transition. More than one leak usually means repiping. Freeze splits get the same treatment, cut out and replaced with new pipe rated for the job.

    Gas Line Repair and Installation in Alexandria, VA

    Smell gas? Leave the house and call the gas utility first, then call us.     Drain Cleaning and Sewer Camera Service in Alexandria

    Wipes are not flushable, whatever the package says. Most slow drains come down to grease, hair or wipes, and for a main line backup we cable it open, then run a camera to look for roots, offset joints or a belly where water sits in the pipe. We send you the video file.

    Gas Lines for New Appliances in Alexandria, VA

    Switching from an electric range to gas means a new line and a shutoff. Pipe size is figured from the BTU load, run it in black iron or CSST, bond it where the code requires and pressure-test before the gas company or inspector signs off on the work. Then we light it and check the flame.
