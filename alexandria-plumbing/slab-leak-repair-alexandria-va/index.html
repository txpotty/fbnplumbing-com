    Pipe Rerouting and Repiping in Alexandria

    Old galvanized or thin copper is hard to patch reliably. Rerouting skips it. We abandon the leaking run in place and install new pipe through walls or the attic, so the problem section is gone from service and the water travels through fresh line that you can reach later.     Direct Slab Leak Repair in Alexandria, VA

    Direct repair is often the most efficient fix for a single leak. A small section of slab gets cut. With the pipe exposed, the damaged length is cut out, new pipe is joined in, and the line is pressure tested before the soil goes back in and gets compacted in layers. The concrete is patched flush with the floor.

    Slab Leak Pipe Rerouting in Alexandria

    A reroute abandons the leaking pipe under the slab. The new pipe runs through the attic. For older Alexandria homes, it bypasses the whole buried run of tired copper or galvanized and removes the chance of another leak there. We cut small openings at fixture walls.

    PEX Repiping for Slab Leaks in Alexandria, VA

    Two or three slab leaks in a few years point to the pipe, not bad luck. A repipe may be cheaper long term. PEX bends with the structure, won't corrode and runs overhead, so a whole-house repipe leaves nothing under the slab. It suits many Alexandria homes.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Repair in Alexandria

    Cold leaks don't make warm spots. The bill gives them away. Look for a rising bill, a meter that spins with nothing on or damp carpet, since a cold leak wets the soil as much as a hot one. Your Alexandria home is no exception. We isolate the cold line and repair or reroute it.

    Spot Repair Through the Slab in Alexandria, VA

    Spot repairs fit leaks in otherwise good pipe. One cut, one fix. The floor gets opened, the pipe replaced and tested, and the trench compacted and patched, ready for your flooring contractor.
