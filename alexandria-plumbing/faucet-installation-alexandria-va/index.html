    Vessel Sink Faucets in Alexandria, VA

    Vessel sinks sit high, so the faucet has to sit higher. A tall single-hole faucet or a wall-mount spout works, and we pick the height so the stream lands near the drain instead of the bowl rim. Spout reach matters too. Too short and you bang your knuckles on the bowl.

    Pot Filler Installation in Alexandria

    A pot filler needs its own cold line. The new cold line comes up inside the wall behind the cooktop, with an accessible shutoff valve so you can service the filler later. The arm folds flat when not in use. The line is tested under pressure before anyone tiles over it.

    Touchless Faucet Installation in Alexandria

    Touchless faucets run on a solenoid valve. The control box mounts under the sink, up off the cabinet floor where a small leak will not soak it, and it runs on batteries or a plug-in transformer. Mineral scale can stick a solenoid open, so we add the inline strainer the maker supplies. Sensor range gets tested last.

    Wall-Mount Faucet Installation in Alexandria, VA

    Wall-mount faucets start before the drywall closes.
