    Grease and Scale Buildup Cleaning in Hampton, VA

    Grease bonds with mineral scale into a hard crust. Private wells serve many rural Virginia homes, and some well water carries iron, manganese, low pH or sulfur that calls for treatment.     Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Lines in Hampton

    Older properties may still have clay or Orangeburg pipe. Both have weak spots. Roots love clay joints, while Orangeburg fiber pipe tends to squash into an oval and eventually collapse, so we clean these lines carefully and map the joints and soft spots on camera.
