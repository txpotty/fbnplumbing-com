    Air Switch Disposal Installation in Hampton

    An air switch puts a push button on the countertop or sink deck instead of a wall switch. Pressing it sends air down a tube. A control box under the sink reads that pulse and flips the power, keeping live wiring well away from the water. We drill the counter or use the spare faucet hole.

    Dishwasher Connection and Air Gap Setup in Hampton

    Nearly every InSinkErator has a dishwasher inlet with a factory knockout plug inside. The plug gets knocked loose and pulled out of the grind chamber.     Standard InSinkErator Installation in Hampton

    A standard install replaces the unit under an existing sink. We take the old disposal down, scrape the old putty off the flange and set a new flange with fresh putty. Then the mount ring goes on. We connect power through a cord kit or the old hardwire and confirm the ground first.
