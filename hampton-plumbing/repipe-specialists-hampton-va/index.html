    PEX Repiping in Hampton, VA

    PEX is flexible plastic tubing that can run long distances without a fitting, which means fewer joints hidden in your walls. Fewer joints, fewer leaks. It resists scale and chlorine, handles a freeze better than copper and costs less to install, which is why most of our repipes use it. We use PEX-A with cold expansion fittings that keep the full inside diameter of the pipe open. Flow stays strong at the last shower.

    Urgent Repipes After Repeated Failures in Hampton, VA

    A widespread pipe failure sometimes forces a repipe sooner than you planned.     Slab Leak Repiping in Hampton

    A slab leak shows up as a warm spot on the floor, a running meter or a wet patch of carpet with no visible cause. Soils in Virginia range from sandy ground with a high water table near the coast to dense clay and rocky karst farther inland. Instead of breaking concrete to patch one pipe, we reroute new supply lines overhead through the attic and down interior walls, then abandon the old lines in the slab. No jackhammer needed. We keep PEX out of sunlight and away from water heater flues.
