    Power Vent Water Heater Replacement in Hampton

    Blower motors and pressure switches are the weak points on power vent tanks. When one fails twice on a tank that is already 8 or 9 years old, replacing the whole heater is usually cheaper over time. We confirm a dedicated outlet first.

    Tankless Gas Conversion in Hampton

    Bradford White Tank Replacement in Hampton

    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Hampton, VA

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust through 2 or 3-inch PVC, so they can sit far from a chimney. A standard outlet has to be within reach of the cord. We run the vent within the maker's length and elbow limits.

    High-Efficiency Gas Water Heater Upgrades in Hampton, VA

    Condensing gas tanks run around 90 percent efficient or better and vent through PVC, while a standard atmospheric tank sits closer to 60 percent. They cost more and need a condensate drain and an outlet. Payback depends on usage.
