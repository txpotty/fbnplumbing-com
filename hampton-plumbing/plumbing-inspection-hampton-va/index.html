    Residential Plumbing Inspections in Hampton

    A full home inspection covers the system from the water meter to the sewer connection. Our plumber looks at all exposed pipe, runs every faucet and toilet and tests the heater, the main valve and the fixture stops. Pressure gets measured too. High pressure gets flagged.

    Pre-Purchase Plumbing Inspections in Hampton

    A house can pass a general inspection and still have a sewer full of roots. Galvanized supply lines, a cracked clay sewer or a water heater past fifteen years are all things a seller can credit for or fix before closing.

    Plumbing Inspection Costs in Hampton, VA

    A basic plumbing inspection usually costs $150 to $300, and adding a sewer camera usually brings it to $250 to $500. Bigger properties cost more. Compared to an unexpected sewer replacement, which can run $4,000 to $15,000 or more, an inspection is cheap protection, and we give you the exact price when you book.

    Sewer Line Camera Inspection in Hampton, VA

    Older homes in Hampton often have cast iron or clay sewer pipe that cracks, scales up or lets roots in at the joints. The camera records every defect along with its distance from the cleanout. We mark it on the ground. Knowing the depth and location means a repair estimate can be accurate, and it helps decide between a spot repair, a liner or a full replacement.

    Pipe Material and Water Quality Review in Hampton, VA

    Pipe material says a lot about what to expect from a house. Our plumber identifies the supply pipe, whether copper, CPVC, PEX, galvanized, poly or Kitec, and explains how each usually fails. Certain types need a plan. Polybutylene and Kitec are known for sudden failures, and galvanized slowly closes up with rust, so those get flagged with replacement options.

    Commercial Plumbing Inspections in Hampton, VA

    Commercial buildings have equipment homes do not, so our commercial inspections cover more ground. We look at backflow assemblies, grease interceptors, commercial water heaters, flush valves and the building sewer. Inspectors like documentation. The written report helps facility managers plan and budget repairs before a health inspector or the water provider writes them up.

    Leak Detection and Assessment in Hampton, VA

    Hidden leaks waste water and quietly damage floors and walls. Start at the meter. With all fixtures off, we watch the meter's leak indicator, then use acoustic listening gear and a thermal camera to trace the leak behind walls, under floors or in the slab. Early detection limits the damage. Basements are common in northern and western parts of Virginia, while crawlspaces and slabs are more typical in the east and south.
