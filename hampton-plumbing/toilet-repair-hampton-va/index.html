    What We Fix on Toilets in Hampton, VA

    Fill Valve Replacement in Hampton

    Old ballcock valves with a brass arm and a big plastic float are loud and slow to refill, and they tend to stick open when scale builds up in the ports. We replace them with an adjustable modern fill valve from Fluidmaster or Korky and set the height so the critical level mark sits at least an inch above the overflow. It is a twenty-minute job. You will notice the difference the first night, because the tank stops hissing.

    Toilet Tank and Bowl Crack Repair in Hampton

    Hairline cracks in a tank can sit for years and then split open when someone sets a heavy lid down or a bolt is tightened too far. Sealants and epoxy are a stopgap at best on a tank that holds water every day. We replace it. If the tank model is still made, a matching tank is often cheaper than a new toilet.

    Running Toilet Fixes in Hampton

    Phantom refills, the tank topping itself off for a few seconds in the middle of the night, almost always mean a slow leak at the flapper. The level falls a little until the fill valve opens back up. Sometimes the cause is a refill tube pushed too far down the overflow, which siphons the tank. We clip it above the water line.

    Running Toilet Repair in Hampton

    A tank that keeps hissing and refilling every few minutes is usually leaking past the flapper or overfilling into the overflow tube. It adds up fast. We look at the chain, float, flapper and seat before touching a part, then replace only what failed. Most running toilets are quiet again in about thirty minutes.

    Clogged Toilet Solutions in Hampton, VA

    Paper towels, wipes, dental floss and toys cause most of the repeat clogs we clear, and none of them break down the way toilet paper does. An auger pulls them back out. If the toilet clogs again within a week, the problem is further down the line. We check the branch drain and the main next.

    Pressure-Assist and Dual-Flush Repair in Hampton

    Pressure-assist toilets use a sealed vessel inside the china that compresses air with incoming water pressure, and the cartridge inside that vessel eventually wears out. Hardware stores rarely stock those parts. We carry Flushmate cartridges and dual-flush seals for the common TOTO and American Standard models, and we check house pressure, since these units need steady line pressure to flush properly. Low line pressure gives you a weak, half-hearted flush.

    Leaky Toilet Repair in Hampton

    Food coloring in the tank is the cheapest test we run. If color shows up in the bowl within fifteen minutes without a flush, water is leaking past the flapper or the flush valve seat, and the toilet is quietly refilling itself all day and night. Most of the time a new flapper does it, unless the seat under it is pitted or cracked.

    Toilet Flush Repair in Hampton, VA

    Two or three tries to get a full flush points to a flapper closing too early, a chain hooked in the wrong hole, scale in the rim jets, or a cracked overflow tube letting water bypass the bowl. Each one has a tell. We watch a full cycle with the lid off and replace the flush valve if the overflow tube is cracked, since it cannot be patched.
