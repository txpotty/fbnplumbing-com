    What We Fix on Well Systems in Hampton, VA

    Pulling a Submersible Pump in Hampton

    Pulling a submersible means lifting the pump and every length of drop pipe out through the pitless adapter, sometimes hundreds of feet of it. We check the torque arrestor and every splice on the way up. Figure on one day.

    Well Pump Brands We Install in Hampton, VA

    Goulds, Grundfos, Franklin Electric and Sta-Rite cover nearly every well pump we install. Each brand has strengths, and we choose based on your well. Goulds and Franklin parts are on every supply shelf, which helps when a control box fails late on a Saturday afternoon.

    Grundfos Well Pump Replacement in Hampton, VA

    Grundfos SQ and SQE pumps have a built-in soft start and dry-run protection, and they run on two wires with no control box at the surface. They cost more up front. The SQE pairs with a CU 301 controller for constant pressure.

    Sandy Wells and Submersible Pumps in Hampton

    Sand kills submersibles early. Grit in a well chews up impellers fast, so we look for it on the old pump and may set the new one higher or add a sand separator. Pump setting depth matters.

    No-Water Calls in Hampton

    Pump trouble rarely gives notice. Usually the faucet spits air, then quits, after the motor has been limping along for weeks. We test the control box and pressure switch before pulling anything.

    Drop Pipe and Wire Replacement in Hampton

    Galvanized drop pipe from the 1970s and 1980s rusts from the inside, and a pinhole at 150 feet will bleed pressure every time the pump shuts off. Depending on depth and pump weight, it gets replaced with schedule 120 PVC or poly. Wire gets the same look. Nicked jackets and old tape splices go.

    Agricultural and Ranch Well Pumps in Hampton

    Irrigation and stock wells often run 3 to 10 HP pumps, sometimes on three-phase power with a motor starter and overload protection at the panel. Starters and overloads get replaced along with the pump. We schedule around watering times.

    Well Pump Sizing in Hampton

    Sizing starts with the well log. The log, or a sounder down the casing, gives us static water level and well depth, and a draw-down test tells us roughly how many gallons per minute the well can actually give. Pump more than the well yields and it runs dry.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Well Pump Replacement in Hampton

    Plan on $1,500 to $3,500 for a typical residential job. A deeper well or a bigger pump pushes it higher, and a new pressure tank or wire run adds to it, sometimes well past that range. The quote follows the diagnosis.

    Keep the tank precharge right and fix leaks fast. A tank low on air or a toilet that runs will keep the pump starting all night, and every start wears the motor. Sandy wells benefit from a filter or separator.

    Our usual picks are Franklin and Grundfos for submersibles, Goulds for jet pumps, and Zoeller for some shallow well work. Matching your current brand is usually easy. Budget matters too.

    Plenty of handy homeowners can swap a pressure switch, but a pump pull is a different job. Doing it right takes a hoist, a second person and the meters to test the motor and wire before it goes back down. Most homeowners call us.

    Location is the difference. A jet pump sits above ground and pulls water up by suction, which limits it to shallow wells, while a submersible hangs in the water and pushes it up from hundreds of feet. At depth, a submersible wins on efficiency.

    Requirements vary across Virginia, and some counties want a well or plumbing permit for pump work. Where one is needed, we pull it and schedule the inspection, so the job is on record with the local health or building department. You do not have to handle it.

    If the tank is old or waterlogged, replace it with the pump. A failed tank causes rapid short cycling, which is one of the most common reasons submersible motors burn out early.

    Ten to twenty years is a normal life for a well-sized pump. Sandy water and short cycling can cut that to under five years. Replace it when it fails.
